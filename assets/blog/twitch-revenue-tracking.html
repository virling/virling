Twitch revenue tracking: which streams bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-20.

To see which Twitch streams bring paying customers, put a tag that names the stream on every link a viewer can click, in chat, behind your bot's commands and in your panels, then join each payment to the visit before it. A stream is a few hours of live attention followed by a tail of people watching the VOD, and the useful number is how much revenue each broadcast, and each sponsored streamer, produced. Concurrent viewers, follows and bits answer a different question.

This is for streamers who sell merch, a course or their own software, and for brands that sponsor streamers and want to know which creators sent buyers, not only viewers.

Chat commands and panel links

Most clicks from a stream come from two places, and they need different tags.

  • Chat commands. A chat bot answers !shop, !merch or !sponsor by posting a link. You own the text behind the command, so edit it before you go live and the link it posts tonight carries tonight's campaign. A moderator pasting your bare address instead is a click nobody can credit, so ask mods to use the command.
  • Panels. The panels under the player are the same on every stream for months, so a panel link cannot name one broadcast. Give it one fixed campaign, such as twitch-panel, and read it as what the channel brings in total.
  • Sponsor reads. During a sponsored segment, the sponsor's link goes behind !sponsor, on screen and in a panel, and it should be the sponsor's own link so its buyers stay apart from yours.
!shop   https://yourdomain.com/shop?utm_source=twitch&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=twitch-2026-11-20-launch
panel   https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=twitch&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=twitch-panel

Start every campaign with twitch- and put the stream's date in it. A command you forgot to update then shows up as last week's date getting clicks today. A long URL is hard to read out loud, so a tracked link suits commands better: in Virling, open the Campaign tab on the Channels card, click + Tracked link, pick Other, type twitch as the source, name it after the stream and pick the page. The bot posts the short link, and the tags are added on the click. See tracked links; the UTM builder makes plain tagged links.

Live clicks and VOD clicks

A link posted in chat is clicked live, but people who watch the past broadcast see the chat replay next to the video, links included, so the same message keeps sending visitors after you go offline. Both arrive with the same campaign.

To split them, set the range to the day of the stream and click its campaign row. On a one-day range the chart shows visits by hour, so the hours you were live stand apart from the hours after. Over a longer range, visits on later days came from the VOD, a clip or someone who kept the link. If you cut highlights or upload the stream to YouTube, give those links a campaign of their own, such as twitch-2026-11-20-launch-vod; revenue per YouTube video covers description links there.

Which channel Twitch visits land in

twitch.tv is not on Virling's list of social hosts, twitch is not a known social source and the Twitch app is not one of the in-app browsers it recognizes, so a click with a twitch.tv referrer or a utm_source=twitch tag counts as Referral, even with utm_medium=social, and an untagged click from the app usually counts as Direct. The twitch- prefix is how you find your streams among other Referral campaigns. The full rule order is in channels.

For brands: one partner link per streamer

When you sponsor several streamers, never let two of them share a link or a campaign. Open Partners in Virling and add each streamer with the page their viewers should land on. Virling makes a short link that puts the streamer's name in utm_source, referral in the medium and its own id in the campaign. Ask each streamer to put it behind !sponsor, in a panel and on screen during the read.

The Partners page ranks streamers by revenue, with visitors, paying customers and refund rate for the range, credited under your attribution model, so a smaller channel that sends buyers ranks above a bigger one that sends only clicks. Copy report link gives each streamer a read-only page with only their own numbers for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, without signing in. Virling does not pay streamers or work out commissions. When the numbers look odd

  • Last week's stream is getting this week's clicks. The chat command still posts the old campaign. Update it before each broadcast.
  • Direct jumped while you were live. An untagged link went out, often a bare address pasted in chat. Check what the commands and panels point at.
  • Two streamers share one row. They were given the same link. Make a partner link per streamer.
  • No Twitch row under Social. Expected. Look in Referral for the twitch- campaigns, or on the Partners page for sponsored streamers.
  • Visits but no revenue. The visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track revenue from Twitch?

Put a tagged link behind each chat command, in your panels and in sponsor reads, with utm_source=twitch and a campaign that names the stream. Pass the visitor ID into checkout and read revenue per campaign in the Campaign tab.

Which channel do Twitch visits land in?

Referral when they carry a twitch.tv referrer or a utm_source=twitch tag, because Twitch is not on Virling's social lists. An untagged click from the Twitch app, which often sends no referrer, counts as Direct.

How do I credit each stream when the chat command stays the same?

Change the link behind the command before each broadcast, so the message it posts carries that stream's campaign. Panels are the same on every stream, so give them one fixed campaign such as twitch-panel.

Can I tell live clicks from VOD clicks?

Roughly. Set the range to the stream's day and click its campaign: on a one-day range the chart shows visits by hour, so clicks while you were live stand apart from clicks from the chat replay afterwards. For highlights and uploads, use a campaign of their own.

How can a brand see which streamer sent paying customers?

Give each streamer their own partner link from the Partners page in Virling. Partners are ranked by revenue, and Copy report link gives each streamer a read-only page with only their own visitors, paying customers and revenue.
