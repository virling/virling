Perplexity referral traffic: which Perplexity visits pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-18.

To see which Perplexity visits turn into paying customers, count the clicks on its citations, join each payment to the visit before it, and read the Perplexity row by revenue per visitor rather than by visits. Perplexity answers list the pages they used as numbered sources, and a click on one is the part of Perplexity you can measure. This post covers what those clicks look like on your side, what Comet, Perplexity's browser, changes, and which of Perplexity's two bots robots.txt actually controls.

For the steps every AI source shares, see is AI search traffic worth anything; ChatGPT has its own post.

What a citation click looks like

  • perplexity.ai in a browser. Sources appear as numbered citations inside the answer and as a list next to it. A click on either opens your page with perplexity.ai or www.perplexity.ai as the referrer. Virling drops the www, so both hosts share one Perplexity row in the AI channel. Only the host is kept, never the question.
  • The Perplexity apps. A citation tapped in the mobile app often reaches you with no referrer. Without a utm_source either, it is Direct, and Virling has no user-agent rule that would name the Perplexity app.
  • Tagged links. When the referrer is missing, utm_source=perplexity.ai is credited to Perplexity and a bare utm_source=perplexity counts as AI. Perplexity does not document adding either tag itself.

The traffic source checker shows the rule for any pair of landing URL and referrer: try https://www.perplexity.ai/ as the referrer, then an empty referrer with ?utm_source=perplexity.ai on the URL. A link tagged utm_medium=cpc stays Paid even with a Perplexity source, so ads you run on Perplexity never mix with citations.

Comet is a browser, not a source

Comet is Perplexity's web browser. A visit made in Comet is credited by where the click came from, exactly like a visit from any other browser:

  • A citation clicked in a Perplexity answer inside Comet carries the perplexity.ai referrer and lands in the Perplexity row.
  • A click on a Google result or a newsletter link in Comet is credited to Google or the newsletter. Using Comet does not make a visit Perplexity's.
  • A full URL typed into Comet's address bar is Direct.

Virling does not detect Comet from its user agent, so there is no Comet row or filter. When Comet's assistant opens one of your pages in a tab while doing a task for someone, the page loads in a real browser and the tracking script runs; no rule in Virling separates that load from a click the person made.

PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User

Perplexity's bot documentation names two, and robots.txt treats them very differently.

  • PerplexityBot crawls pages to surface and link them in Perplexity's search results, and Perplexity says it does not collect content for training foundation models. Its user agent is Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; PerplexityBot/1.0; +https://perplexity.ai/perplexitybot). Perplexity recommends allowing it in robots.txt so your pages can appear as sources. Virling lists it as a Search crawler.
  • Perplexity-User fetches a page when someone asks Perplexity a question, so the answer can read and link it. Its user agent is Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; Perplexity-User/1.0; +https://perplexity.ai/perplexity-user). Because a person asked for the fetch, Perplexity says it generally ignores robots.txt, so a Disallow line for it does little. Virling lists it as an Answer fetch: with the server snippet, each one counts as an answer that used your page.

Perplexity publishes the IP ranges of both bots, at www.perplexity.ai/perplexitybot.json and www.perplexity.ai/perplexity-user.json, for checking that a fetch is genuine. Virling matches the bot name anywhere in the user agent, ignoring case, so a version bump changes nothing. The AI crawler checker tells you whether your robots.txt lets PerplexityBot read a page.

Set up and read the Perplexity row

Nothing about Perplexity needs its own setup. Install the tracking script, connect your payment provider and pass the visitor ID into checkout, following link checkout to visits; the server snippet adds Perplexity-User fetches on top.

Then click Perplexity in the AI channel to filter the dashboard. The Pages card shows which pages its citations send people to and which of them end in a payment, which is often a short list of comparison and pricing pages. Open any payment to see the buyer's visits in order, with the credited one marked.

When the Perplexity numbers look wrong

  • Perplexity never cites you. Check that robots.txt allows PerplexityBot. Rules for Perplexity-User make little difference either way.
  • You disallowed Perplexity-User and still see its fetches. That matches Perplexity's documentation. Blocking its published IP ranges at your server is the firm way, at the cost of Perplexity no longer reading your pages live.
  • Comet users show up under Google or Direct. Expected: Comet is credited by where the click came from, as above.
  • Perplexity sends visitors but no revenue. Look at Matched revenue under the Overview numbers; a large unmatched share means checkout is not getting the visitor ID. See Unattributed revenue.

Two posts cover the nearest comparisons: Claude referrals and the bots that read your pages, and AI Overview clicks, which arrive from Google and cannot be split from its other results.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Perplexity visits turn into paying customers?

Pass the Virling visitor ID into checkout and click Perplexity in the AI channel. The row shows visitors from perplexity.ai, the revenue credited to them and revenue per visitor, and the Pages card shows which cited pages lead to payments.

Is www.perplexity.ai counted separately from perplexity.ai?

No. Virling drops the www, so clicks from either host share one Perplexity row in the AI channel.

How does Virling handle visits from the Comet browser?

Like visits from any browser. A citation clicked in Comet carries the perplexity.ai referrer and counts as Perplexity; a Google result clicked in Comet counts as Google. Virling does not detect Comet itself, so it has no separate row.

Does blocking Perplexity-User in robots.txt stop it?

Not reliably. Perplexity's documentation says Perplexity-User generally ignores robots.txt because a person requested the fetch. robots.txt does control PerplexityBot, the crawler Perplexity uses to surface and link sites in its results.

Do PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User count as visitors?

No. Virling stores bot fetches apart from visits. PerplexityBot shows as a Search crawler, and with the server snippet each Perplexity-User fetch shows on the AI card as an answer that used your pages.
