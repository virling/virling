Perplexity referral traffic: which Perplexity visits pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-18.

To see which Perplexity visits turn into paying customers, count every visit that arrives from perplexity.ai or carries a Perplexity utm_source, pass the visitor ID into checkout, and join each payment to the visit before it. You then see the revenue Perplexity referral traffic brings and what each Perplexity visitor is worth next to search, social and email. A visit count alone cannot tell you that, because Perplexity usually sends few visitors and the question is how many of them pay.

This post covers Perplexity only. For ChatGPT, see ChatGPT revenue tracking; for AI assistants in general, see is AI search traffic worth anything.

How Perplexity visits show up

Perplexity answers list the pages they cite as sources. When someone clicks one, the visit can reach your site in three ways, and each one is classified differently.

  • The perplexity.ai referrer. A click on a cited link in the browser usually arrives with perplexity.ai or www.perplexity.ai as the referrer. Virling puts any perplexity.ai host in the AI channel, and www.perplexity.ai and perplexity.ai share one Perplexity row. The referrer is a host only, so it never says which answer or question the click came from.
  • A utm_source tag. When the referrer is missing, Virling reads utm_source. A visit tagged utm_source=perplexity.ai is credited to Perplexity, in the AI channel, exactly as if the referrer had been there. A bare utm_source=perplexity also counts as AI. You only see these tags on links that carry them; Virling does not add them.
  • No referrer and no tag. Links opened from the Perplexity apps, or anywhere else the referrer is dropped, can arrive with neither. Nothing in the visit names Perplexity, so it counts as Direct, in Virling and in most other tools. Virling recognizes some apps' in-app browsers by their user agent, such as Instagram and TikTok, but Perplexity is not one of them.

So the Perplexity row is a floor, not a full count. People who read an answer and later type your name into a browser or a search engine arrive as Direct or Organic search; no analytics tool can tie that visit to the answer. The full rule order is in channels, and the traffic source checker shows how a given landing URL and referrer are credited: paste a URL with utm_source=perplexity.ai and an empty referrer to see it land in AI.

One exception to the order: a paid medium wins over everything. A link with utm_medium=cpc counts as Paid even if its source is perplexity.ai, so ads you run stay apart from answers.

Let Perplexity read your pages

Perplexity can only cite pages it can read. It uses two bots. PerplexityBot builds Perplexity's search index. Perplexity-User fetches a page live when someone asks Perplexity a question. If robots.txt blocks them, your pages are less likely to be cited.

The AI crawler checker reads your robots.txt and tells you which of these bots may read a page. Bot fetches are never counted as visitors in Virling. With the server snippet, each Perplexity-User fetch shows on the AI card as an answer that used one of your pages; see AI answers.

Set up Perplexity revenue tracking in Virling

  1. Add the tracking script to every page. See the tracking script. Nothing Perplexity-specific is needed: the referrer and the tag are read on every visit.
  2. Connect your payment provider: Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments, Creem, RevenueCat, Shopify or WooCommerce.
  3. Pass the visitor ID into checkout. Stripe Payment Links, Polar checkout links and Dodo Payments links get it from the script on click. For a checkout you create on your server, pass the virling_vid cookie, for example as client_reference_id on a Stripe Checkout Session. See link checkout to visits. Without this step Perplexity shows visitors and no revenue.
  4. Open the dashboard and pick a range of a few weeks or more. Perplexity usually sends fewer visitors than search, so short ranges are noisy.
  5. In the Channels card, click AI, or switch to the Source tab, and find Perplexity. The row shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, with a chip that compares it with your site average.
  6. Click the row to filter the whole page. The Pages card now shows which landing pages Perplexity sends people to, and which of them lead to payments.
  7. Open a payment from the Payments page to see the buyer's journey: every visit before the payment, with the credited one marked.

How Virling credits the payment

People often click a cited link in Perplexity, leave, and come back days later by typing your URL. With the default model, last non-direct, that later Direct visit does not take the credit: the payment goes to the Perplexity visit, as long as it started inside the lookback window, 90 days by default. If the buyer subscribes, every renewal keeps counting toward Perplexity. If they come back through a Google search or a newsletter before paying, that later visit takes the credit instead.

To see how often Perplexity starts journeys that another channel finishes, switch the model to first touch in Site settings. First-touch or last-touch attribution explains the difference. Changing the model recalculates every past payment, so you can switch back.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • Perplexity has visitors but no revenue. Check the Matched revenue line under the Overview numbers. If a large share of revenue is unmatched, the visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.
  • A buyer says they found you in Perplexity but the payment is credited elsewhere. They may have read the answer without clicking, or opened the link from an app that dropped the referrer. That visit has no trace of Perplexity.
  • Perplexity shows no visits at all. Check that robots.txt does not block PerplexityBot or Perplexity-User, and look at the Answers tab of the AI card to see whether Perplexity names you when asked your buyer questions.
  • Visitors who block the script are not counted, so Perplexity's real visitor number can be higher than any analytics tool shows. Their revenue still counts in your total, as Unattributed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Perplexity visits turn into paying customers?

Use a tool that groups perplexity.ai visits into their own source and joins each payment to the visit before it. In Virling, pass the visitor ID into checkout, then open the AI channel and the Perplexity row: it shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and the Payments page shows each buyer's visits.

How do I track traffic from Perplexity?

Look for perplexity.ai in your referrers. Virling puts every visit with a perplexity.ai or www.perplexity.ai referrer in the AI channel and lists it as Perplexity. A visit with no referrer but utm_source=perplexity.ai or utm_source=perplexity also counts as AI.

Why does some Perplexity traffic show up as Direct?

A visit with no referrer and no utm_source has nothing that names Perplexity, so it is Direct in Virling and in most other tools. That can happen when a link is opened from an app that drops the referrer. Treat the Perplexity number as a floor.

Does a Perplexity visit still get credit if the buyer pays a week later?

With the default model, last non-direct, yes. If the buyer comes back by typing your URL, that Direct visit does not take the credit; the payment goes to the Perplexity visit as long as it is inside the lookback window, 90 days by default.

Do PerplexityBot and Perplexity-User count as visitors?

No. They are Perplexity's bots, not people. Virling stores crawler hits apart from visits; with the server snippet, Perplexity-User fetches show on the AI card as answers that used your pages.
