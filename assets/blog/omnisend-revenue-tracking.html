Omnisend revenue tracking: which emails bring orders

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-26.

Omnisend revenue tracking on a Shopify or WooCommerce store means joining each order to the click that brought the buyer. Omnisend already adds UTM tags to every link it sends. Give each campaign and automation message a short utm_campaign, add the Virling script to your store, and store the visitor ID on each order. Virling counts an email click as Email and credits the order to it, so the Campaign tab shows revenue per email, counted once, next to every other channel.

How Omnisend tags your links

Omnisend adds UTM tags to all links in email campaigns, SMS campaigns, push notifications and automation workflows. You can change them but not remove them (source: Omnisend, Omnisend Reports: UTM Tags). When we read that page in November 2026, it listed these values:

  • utm_source: omnisend.
  • utm_medium: email, sms or PushNotification, by message type.
  • utm_campaign: built from the campaign or automation name and its ID.
  • utm_content: the message ID, on automation messages only.

To change them:

  • Campaigns. In the campaign, go to the Settings step, scroll to the bottom, click Edit UTM tags, change Source, Medium or Campaign, and click Save.
  • Automations. Open the workflow, select a message (Email, SMS or Push Notification), click Edit UTM tags in the message editor, and save.

All links in one email campaign share the same source, medium and campaign. The same page says tags are case-sensitive and recommends lowercase. Texts are shortened through Omnisend’s own link domain, and turning shortening off also turns off click tracking and the automatic UTM tags (source: Omnisend, How Omnisend Shortens Links in SMS Messages). Leave it on.

Name campaigns, automations and SMS

Whatever arrives as utm_campaign becomes a report row in Virling. The default value works, but a name with an ID in it is hard to read, and every message in one automation shares it. Keep the source omnisend, keep Omnisend’s medium, and set the campaign yourself:

  • Campaigns: one short lowercase name per send, with the date, such as bf-sale-2026-11.
  • Automation emails: the flow and the step, such as abandoned-cart-1 and abandoned-cart-2, set on each message so each step is its own row. Keep them the same for as long as the flow runs.
  • SMS: the same pattern with a prefix, such as sms-bf-sale-2026-11 or sms-abandoned-cart-1, and the medium left at sms.

For links you send outside Omnisend, the free UTM builder makes tagged URLs in the same style.

How Virling sorts an Omnisend visit

Every session gets one channel from its first pageview, and the first matching rule wins:

  • An email medium. email, newsletter or e-mail, in any case, is Email, whatever the referrer says. Omnisend email clicks land here with omnisend as the source.
  • A paid medium. cpc, ppc, paid, paidsearch, paid_social or paidsocial is Paid.
  • Neither, with a referrer. The referring site decides.
  • Neither, no referrer, with a source. omnisend is not a known search, social, community or AI source, so an SMS click (utm_medium=sms) or a push click (PushNotification) is Referral. A tap in a text app usually sends no referrer.
  • No tags and no referrer. The visit is Direct.

The campaign is stored with the visit either way, so SMS still gets its own rows on the Campaign tab. For more on texts, see SMS marketing revenue tracking, and for the rules in full, how channels are decided.

Why Omnisend revenue and your order list disagree

Omnisend credits a sale to a message when the contact engaged with it before buying (source: Omnisend, How Omnisend Attributes Sales):

  • Opens count. An email or push open is enough; for SMS it takes a click. A subscriber who opened your sale email and then bought from a search result still counts as Omnisend revenue.
  • A window, not a visit. By default the window is 7 days for email and 24 hours for SMS and push. You change it under Store settings, Sales attribution, where you can also exclude Apple Mail Privacy Protection opens.
  • Last message wins. The sale goes to the last message engaged with inside its window.
  • Send date, not order date. Campaign and automation reports put revenue on the day the message was sent.
  • Refunds stay in. Refunded and cancelled orders do not lower attributed sales, and store-wide sales, POS included, can be attributed.

Omnisend’s page says both numbers are accurate and measure differently, and that totals will rarely match one to one. Your Shopify or WooCommerce order list counts each order once, on the day it was placed. Virling does the same: each order goes to one visit and one channel, refunds lower it, and an email gets the credit only when a click from it led to the order. Connect your store

Add the Virling script before </head> on every page an email can land on; see the script docs. Then connect Shopify with a custom app token that can only read orders, or WooCommerce with a read-only REST API key. Virling imports new orders every minute, and a short snippet stores the visitor ID on each order, so the order is joined to the visits before it. See set up Virling for an online store.

By default the whole order goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct. A shopper who clicks your abandoned cart email and comes back the next day by typing your address is credited to that email; one who returns from a search result, to Organic search. Read revenue per email

  • In the Channels card, click Email. The page filters to visits with an email medium.
  • Open the Campaign tab. Each utm_campaign is a row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor.
  • Open an order from the Payments page to see the visits before it, with the credited one marked.

Check it with one email and one order

  1. Make a segment with only your own address and send it a campaign with one link. Under Edit UTM tags, set the campaign to tracking-test.
  2. Copy the link from the email into a private window. Expected: your store opens and the address bar shows utm_source=omnisend, utm_medium=email and utm_campaign=tracking-test.
  3. In the dashboard, look at the Channels card. Expected: a visit in Email, and a tracking-test row on the Campaign tab.
  4. In the same window, place a test order. On Shopify, use Shopify Payments in test mode or the Bogus Gateway. On WooCommerce, put your gateway in test mode, or use Cash on delivery and set the order to Processing. See test your setup.
  5. Wait a minute for the next sync and open the order on the Payments page. Expected: the channel is Email and the journey starts at your tagged visit. Cancel and refund the test order afterwards.

Repeat the first three steps with one automation email and one text.

What to check if the numbers look off

  • Email revenue is lower than in Omnisend. Expected: opens, and orders that came through another channel, are not credited to email here.
  • Omnisend orders show as Direct. A link was pasted without tags, or a redirect on your store dropped them; see UTM parameters lost after redirect.
  • Email revenue shows as Referral. The medium was changed to something other than the three email values.
  • One flow is split across rows. The campaign value changed, or differs in case.
  • Orders show as Unattributed. The order has no visitor ID. Check that the Shopify or WooCommerce snippet is in place.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Omnisend email brought an order?

Set the UTM tags on each Omnisend campaign and automation message so links carry utm_medium=email and a short utm_campaign. Add the Virling script to your store, connect Shopify or WooCommerce, and store the visitor ID on each order. Each order is credited to the tagged visit, and the Campaign tab shows revenue per email.

Does Omnisend add UTM tags automatically?

Yes. Omnisend says it adds UTM tags to all links in email campaigns, SMS campaigns, push notifications and automation workflows. You can edit the tags but not remove them. The source is omnisend and the medium is email, sms or PushNotification.

Which channel does an Omnisend click land in?

An email click carries utm_medium=email, so it lands in Email whatever the referrer. An SMS click carries utm_medium=sms and a push click carries PushNotification, which are not email mediums, so with no referrer they land in Referral with omnisend as the source.

Why does Omnisend show more revenue than Virling?

Omnisend credits a sale to the last message the contact opened or clicked within the attribution window, 7 days for email and 24 hours for SMS and push by default, even if they reached the store another way. Virling credits each order once, to one visit, and only an email that was clicked can get it.

Does Virling connect to my Omnisend account?

No. The setup on this page uses only the tags on your links, the Virling script and your Shopify or WooCommerce orders. Keep sending with Omnisend.
