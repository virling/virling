Moving from Lemon Squeezy to Stripe Managed Payments without losing revenue attribution

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-27.

Lemon Squeezy stops processing payments on March 31, 2027 and points its merchants to Stripe Managed Payments. If you track which channel brings paying customers, the move does not have to cost you that history. Keep Lemon Squeezy connected in Virling until your last renewal there, connect Stripe with a read-only key, and pass the visitor ID into Stripe Checkout. Sales made through Managed Payments are ordinary Stripe objects in your own account, so they are attributed like any other Stripe payment.

What changes for your payment data

Managed Payments makes Stripe the merchant of record, the role Lemon Squeezy played. What matters for attribution, from Stripe's documentation:

  • Sales land in your own Stripe account. Each one is a Checkout Session, PaymentIntent and Charge, plus a Subscription and Invoices for recurring plans, the same objects any Stripe integration reads.
  • Only Stripe Checkout and Payment Links. Managed Payments does not support Elements or subscriptions created outside Checkout, so every sale passes through a checkout you can hand a visitor ID to.
  • Local currency by default. Adaptive Pricing is always on, so buyers pay in their own currency. Revenue reports convert it, as they do for any foreign-currency payment.
  • Fees and tax in one number. Stripe's balance reports combine its fee and the tax it withholds, and a refund includes the tax the buyer paid.
  • A new statement descriptor. Buyers see LINK.COM* before your name, which matters if you ask buyers where they heard of you and they check a bank statement.

Keep Lemon Squeezy connected until your last renewal there

Do not disconnect Lemon Squeezy when you switch checkouts. Subscriptions that stay on Lemon Squeezy until you move them keep renewing there, and each renewal is credited to the visit that brought the first payment only while Virling still reads them. Payments Virling has already imported stay in your reports after Lemon Squeezy shuts down, so your history before the move is kept. See tracking Lemon Squeezy revenue by traffic source for how that setup works.

Connect Stripe with a read-only key

In Virling, open your site, then Setup, then Connect payments, and add Stripe with a restricted key. The Stripe setup guide lists the read permissions to tick. Virling then imports the last 90 days and keeps syncing. Both connections can run side by side, and your reports add them up.

Pass the visitor ID into Stripe Checkout

UTM tags on your landing page do not reach Stripe's checkout page on their own. Hand Stripe one visitor ID instead, and every earlier visit stays joined, tagged or not.

  • Payment Links need no code. Keep the buy.stripe.com link as a normal link on a page that runs the Virling script, and the script adds client_reference_id when the page loads.
  • Checkout Sessions your server creates need one line: read the virling_vid cookie and pass it as client_reference_id.
// Node, stripe: a Managed Payments Checkout Session with the visitor ID
const session = await stripe.checkout.sessions.create({
  // ...line items, mode and the Managed Payments settings from Stripe's guide
  client_reference_id: req.cookies.virling_vid,
  success_url: "https://yourdomain.com/thanks",
});

If client_reference_id already holds your own user ID, put the visitor ID in metadata.virling_vid instead. Stripe's list of parameters you must remove for Managed Payments covers tax, payment method, shipping and receipt fields, not these two. More detail is in Stripe Checkout UTM parameters.

Subscribers you move to Stripe

A subscription migrated from Lemon Squeezy becomes a new customer in Stripe, and its first Stripe renewal carries no visitor ID. Virling cannot tell on its own that it is the same buyer, so that renewal shows as Unattributed. If your app knows the subscriber's email, send it once as an identify event: a Stripe payment with no visitor ID whose receipt email matches is then credited to that visitor and marked Matched by email. See recover payments by email.

What Stripe has not documented yet

Three things matter for attribution that Stripe's Managed Payments pages do not cover as of this post. Check them with one test payment before you move real traffic:

  • Whether a restricted key can read Managed Payments sales. If connecting Stripe in Virling fails with a permission error, the key needs more read access.
  • Whether charge.succeeded events fire for these sales. Virling also lists charges directly, so a sale is still imported, but a payment that settles late may take longer to appear.
  • Whether metadata set on the Checkout Session is copied anywhere else. client_reference_id lives on the session itself, which is why it is the safer field.

To test: in test mode, open a page that runs the script through a tagged link such as ?utm_source=test, run window.virling.vid() in the browser console, pay with Stripe's test card, and check that the payment appears on Virling's Payments page credited to that visit within a minute or two.

If Managed Payments is not available in your country

Managed Payments is open to businesses in the US, Canada, the UK, most of the EU, Switzerland, Norway, Australia, Japan, Hong Kong and Singapore, after Stripe reviews each account. Elsewhere, Lemon Squeezy tells merchants to export their data and move to another provider. Virling also reads Paddle, Polar, Dodo Payments and Creem, which act as merchant of record too, so the same steps apply: keep Lemon Squeezy connected, connect the new provider, and pass the visitor ID at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

When does Lemon Squeezy stop working?

It stops processing payments on March 31, 2027, and closed new store sign-ups on November 16, 2026. Until then existing stores can keep selling.

Will I lose my revenue attribution history when Lemon Squeezy shuts down?

Not in Virling. Payments it has already imported stay in your reports. Keep Lemon Squeezy connected until your last renewal there so nothing is missed.

Do Stripe Managed Payments sales show up in Stripe's API?

Yes. Stripe documents them as Checkout Sessions, PaymentIntents, Charges, Subscriptions and Invoices in your own account, so tools that read Stripe can read them.

How do I keep UTM attribution after moving to Stripe?

Pass a visitor ID into checkout: Payment Links get it from the Virling script, and Checkout Sessions take it as client_reference_id from the virling_vid cookie.

Why do my migrated subscribers show as Unattributed?

A migrated subscription is a new Stripe customer with no visitor ID. Send the subscriber's email once as an identify event and Virling matches their Stripe payments by email.
