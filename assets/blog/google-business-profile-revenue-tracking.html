Google Business Profile UTM tracking: which Maps clicks pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-22.

To see which Google Business Profile visits turn into paying customers, swap the plain website address on your profile for one with UTM tags, tag the appointment link the same way, and pass the visitor ID into whatever takes the payment. A profile is mostly used on phones and in the Maps app, so untagged clicks scatter: some arrive with a Google referrer and are filed as Organic search, others arrive with none and are filed as Direct. A business-profile campaign pulls them back into one row.

This is for local shops, clinics, salons, trades and restaurants that sell or take bookings on their website. The profile's performance report counts website clicks, calls and direction requests; it does not know which of those people paid.

The links on a profile you can tag

Each link you control on the profile is a separate door to your site. Tag every one with the same source, medium and campaign, and use utm_content to say which door it was:

Profile linkWhere to edit itExample URL
Website buttonContact, Websitehttps://yourbusiness.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=business-profile&utm_content=website
Appointment or booking linkContact, Appointment linkshttps://yourbusiness.com/book?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=business-profile&utm_content=appointment
Menu or order linkThe menu or ordering section, where your category offers ithttps://yourbusiness.com/menu?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=business-profile&utm_content=menu
Button on an update postAdd update, Add a button, Learn more or Bookhttps://yourbusiness.com/offer?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=business-profile&utm_content=post

Why these values:

  • utm_source=google matters most for Maps app clicks. When there is no referrer, Virling falls back to the source tag, and google is a known search source, so the visit is Organic search instead of Direct. Invented sources like gbp or google_maps are not known and turn into Referral.
  • utm_medium=organic, or anything outside the paid list (cpc, ppc, paid, paidsearch, paid_social, paidsocial) and the email list. A referrer from www.google.com or maps.google.com then decides the channel as usual.
  • utm_campaign=business-profile stays the same on every link and every location, so the Campaign tab can show the profile as one total.
  • utm_content tells the links, or the branches, apart. Virling saves it on each visit.

After saving, Google may take a while to show the new link. Then tap Website on your live profile from a phone and paste the resulting address into the UTM checker. The UTM builder makes the links, and channels has the full rule order.

Several locations

A business with branches has one profile per branch. Keep business-profile as the campaign on all of them and put the branch in utm_content, for example utm_content=website-northside. The campaign row is the total for all profiles; filter to it and the Landing page tab shows which pages each door led to. If you would rather compare branches side by side in the Campaign tab, use one campaign per branch, such as business-profile-northside, at the cost of losing the single total. Local ads belong on utm_medium=cpc with a campaign of their own; see tracking Google Ads by campaign.

What Virling cannot see from a profile

A lot of profile activity never touches your website, and nothing on your site can record it:

  • Calls. Tapping Call opens the phone dialler. No page loads, so there is no visit.
  • Direction requests. These open Maps navigation. If the customer then walks in and pays at the till, that sale is not in your payment provider's online data, or it is there with no visitor ID.
  • Messages and reviews. These stay inside Google.
  • Bookings made on Google itself, through a booking partner, are taken on the partner's page, not yours, unless the partner sends people to your site.

If you take a deposit after a call by emailing a payment link, that payment has no visitor ID and counts in your total as Unattributed. Use the profile's own report for calls and directions, and Virling for what happens on the site.

Install the script and connect payments

Put the Virling script in the <head> of every page. Shopify themes take it in theme.liquid, and WordPress or WooCommerce sites can use the Virling WordPress plugin (add the script). In the app, go to Setup, Connect payments, and click Connect and backfill with a read-only key.

  • Deposits and bookings through Stripe Payment Links, Polar or Dodo Payments links need no code: the script attaches the visitor ID when the link is clicked on your site. A custom checkout passes the virling_vid cookie; see link checkout to visits.
  • Shopify needs a read_orders token and the cart snippet (Shopify docs). WooCommerce needs a Read REST key and the order snippet (WooCommerce docs).

More options are on ecommerce integrations. Test it by tapping your profile's appointment link on a phone and completing a booking in test mode.

A salon tags its Website button utm_content=website and its appointment link utm_content=appointment, both on business-profile. After a month, the Campaign tab row for business-profile shows 300 visitors and $1,500 in revenue, or $5.00 per visitor. Filtering to that campaign, the Landing page tab splits it:

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Google Business Profile visits turn into paying customers?

Replace the website address on your profile with a tagged one: utm_source=google, utm_medium=organic and utm_campaign=business-profile. Tag the appointment link the same way, add the script to your site and pass the visitor ID into checkout. The business-profile row in the Campaign tab then shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor.

Should the appointment link use the same tags as the website button?

Use the same source, medium and campaign so both add up to one profile total, and a different utm_content, such as website and appointment. Filtering to the campaign and opening the Landing page tab then shows which link brought the paying customers.

Why do clicks from the Google Maps app show up as Direct?

The Maps app often opens your site with no referrer. With no referrer and no tag, Virling has nothing to go on and counts the visit as Direct. With utm_source=google on the link, the same click counts as Organic search and carries your campaign.

How do I track several locations?

Give every location's profile the same utm_campaign and put the branch name in utm_content. If you want each branch as its own row in the Campaign tab, use one campaign per branch instead, such as business-profile-northside.

Does Virling count calls and direction requests from my profile?

No. A call opens the phone dialler and a direction request opens Maps, so neither loads your website. Use the profile's own report for those. A payment link you send after a call has no visitor ID and counts as Unattributed.
