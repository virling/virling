Snapchat revenue tracking: which snaps and stories bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-20.

To see which Snaps and Stories bring paying customers, attach a tagged link to each Snap you post, tag the website on your Public Profile, and join each payment to the visit before it. Snapchat clicks come in short bursts: a Story is gone after 24 hours, and most of its taps come soon after you post. Snapchat's own insights count views and taps on the link; they stop before anyone pays.

This is for online stores and for apps that sell subscriptions. The difference is where the payment happens: a store checkout, or a web paywall connected through RevenueCat.

Links in Stories and Spotlight

  • Stories. Attach a link to a Snap with the link tool before you add it to your Story. Each Story is its own burst of a day, so give it its own campaign, such as snap-2026-11-20-apron-restock, and point it at the product it shows, not your home page.
  • Spotlight. Spotlight shows your Snaps to people who do not follow you, and a Snap there can keep getting views long after a Story would have expired. Where a Spotlight Snap cannot carry a link, viewers who want more open your profile instead.
  • Public Profile. The website on your profile stays up for months, so it gets one fixed campaign, snap-profile. A jump in snap-profile visits the day a Spotlight Snap takes off is usually that Snap at work.

Text written on a Snap is not a link. Anyone who types an address they saw in a caption arrives as Direct, so if a Snap must show one, use a short page of its own, such as /snap, and its visits are still visible in the Pages card. The UTM builder makes tagged links, and a tracked link keeps them short: open the Campaign tab on the Channels card, click + Tracked link, pick Other and type snapchat as the source. The Snapchat in-app browser

Snapchat opens links in its own browser, on top of the Snap, and that browser often sends no referrer. Virling credits the in-app browsers of Instagram, TikTok and a few other apps from their user agent, but not Snapchat's, so an untagged tap from a Story counts as Direct. A tagged one counts as Referral, because snapchat is not a known social source, and a visit that does bring a Snapchat referrer is Referral too. The full rule order is in channels.

The in-app browser also keeps its own cookies, apart from Safari or Chrome. A viewer who taps a Story, closes it and buys later in their usual browser gets a new visitor ID there, so that purchase is joined to the visits in that browser, not to the Snap. A Story that links straight to the product page, with checkout a tap away, keeps more of its buyers inside the visit it started.

Snapchat Ads or organic Snaps

Use the same utm_source=snapchat for both and let the medium tell them apart. A paid medium, paid_social, paid or cpc, puts the visit in the Paid channel whatever the source; organic Snaps keep utm_medium=social and land in Referral.

An ad link with no paid medium lands wherever its referrer puts it, often Direct, and its spend is then mixed with organic results. Snapchat Ads Manager counts conversions with its own pixel and its own windows, so expect its numbers to differ from the payments Virling joins to visits.

When a Snap opens your app instead of your site

If you have an app, a link to your domain can open the app on a phone where it is installed, depending on how the app's links are set up, and a link to an app store page always leaves the web. Either way the Virling script never runs, no visit is recorded, and a purchase made in the app counts as Unattributed.

To keep the credit, point Snaps at a web page the app does not claim, such as a web paywall, set the visitor ID as the virling_vid attribute on the RevenueCat customer before the purchase, and connect RevenueCat. Renewals keep the channel of the first payment, so a Story that brought a subscriber keeps the credit. See set up Virling for a mobile app.

Joining payments to Snaps

For a store, add the script to every page and pass the virling_vid cookie into checkout; Stripe Payment Links, Polar and Dodo Payments links get it on click, and Shopify and WooCommerce stores use a small snippet. Each provider is in link checkout to visits.

Pick a range that covers the Story's day and a few days after, open Referral in the Channels card and click it, then switch to the Campaign tab: each snap- campaign is a row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and ad campaigns are under Paid. A buyer who comes back later by typing your address is still credited to the Snap under the default model, last non-direct, inside the 90-day lookback window; see model and lookback window. If you post Stories on Instagram too, tracking Instagram story stickers covers links there.

When the numbers look odd

  • Direct rose the day you posted a Story. A Snap went out without a link attached, or showed an address people typed. Attach tagged links, or use a short page such as /snap.
  • Ad clicks show up in Referral. The ad links use utm_medium=social. Switch them to paid_social.
  • App installs went up but no Snapchat revenue. The Snaps opened the app or the app store, so no visit was recorded. Send them to a web paywall.
  • Taps but no revenue. Buyers may be finishing in another browser, or the visitor ID is not reaching checkout or RevenueCat. See Unattributed revenue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track revenue from Snapchat?

Attach a tagged link to each Snap you add to your Story, with utm_source=snapchat and a campaign for that Story, tag the website on your Public Profile, and pass the visitor ID into checkout or RevenueCat. The Campaign tab then shows revenue per Story.

Why do Snapchat visits show up as Direct?

Snapchat opens links in its own in-app browser, which often sends no referrer, and Virling does not recognize that browser from its user agent. A utm_source=snapchat tag moves those visits to Referral.

Do Snapchat Ads and organic Snaps land in the same channel?

No, if you tag them. Ad links with utm_medium=paid_social, paid or cpc count as Paid; organic Snaps tagged utm_medium=social count as Referral, because snapchat is not a known social source.

Can I see which Story sent a paying customer?

Yes, if each Story has its own utm_campaign or tracked link. Each payment's journey on the Payments page shows the visit it was credited to.

What happens when a Snap opens my app instead of my site?

No visit is recorded, so a purchase made in the app counts as Unattributed. Point Snaps at a web paywall your app does not claim and pass the visitor ID to RevenueCat before the purchase.
