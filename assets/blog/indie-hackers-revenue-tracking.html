Indie Hackers revenue tracking: which posts and milestones bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-18.

Indie Hackers revenue tracking means following the money paid by customers whose credited visit came from indiehackers.com or from a link tagged indiehackers. On Indie Hackers you rarely post once. You share milestones, revenue updates and lessons for months, and your profile and product page link to your site the whole time, so the question is which of those posts and links bring paying customers, not how many upvotes one got.

Readers there are mostly other founders. Many read a revenue post to compare notes, follow your updates for a while, and try your product much later, if it is for them at all. That makes Indie Hackers a slow channel: a little traffic from each post, and payments spread over weeks.

Milestone and revenue posts

A post about your first customer or a revenue number is the most read thing you will put on Indie Hackers, and you will write several. Each one sends a few days of visits, and readers who sign up from one may pay after a later one reminds them. To compare posts, each needs its own tag, because the referrer only says indiehackers.com:

https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=ih-first-customer
https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=ih-revenue-update-nov
https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=indiehackers&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=ih-product-page

Keep utm_source=indiehackers and utm_medium=community the same on every link and change only the campaign. Add utm_content if one post links twice, for example in the text and in a comment. The UTM builder makes the links, and the build in public revenue post template writes the update itself.

Profile and product page links

Your profile and your product page on Indie Hackers link to your site and keep sending visitors long after any post has dropped off the feed. That steady trickle is easy to mistake for post traffic. Put a tag with its own campaign on those links, so the Campaign tab shows the product page as one row that grows slowly over months, next to the posts that each had their week. Links you leave in comments on other people's threads are hard to tag one by one; untagged, they still count as Indie Hackers through the referrer.

The Indie Hackers referrer

A click on indiehackers.com in a browser usually arrives with indiehackers.com as the referrer, with or without www. In Virling that host is in the Communities channel and its row is named Indie Hackers. With no referrer, Virling reads utm_source, then ref; indiehackers is known and lands on the same row, while a short form such as ih is not, so it would become a Referral row of its own. A medium of cpc or email moves the visit to Paid or Email even with the referrer, which is why community is the medium to use. The full rule order is in channels.

Behind that row, Virling ties a reader's later visits to the first one with a cookie and joins the payment to them through the visitor ID you pass into checkout. The quickstart covers the script, the payment provider and that checkout step.

Share revenue by channel publicly

Revenue posts on Indie Hackers are usually a screenshot of a total. With a public dashboard with revenue by channel, the milestone can link a live page instead, showing which channels the money came from, Indie Hackers among them. Turn it on in Site settings under Public dashboard. If you do not want to show amounts, Hide revenue amounts swaps them for shares of revenue and ratios against your average, and visitor counts stay visible. Readers can check the number themselves, and the link in the post is one more tagged source on your own dashboard.

See which post brought paying customers

  1. Pick a range of a few months, so slow payments from older posts and the product page are inside it.
  2. In the Channels card, open the Source tab and click Indie Hackers to filter the page.
  3. Switch to the Campaign tab. Each tagged post and the product page has its own row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor.
  4. Compare the default model, last non-direct, with first touch. If a post gains revenue under first touch, readers found you there and paid after a later search or post. See the model and lookback window.
  5. Open a payment from the Payments page to see the visits before it. A reader who clicked two of your posts before paying shows both.

With only a handful of paying readers, one customer can make a post look like your best one; see how a startup can decide with few payments.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • An Indie Hackers campaign shows up in Referral. The source is one Virling does not know, such as ih. Use indiehackers.
  • Every post looks the same size. The links are untagged, so all clicks share one Indie Hackers row. Tag the next post.
  • Product page revenue shows under a post. The product page link carries a post's campaign. Give it its own.
  • Visits but no revenue at all. The visitor ID is not reaching checkout; see Unattributed revenue.

If you also launch on Product Hunt, Product Hunt launch revenue covers that day, and which Hacker News visits pay covers a Show HN spike; both stay separate rows in Communities. Community revenue tracking covers Discord, Slack and other forums.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see revenue from Indie Hackers?

Find Indie Hackers in the Source tab of the Channels card, where visits from indiehackers.com land, and pick a range of a few months. To split it by post, milestone or product page, tag each link with its own utm_campaign and open the Campaign tab.

Can I tell which Indie Hackers post brought a customer?

Not from the referrer alone. Posts, milestones, product pages and comments all share the indiehackers.com host, and the referrer carries only the host. Give each link its own utm_campaign, and utm_content if you want the place inside the post, and the Campaign tab shows each one separately.

Which utm_source should I use for Indie Hackers?

Use indiehackers. Virling knows it as a Communities source and puts it on the same Indie Hackers row as the indiehackers.com referrer. A short tag such as ih is not known, so it would land in Referral as its own row. When a referrer is present it wins over the tag.

Can I share my revenue by channel in an Indie Hackers milestone?

Yes. Turn on Public dashboard in Site settings and link the page in your post. It shows revenue by channel and stays up to date, and Hide revenue amounts shows shares and ratios instead of dollar amounts if you would rather not share the total.
