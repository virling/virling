Le Chat traffic analytics: which Mistral referral visits pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-21.

To see whether visits from Mistral's Le Chat turn into paying customers, find the visits that arrive from chat.mistral.ai, pass the visitor ID into checkout, and join each payment to the visit before it. You then see the revenue Le Chat referral traffic brings and what each Le Chat visitor is worth next to search, social and email. A visit count alone cannot tell you that, because the question is not how many people Le Chat sends but how many of them pay. One thing to know up front: Virling does not list Le Chat as an AI assistant yet, so its visits show up in the Referral channel, as chat.mistral.ai.

This post covers Le Chat only. For assistants Virling counts under AI, see ChatGPT revenue tracking and DeepSeek revenue tracking; for AI assistants in general, see is AI search traffic worth anything.

How Le Chat visits show up

The AI channel covers a fixed list of assistant hosts: chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com. chat.mistral.ai is not on it. When a Le Chat answer links to your site and someone clicks, the visit can reach you in two ways.

  • The chat.mistral.ai referrer. Le Chat on the web runs at chat.mistral.ai, so a click there arrives with that host as the referrer when the browser sends one. Any referrer host that is not a known AI, search, community or social host is Referral, so the visit lands there and shows as chat.mistral.ai in the Referrer breakdown and in the Referral channel's sources, with the revenue credited to it. A visit referred by mistral.ai itself, such as its docs, is a separate Referral row. The referrer is a host only, so it never says which answer or question the click came from.
  • No referrer. Links opened from the Le Chat mobile apps, or anywhere else the referrer is dropped, can arrive with none. Nothing in the visit names Le Chat, so it counts as Direct, in Virling and in most other tools.

So the chat.mistral.ai row is a floor, not a full count, and it is not part of your AI total. When you compare assistants, add chat.mistral.ai from Referral to the AI channel yourself. People who read an answer and later type your name into a browser or a search engine arrive as Direct or Organic search; no analytics tool can tie that visit to the answer. The full rule order is in channels, and the traffic source checker shows how a given landing URL and referrer are credited: enter chat.mistral.ai as the referrer to see it land in Referral.

Tag the links you control

You cannot put the referrer back on a visit that arrived without one, but a utm_source keeps a visit out of Direct. Wherever you place a link meant for Le Chat users, such as a prompt you share or a page you expect to be quoted, tag it, for example ?utm_source=chat.mistral.ai&utm_campaign=le-chat.

  • The source. With no referrer, Virling reads utm_source. Neither mistral nor chat.mistral.ai is a known source, so a tagged visit counts as Referral, not AI, and not Direct.
  • The campaign. The Source tab is keyed by referrer host, so a tagged visit with no referrer does not join the chat.mistral.ai row. Add utm_campaign=le-chat and those visits, with their revenue, show by name in the Campaign tab of the Channels card. The UTM builder makes the links.
  • A referrer wins. When the referrer is present it decides the channel, and the tag only names the campaign. A paid medium wins over both: utm_medium=cpc counts as Paid.

A "where did you hear about us" field at signup catches people who read a Le Chat answer and came back later. Keep it next to the dashboard number rather than mixing the two.

Set up Le Chat revenue tracking in Virling

  1. Add the tracking script to every page. See the tracking script. Nothing Le Chat-specific is needed: the referrer and the tags are read on every visit.
  2. Connect your payment provider: Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments, Creem, RevenueCat, Shopify or WooCommerce.
  3. Pass the visitor ID into checkout. Stripe Payment Links, Polar checkout links and Dodo Payments links get it from the script on click. For a checkout you create on your server, pass the virling_vid cookie, for example as client_reference_id on a Stripe Checkout Session. See link checkout to visits.   4. Open the dashboard and pick a range of a few weeks or more. Short ranges with few visitors are noisy.
  5. In the Channels card, click Referral, or switch to the Source tab, and find chat.mistral.ai. The row shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, with a chip that compares it with your site average. If you tag links, check the Campaign tab for your Le Chat campaign too.
  6. Click the chat.mistral.ai row to filter the whole page. The Pages card now shows which landing pages Le Chat sends people to, and which of them lead to payments.
  7. Open a payment from the Payments page to see the buyer's journey: every visit before the payment, with the credited one marked.

How Virling credits the payment

People often click a link in a Le Chat answer, leave, and come back days later by typing your URL. With the default model, last non-direct, that later Direct visit does not take the credit: the payment goes to the Le Chat visit, as long as it started inside the lookback window, 90 days by default. If the buyer subscribes, every renewal keeps counting toward chat.mistral.ai. If they come back through a Google search or a newsletter before paying, that later visit takes the credit instead.

To see how often Le Chat starts journeys that another channel finishes, switch the model to first touch in Site settings. First-touch or last-touch attribution explains the difference. Changing the model recalculates every past payment, so you can switch back.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • Le Chat is missing from the AI channel. That is expected today. Look in Referral, or in the Referrer breakdown, for chat.mistral.ai.
  • chat.mistral.ai has visitors but no revenue. Check the Matched revenue line under the Overview numbers. If a large share of revenue is unmatched, the visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.
  • Your tagged Le Chat links are not in the chat.mistral.ai row. Tagged visits with no referrer have no referrer host, so they are not in the Source tab row. Find them by campaign in the Campaign tab.
  • A buyer says they found you in Le Chat but the payment is credited elsewhere. They may have read the answer without clicking, or opened the link from an app that dropped the referrer. That visit has no trace of Le Chat.
  • Visitors who block the script are not counted, so Le Chat's real visitor number can be higher than any analytics tool shows. Their revenue still counts in your total, as Unattributed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Le Chat visits turn into paying customers?

Join each payment to the visit before it. In Virling, pass the visitor ID into checkout, then open the Referral channel, or the Referrer breakdown, and find chat.mistral.ai: it shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor. The Payments page shows each buyer's visits, with the credited one marked.

How do I track Mistral Le Chat referral traffic?

Look for chat.mistral.ai in your referrers. Virling does not list Le Chat as an AI assistant yet, so a visit with a chat.mistral.ai referrer counts in the Referral channel and shows as chat.mistral.ai, with the revenue it brought.

Why is Le Chat not in the AI channel?

The AI channel covers a fixed list of assistant hosts: chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com. chat.mistral.ai is not on it, so Le Chat visits are Referral. Add them up yourself when you compare AI traffic.

Why does some Le Chat traffic show up as Direct?

A visit with no referrer and no utm_source has nothing that names Le Chat, so it is Direct in Virling and in most other tools. That can happen when a link is opened from the mobile app or a browser that drops the referrer. Treat the chat.mistral.ai number as a floor.

Does a Le Chat visit still get credit if the buyer pays a week later?

With the default model, last non-direct, yes. If the buyer comes back by typing your URL, that Direct visit does not take the credit; the payment goes to the Le Chat visit as long as it is inside the lookback window, 90 days by default.
