MailerLite revenue tracking: which emails bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-25.

MailerLite revenue tracking means joining each payment to the email click that brought the buyer. Turn on UTM tags in MailerLite so links carry utm_source=mailerlite, utm_medium=email and a utm_campaign, add the Virling script to your site, and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Virling counts the click as Email and credits the payment to it, so the Campaign tab shows revenue per email, next to every other channel.

Step 1: turn on UTM tags in MailerLite

MailerLite can add the tags for you, but campaigns and automation emails have separate switches (source: MailerLite, How To Use Google Analytics On Your Campaigns and Websites):

  • Campaigns. On the Campaign details page, find Tracking options and toggle UTM tags.
  • Automations. Open the workflow editor, click the email step and, under UTM tags in the sidebar, check Add parameters to your URLs to track newsletter traffic on web analytics platforms. Do it for each email step.
  • Your own values. Under Account settings, the Link tracking tab has UTM code fields where you enter the values you want.

When we read it in November 2026, that page did not list the default values, say whether a field can hold the name of the campaign, or say what happens to a link that already has UTM tags. So send yourself one email and read the address bar, as in the check below, before you rely on it. If every email arrives with the same utm_campaign, all of them share one row. In that case, turn UTM tags off for that email and paste links you tagged yourself:

https://example.com/pricing?utm_source=mailerlite&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=launch-teams-2026-11

The free UTM builder makes these. Use one method per email, so the same key is not set twice.

Name campaigns, automations and landing pages

Whatever arrives as utm_campaign becomes a report row. Keep the source mailerlite and the medium email on everything you send, and change only the campaign:

  • Campaigns: one short lowercase name per send, with the date, such as launch-teams-2026-11.
  • Automations: the workflow and the step, such as welcome-1 and welcome-2. Keep them the same for as long as the workflow runs.
  • Landing pages: the prefix lp- and the page, such as lp-free-guide, with utm_medium=landing_page on the buttons that lead to your site. Keep email for links people click in an email.

How Virling sorts a MailerLite visit

Every session gets one channel from its first pageview, and the first matching rule wins:

  • An email medium. email, newsletter or e-mail, in any case, is Email, whatever the referrer or source says.
  • A paid medium. cpc, ppc, paid, paidsearch, paid_social or paidsocial is Paid.
  • Neither, with a referrer. The referring site decides. A button on a landing page hosted on a domain that is not yours is Referral, even with utm_medium=landing_page.
  • Neither, no referrer, with a source. mailerlite is not a known search, social, community or AI source, so utm_source=mailerlite alone is Referral.
  • No tags and no referrer. The visit is Direct. Many email apps send no referrer, so this is where untagged clicks usually go.

Spelling counts: mail and e_mail are not Email. See how channels are decided.

Why opens and clicks do not show revenue

  • An open is not a visit. MailerLite counts opens with an invisible pixel and says Apple Mail Privacy Protection may affect your campaign reports (source: MailerLite, How to enable and disable tracking in MailerLite campaigns).
  • A click is not always a person. Some mail servers click links to check them, and MailerLite has a help page on it (source: MailerLite, Why does my campaign show unusually high opens or clicks?).
  • A click happens before the payment. The report lists total and unique clicks per link (source: MailerLite, How To Analyze And Export Reports). Clicks alone do not say who paid days later.

MailerLite does have e-commerce tracking. With a connected store and tracking on, a purchase goes to the last campaign or automation email the subscriber clicked, within 7, 14 or 30 days, if they buy with the same email address (source: MailerLite, How E-Commerce Tracking Works). That names the last email clicked. Virling also sees the search or post visit that came after it, and credits each payment once.

Step 2: add the script and link checkout

Add the Virling script to the <head> of every page an email can land on, and to your app; see the script docs. Then connect your payment provider and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Payment links from Stripe, Polar, Dodo Payments and LemonSqueezy get the ID from the script when they are on a page that runs it; a checkout you create on your server needs the virling_vid cookie. Shopify and WooCommerce need a short snippet. See link checkout to visits.

Link emails to a page on your site, not straight to a payment link. A payment link opened from an email has no visitor ID.

MailerLite landing pages

Virling keeps visits from your domain and its subdomains. On a landing page at a subdomain of yours, you can add the script and the buttons need no tags, because the visit is already tracked with the source that brought it. MailerLite puts custom code under Edit settings, in Analytics and custom code, and its blog says adding it to a website takes a paid account (source: MailerLite, 14 Tools to Expand Your MailerLite Website Capabilities). On any other address, tag the buttons as above.

MailerLite can also sell Stripe products from a Products block on a landing page (source: MailerLite, How to Sell Stripe Products in MailerLite). That help page shows no place for a visitor ID, so expect those payments to be Unattributed unless the payer email matches a visitor who shared it on your site before.

Step 3: read revenue per email

  • In the Channels card, click Email. The page filters to visits with an email medium.
  • Open the Campaign tab. Each utm_campaign is a row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor.
  • Open a payment from the Payments page to see the visits before it, with the credited one marked.

By default the whole payment, after refunds, goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct. A subscriber who clicks on Monday and types your address on Thursday is credited to the email; one who returns from a search result, to Organic search. Renewals copy the credit of the first payment. Check it with one email and one payment

  1. Make a group with only your own address. Send it a real campaign with one link and utm_campaign=tracking-test.
  2. Copy the link from the email into a private window. MailerLite sends clicks through its own domain, or yours if you set that up, to count them (source: MailerLite, What Is Mlsend?). Expected: your page opens and the address bar shows utm_source, utm_medium=email and utm_campaign=tracking-test.
  3. In the dashboard, look at the Channels card. Expected: a visit in Email, and a tracking-test row on the Campaign tab.
  4. In the same window, go to checkout and pay in your provider’s test mode, with a test key connected. See test your setup.
  5. Wait a minute for the next sync and open the payment. Expected: the channel is Email and the journey starts at your tagged visit. Test payments stay in your reports after you switch to a live key.

Repeat the first three steps with one automation email.

What to check if the numbers look off

  • MailerLite revenue shows as Direct. UTM tags are off for that email, or a redirect on your site dropped them; see UTM parameters lost after redirect.
  • Email revenue shows as Referral. The medium is not one of the three email values.
  • One email is split across rows. The campaign value changed, or differs in case.
  • MailerLite shows more clicks than Virling shows visitors. Mail servers click, people click twice, and some browsers block scripts.
  • Revenue shows as Unattributed. The visitor ID is not reaching checkout, or the visit is older than the window.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which MailerLite email brought a paying customer?

Turn on UTM tags in MailerLite so your links carry utm_medium=email and a utm_campaign, add the Virling script to your site, and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Each payment is credited to the tagged visit, and the Campaign tab shows revenue per campaign.

Which channel does a MailerLite click land in?

Email, if the link carries utm_medium=email, newsletter or e-mail. The medium is checked first, so it wins over any referrer. With only a utm_source of mailerlite and no referrer the visit is Referral, and with no tags and no referrer it is Direct.

Does MailerLite add UTM tags to automation emails?

Yes, if you check the box. Open the email step in the workflow editor and, under UTM tags in the sidebar, check Add parameters to your URLs to track newsletter traffic on web analytics platforms. Do it for every email step.

Do MailerLite opens count as revenue in Virling?

No. Virling credits payments to visits, so an email counts only when someone clicks a link in it and lands on a page that runs the script. An email that was only opened is never credited.

Why does MailerLite show different revenue from Virling?

They use different rules. MailerLite credits store purchases to the last email the subscriber clicked within 7, 14 or 30 days, when they buy with the same email address. Virling credits each payment to one visit, by default the latest in 90 days that was not Direct, from any channel.

Does Virling connect to my MailerLite account?

No. The setup on this page uses only the tags on your links, the Virling script and your payments. Keep sending with MailerLite.
