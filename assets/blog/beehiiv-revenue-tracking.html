beehiiv UTM tracking: see which issue brings paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-23.

beehiiv UTM tracking lets you join each payment on your product or store to the beehiiv issue and link that sent the buyer. beehiiv already adds UTM tags to the links in every post: your publication domain as utm_source, Email as utm_medium and the post title as utm_campaign. Keep those tags on your links, pass the visitor ID into checkout, and Virling credits each payment to the tagged visit, so the Campaign tab shows revenue per issue. beehiiv’s Clicks Report counts verified clicks per link; it ends at the click and never sees the sale.

beehiiv’s UTM Analytics settings

According to beehiiv’s help article on UTM tracking, the Automatic UTM Tagging toggle is on by default. It lives under Settings, General Info, in the UTM Analytics section, and it fills in three values:

  • utm_source: your publication domain, such as yourname.beehiiv.com or your custom domain.
  • utm_medium: Email for a subscriber who opened the newsletter, Referral for someone viewing the post online.
  • utm_campaign: a URL-friendly version of the post title, such as pricing-teardown.

You can edit all three in that section. Two rules matter for revenue: if a link already has utm_source, utm_medium or utm_campaign, beehiiv does not overwrite it, and utm_term and utm_content cannot be set in the settings at all. Add those by hand; beehiiv places them before its own tags in the URL.

beehiiv’s Clicks Report and where it stops

On paid plans, Analyze, Clicks Report lists every link in your posts with its verified total and unique clicks from email subscribers, shown with the first UTM on the URL (beehiiv: understanding your Clicks Report). That tells you which link was clicked most. It cannot tell you whether the reader bought, cancelled or came back a week later and paid. A link with fewer clicks can still bring more money, which is why the payment has to be joined to the visit on your side.

An email app usually sends no referrer, so the UTM tags are the only proof a visit came from beehiiv. Turn tagging off, or let a redirect strip the query string, and that visit and its payment fall into Direct. More on visits with no referrer: dark social revenue tracking.

An issue link, end to end

With the defaults, a reader who clicks the pricing link in your email arrives at:

https://example.com/pricing?utm_source=yourname.beehiiv.com&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=pricing-teardown

  • Leave the source alone. The publication domain is the same on every send, so the whole newsletter rolls up under one source.
  • Let the post title name the campaign. Every issue gets its own row without you typing anything.
  • Add utm_content=intro or utm_content=footer when one issue links to the same page twice and you want to know which placement sold.
  • Welcome emails and automations are not posts, so check whether their links carry tags; if not, build them with the free UTM builder.

Send yourself a test, click through from your inbox and read the address bar on your site. Whatever tags survive to that page are what Virling records.

Paid recommendations and the Recommendations Network

Boosts are now called paid recommendations, and Email Boosts and Direct Links have been retired (beehiiv: Boosts to paid recommendations). Together with free recommendations they form the beehiiv Recommendations Network: your top four picks can appear in your signup flow after someone subscribes, or inside posts (beehiiv: how free recommendations work).

  • Subscribers you gain from recommendations join your list inside beehiiv; they do not visit your site at that moment, so Virling has nothing to record yet. Once they click a link in one of your issues, that visit is tagged like any other and the issue gets the credit.
  • Money you earn by recommending others is paid per verified subscriber you send (beehiiv: paid outgoing recommendations) through a separate Stripe Express account. It is not a buyer paying you after a visit, so it does not belong in your attribution.
  • If you pay for incoming recommendations, compare the price per subscriber with what a subscriber is worth to you. Revenue on the Email channel over a period, divided by your list size, is a rough figure; the email subscriber value calculator does the sum.

How Virling files a beehiiv click

Virling picks a session’s channel from its first pageview and reads utm_medium before anything else, case-insensitively. email, newsletter and e-mail all mean the Email channel, so beehiiv’s Email qualifies. Its web value, Referral, is not on that list, so a web reader is filed by referrer instead, or by utm_source when the referrer is missing. beehiiv hosts are not on Virling’s known lists, so those readers show in Referral under your publication’s domain. Either way the campaign is saved. Details: how channels are decided.

By default a payment, net of refunds, goes to the buyer’s most recent non-Direct visit within 90 days. So a subscriber who clicks Tuesday’s issue and buys on Friday after typing your URL is still counted for Tuesday’s issue. Details: the attribution model.

Reading a paid subscriber or a product sale

Where the money is taken decides what Virling can see.

  • A sale in your own checkout, such as a course, an app plan or a store order. Pass the visitor ID into checkout (link checkout to visits) and the payment is credited to the issue that sent the buyer. It appears under Email, on that issue’s campaign row.
  • A beehiiv paid subscription or digital product. beehiiv processes both through the Stripe account you connect to it (beehiiv: Stripe for paid subscriptions and digital products). Connect that same Stripe account to Virling with a restricted key and the payments are imported, but the checkout starts inside beehiiv, so no visitor ID reaches it and they show as Unattributed.
  • Matching those by email. If readers also give you their email on your own site, send it as an identify event. A later Stripe payment whose billing email matches is credited to that visitor and shows Matched by email. See unattributed revenue.

On the dashboard, click Email on the Channels card, then open the Campaign tab: each beehiiv issue is a row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor. Rank issues by revenue per visitor rather than clicks. Click a row to filter everything to that issue, and open a payment on the Payments page to see the sessions before it and which one was credited. Web readers sit under Referral instead.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which beehiiv issue brought a paying customer?

Keep beehiiv's Automatic UTM Tagging on, so each link carries your publication domain as utm_source, Email as utm_medium and the post title as utm_campaign. Connect your payment provider and pass the visitor ID into your checkout. Virling then credits each payment to the tagged visit, and the Campaign tab under Email lists revenue per issue.

Where are beehiiv's UTM settings?

Under Settings, General Info, in the UTM Analytics section. The Automatic UTM Tagging toggle is on by default, and you can edit the source, medium and campaign values there. utm_term and utm_content cannot be set there; add them to a link by hand.

Why do some beehiiv readers show under Referral instead of Email?

beehiiv sets utm_medium to Email for subscribers who opened the newsletter and to Referral for people viewing the post online. Referral is not an email medium in Virling, so web readers are filed by referrer and show in Referral under your publication's domain.

Can Virling attribute beehiiv paid subscriptions and digital products?

beehiiv takes those payments through the Stripe account you connect to it. Connect the same Stripe account to Virling and they are imported, but the checkout starts inside beehiiv with no visitor ID, so they show as Unattributed. If readers give you their email on your own site, an identify event lets Virling match a later Stripe payment by billing email.

Do beehiiv paid recommendations, formerly Boosts, show in Virling?

Not directly. A subscriber gained through a recommendation joins your list inside beehiiv without visiting your site, and money earned by recommending others is paid by beehiiv per verified subscriber, not by a buyer after a visit. Once a recommended subscriber clicks a link in one of your issues, that visit is tracked like any other.
