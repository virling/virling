Gemini referral traffic: which Gemini visits pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-18.

To see which Gemini visits turn into paying customers, count every visit that arrives from gemini.google.com or carries a Gemini utm_source, pass the visitor ID into checkout, and join each payment to the visit before it. You then see the revenue Gemini referral traffic brings and what each Gemini visitor is worth next to search, social and email. A visit count alone cannot tell you that, because Gemini usually sends few visitors and the question is how many of them pay.

This post covers Gemini only. For ChatGPT, see ChatGPT revenue tracking; for AI assistants in general, see is AI search traffic worth anything.

How Gemini visits show up

Gemini answers can link to the pages they draw on. When someone clicks one, the visit can reach your site in three ways, and each one is classified differently.

  • The gemini.google.com referrer. A click on a link in Gemini in the browser usually arrives with gemini.google.com as the referrer. Virling puts that host in the AI channel and lists it as Gemini. It is checked before the search rules, so it is not counted as Google search even though it is a Google address, and it never merges into the google.com row. The referrer is a host only, so it never says which answer or question the click came from.
  • A utm_source tag. When the referrer is missing, Virling reads utm_source. A visit tagged utm_source=gemini.google.com is credited to Gemini, in the AI channel, exactly as if the referrer had been there. A bare utm_source=gemini also counts as AI. You only see these tags on links that carry them; Virling does not add them.
  • No referrer and no tag. Links opened from the Gemini app, or anywhere else the referrer is dropped, can arrive with neither. Nothing in the visit names Gemini, so it counts as Direct, in Virling and in most other tools. Virling labels some Direct visits by the app they were opened in, and one label is Google app, but that covers anything opened in the Google app, not only Gemini answers.

So the Gemini row is a floor, not a full count. People who read an answer and later type your name into a browser or a search engine arrive as Direct or Organic search; no analytics tool can tie that visit to the answer. AI Overviews and AI Mode are part of Google Search, not the Gemini app: clicks from them arrive from google.com and count as Organic search. The full rule order is in channels, and the traffic source checker shows how a given landing URL and referrer are credited: paste a URL with utm_source=gemini.google.com and an empty referrer to see it land in AI.

One exception to the order: a paid medium wins over everything. A link with utm_medium=cpc counts as Paid even if its source is gemini.google.com, so ads you run stay apart from answers.

Let Gemini read your pages

Gemini can only link to pages Google can read. Google does not use a separate Gemini crawler for this; Googlebot fetches your pages. Google-Extended is a robots.txt token, not a separate bot, that tells Google whether it may use what Googlebot crawls for Gemini. Blocking it does not remove your pages from Google Search.

The AI crawler checker reads your robots.txt and tells you whether Google-Extended and the other AI bots may read a page. Bot fetches are never counted as visitors in Virling. Virling recognizes live answer fetches from ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity, but no Gemini bot of that kind, so Gemini answers do not show as fetches on the AI card.

Set up Gemini revenue tracking in Virling

  1. Add the tracking script to every page. See the tracking script. Nothing Gemini-specific is needed: the referrer and the tag are read on every visit.
  2. Connect your payment provider: Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments, Creem, RevenueCat, Shopify or WooCommerce.
  3. Pass the visitor ID into checkout. Stripe Payment Links, Polar checkout links and Dodo Payments links get it from the script on click. For a checkout you create on your server, pass the virling_vid cookie, for example as client_reference_id on a Stripe Checkout Session. See link checkout to visits. Without this step Gemini shows visitors and no revenue.
  4. Open the dashboard and pick a range of a few weeks or more. Gemini usually sends fewer visitors than search, so short ranges are noisy.
  5. In the Channels card, click AI, or switch to the Source tab, and find Gemini. The row shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, with a chip that compares it with your site average.
  6. Click the row to filter the whole page. The Pages card now shows which landing pages Gemini sends people to, and which of them lead to payments.
  7. Open a payment from the Payments page to see the buyer's journey: every visit before the payment, with the credited one marked.

How Virling credits the payment

People often click a link in Gemini, leave, and come back days later by typing your URL. With the default model, last non-direct, that later Direct visit does not take the credit: the payment goes to the Gemini visit, as long as it started inside the lookback window, 90 days by default. If the buyer subscribes, every renewal keeps counting toward Gemini. If they come back through a Google search or a newsletter before paying, that later visit takes the credit instead.

To see how often Gemini starts journeys that another channel finishes, switch the model to first touch in Site settings. First-touch or last-touch attribution explains the difference. Changing the model recalculates every past payment, so you can switch back.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • Gemini has visitors but no revenue. Check the Matched revenue line under the Overview numbers. If a large share of revenue is unmatched, the visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.
  • A buyer says they found you in Gemini but the payment is credited elsewhere. They may have read the answer without clicking, opened the link from an app that dropped the referrer, or seen you in an AI Overview, which counts as Organic search. That visit has no trace of Gemini.
  • Gemini shows no visits at all. Check that robots.txt does not block Googlebot, and look at the Answers tab of the AI card to see whether Gemini names you when asked your buyer questions.
  • Visitors who block the script are not counted, so Gemini's real visitor number can be higher than any analytics tool shows. Their revenue still counts in your total, as Unattributed.

If your Google clicks come from the results page rather than the Gemini app, read what you can measure from AI Overviews and AI Mode. Microsoft has a similar split, covered in Copilot visits versus Bing search.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Gemini visits turn into paying customers?

Use a tool that groups gemini.google.com visits into their own source and joins each payment to the visit before it. In Virling, pass the visitor ID into checkout, then open the AI channel and the Gemini row: it shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and the Payments page shows each buyer's visits.

How do I track traffic from Gemini?

Look for gemini.google.com in your referrers. Virling puts every visit with that referrer in the AI channel and lists it as Gemini, apart from Google search. A visit with no referrer but utm_source=gemini.google.com or utm_source=gemini also counts as AI.

Why does some Gemini traffic show up as Direct?

A visit with no referrer and no utm_source has nothing that names Gemini, so it is Direct in Virling and in most other tools. That can happen when a link is opened from an app that drops the referrer. Treat the Gemini number as a floor.

Is Gemini traffic counted as Google search?

No. Virling checks AI hosts before search hosts, so gemini.google.com goes to the AI channel even though it is a Google address. Clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode inside Google Search arrive from google.com and count as Organic search.

Does a Gemini visit still get credit if the buyer pays a week later?

With the default model, last non-direct, yes. If the buyer comes back by typing your URL, that Direct visit does not take the credit; the payment goes to the Gemini visit as long as it is inside the lookback window, 90 days by default.
