ActiveCampaign revenue tracking: which emails lead to paid plans

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-26.

ActiveCampaign revenue tracking means joining each payment to the email click that brought the buyer. Turn on Google Analytics for each campaign and automation email, so links carry utm_medium=email and a utm_campaign, add the Virling script to your site, and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Virling counts the click as Email and credits the payment to it, including a trial that turns paid weeks later, so the Campaign tab shows revenue per email next to every other channel.

Step 1: turn on UTM tags in ActiveCampaign

ActiveCampaign adds UTM tags through its Google Analytics option. You do not need Google Analytics itself: the tags are plain URL parameters, and Virling reads them. There are two switches (source: ActiveCampaign, Use Google Analytics with your email campaigns):

  • One campaign. Click Email, open the campaign, go to the Campaign Summary page and set the Google Analytics toggle to On. A modal shows the Google Analytics Campaign Name, filled with the name of your campaign. Change it if you like and click Done.
  • Every new campaign. An admin can open Settings, then Advanced, set the Google Analytics Tracking toggle to On and click Save Settings. It applies to campaigns made after that, not to ones you already have.
  • Automation emails. The same page says the option works on direct and automated campaigns, and another says it is enabled on a per campaign or automation email basis (source: ActiveCampaign, How are UTM parameters used in campaigns?). Turn it on for each email in the automation.

ActiveCampaign then sets these values on every link in the email:

  • utm_source: ActiveCampaign with branding on, Newsletter with branding off.
  • utm_medium: email.
  • utm_campaign: the Google Analytics Campaign Name.
  • utm_content: the subject line.

The tags go on all links in the campaign, and you cannot leave one link out (source: ActiveCampaign, Can you enable Google Analytics for a campaign, but exclude a specific link from receiving parameters?). When we read these pages in November 2026, they did not show where the switch sits in the automation editor, or say what happens to a link that already has UTM tags. So send yourself one email and read the address bar, as in the check below, before you rely on it.

Name nurture, onboarding and trial emails

Whatever arrives as utm_campaign becomes a report row, and the default is the name of your campaign, spaces and capitals included. Type a short lowercase value into the Campaign Name field instead, and keep it the same for as long as the email runs:

  • Nurture, for subscribers who have not signed up yet: nurture-, the sequence and the step, such as nurture-guide-1 and nurture-guide-2.
  • Onboarding, for new users: onboarding- and the step, such as onboarding-day-2.
  • Trial, for users close to the end of a trial: trial- and the moment, such as trial-ends-3d.

Rows are split by exact value, so Onboarding-Day-2 and onboarding-day-2 are two rows. For links outside ActiveCampaign, the free UTM builder makes tags in the same style.

How Virling sorts an ActiveCampaign visit

Every session gets one channel from its first pageview, and the first matching rule wins:

  • A paid medium. cpc, ppc, paid, paidsearch, paid_social or paidsocial is Paid.
  • An email medium. email, newsletter or e-mail, in any case, is Email, whatever the referrer or source says.
  • Neither, with a referrer. The referring site decides, such as Organic search, Social or Referral.
  • Neither, no referrer, with a source. activecampaign is not a known search, social, community or AI source, so utm_source=activecampaign alone is Referral.
  • No tags and no referrer. The visit is Direct. Many email apps send no referrer, so an email with Google Analytics off usually lands here.

Step 2: add the script and link checkout

Add the Virling script to the <head> of every page an email can land on, and to your app; see the script docs. Then connect your payment provider and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Payment links from Stripe, Polar, Dodo Payments and LemonSqueezy get the ID from the script when they are on a page that runs it; a checkout you create on your server needs the virling_vid cookie. See link checkout to visits.

Link emails to a page on your site, not straight to a payment link. A payment link opened from an email has no visitor ID.

How a trial that converts weeks later is credited

By default Virling credits the whole payment to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct. Which visits count depends on how the trial starts:

  • Card up front. The trial starts with a checkout that charges nothing, and the card is charged later with no visit of its own. Virling reads the trial start (on Stripe, a checkout.session.completed event with a total of 0) and credits that later charge from the visits before the trial started, never counting the trial itself as revenue. A nurture click on day 1 and a trial on day 3 credit the first charge on day 17 to the nurture email. Clicks on onboarding or trial emails after the trial started do not change it.
  • No card. The user upgrades through checkout, which carries the visitor ID, and the window counts back from that payment. A tagged click on trial-ends-3d right before the upgrade is the latest visit that was not Direct, so it gets the credit, even if a search brought the user in.

If you want the visit that introduced each customer instead, switch to first touch in site settings; history is recomputed. Renewals copy the credit of the first payment. See the attribution model and renewals and free trials, and for the funnel side, trial-to-paid conversion by traffic source.

Step 3: read revenue per email

  • In the Channels card, click Email. The page filters to visits with an email medium.
  • Open the Campaign tab. Each utm_campaign is a row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor.
  • Open a payment from the Payments page to see the visits before it, with the credited one marked.

Check it with one email and one payment

  1. Make a list with only your own address. Send it a real campaign with one link, Google Analytics on and the campaign name tracking-test.
  2. Click the link in a private window. ActiveCampaign sends the click through one of its link tracking domains first, then to your page (source: ActiveCampaign, About link tracking). Expected: your page opens and the address bar shows utm_source, utm_medium=email and utm_campaign=tracking-test.
  3. In the dashboard, look at the Channels card. Expected: a visit in Email, and a tracking-test row on the Campaign tab.
  4. Connect a Stripe restricted key that starts with rk_test_. In the same window, go to checkout and pay with a Stripe test card. See test your setup.
  5. Wait a minute for the next sync and open the payment. Expected: the channel is Email and the journey starts at your tagged visit. Test payments stay in your reports after you switch to a live key.

Repeat the first three steps with one automation email.

What to check if the numbers look off

  • Email revenue shows as Direct. Google Analytics is off for that email, or a redirect on your site dropped the tags; see UTM parameters lost after redirect.
  • One email is split across rows. The campaign name changed, or differs in case.
  • Revenue shows as Unattributed. The visitor ID is not reaching checkout, or the visit is older than the window.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which ActiveCampaign email brought a paying customer?

Turn on Google Analytics on the Campaign Summary page so links carry utm_medium=email and a utm_campaign, add the Virling script to your site, and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Each payment is credited to a visit, and the Campaign tab shows revenue per campaign.

Which channel does an ActiveCampaign click land in?

Email. ActiveCampaign sets utm_medium=email, and Virling sorts a visit with a medium of email, newsletter or e-mail into Email, whatever the referrer or source says. A click with no tags and no referrer is Direct.

Does ActiveCampaign add UTM tags to automation emails?

Yes, when Google Analytics is turned on for that email. ActiveCampaign says the option works on direct and automated campaigns and is enabled per campaign or automation email. Send one test email to check the tags before you rely on them.

How is a trial that converts weeks after an email credited?

If the trial starts with a checkout that charges nothing, Virling credits the later first charge from the visits before the trial started, by default the latest in 90 days that was not Direct. If the user upgrades through checkout, the window counts back from that payment instead.

Do ActiveCampaign opens count as revenue in Virling?

No. Virling credits payments to visits, so an email counts only when someone clicks a link in it and lands on a page that runs the script. An email that was only opened is never credited.

Does Virling connect to my ActiveCampaign account?

No. The setup on this page uses only the tags on your links, the Virling script and your payments. Keep sending with ActiveCampaign.
