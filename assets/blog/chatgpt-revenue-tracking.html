ChatGPT revenue tracking: which ChatGPT visits pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-17, updated 2026-11-28.

To see which ChatGPT visits turn into paying customers, count every visit that arrives from chatgpt.com or carries utm_source=chatgpt.com, pass the visitor ID into checkout, and join each payment to the visit before it. You then see the revenue ChatGPT brings and what each ChatGPT visitor is worth next to search, social and email. A traffic count alone cannot tell you that, because ChatGPT sends few visitors and the question is how many of them pay.

This post covers ChatGPT only. For AI assistants in general, see is AI search traffic worth anything.

How ChatGPT visits show up

A visit from ChatGPT can reach your site in three ways, and each one is classified differently.

  • The chatgpt.com referrer. When someone clicks a link in an answer, the visit usually arrives with chatgpt.com as the referrer. Older links can arrive from chat.openai.com, ChatGPT's earlier address. Virling puts both in the AI channel and shows them as one ChatGPT row. The referrer is a host only, so it never says which answer or question the click came from.
  • The utm_source=chatgpt.com tag. OpenAI's publisher FAQ says ChatGPT adds utm_source=chatgpt.com to referral links so analytics tools can identify the traffic. You do not add it; ChatGPT does. When the referrer is missing, Virling reads this tag and credits the visit to ChatGPT, in the AI channel, exactly as if the referrer had been there. A bare utm_source=chatgpt also counts as AI.
  • Links opened from the ChatGPT apps. In the desktop and phone apps, a link leaves ChatGPT for your browser or an in-app view instead of a chatgpt.com tab, so the visit can arrive with no referrer at all. Then the tag is the only thing that names ChatGPT, and it decides whether the visit lands in the ChatGPT row.
  • No referrer and no tag. If the referrer is dropped and the link carries no utm_source, nothing in the visit names ChatGPT, and it counts as Direct. Virling names a few apps from their user agent when the referrer is missing, such as Instagram and LinkedIn, but ChatGPT is not one of them.

So the ChatGPT row is a floor, not a full count. People who read an answer and later type your name into a browser or a search engine arrive as Direct or Organic search; no analytics tool can tie that visit to the answer. Answers in Google AI Mode are different again: their clicks pass google.com and count as Organic search. To test a single link, paste it into the traffic source checker with an empty referrer: a URL carrying utm_source=chatgpt.com lands in AI, and adding utm_medium=cpc moves it to Paid, so a paid campaign tagged chatgpt.com never mixes with answers. Every rule, in order, is listed under channels.

Let ChatGPT read your pages

ChatGPT can only link to pages it can read. OpenAI's crawler documentation describes two bots that matter here. OAI-SearchBot is used to show sites in ChatGPT's search features, and sites that block it are not shown in ChatGPT search answers. ChatGPT-User visits a page when someone asks ChatGPT a question. GPTBot, the training crawler, is separate: you can block it and still allow OAI-SearchBot, since blocking GPTBot only keeps your pages out of model training and does not remove you from ChatGPT search answers.

Paste your robots.txt and a page path into the AI crawler checker to see whether OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot and ChatGPT-User may read that page, and let the robots.txt generator for AI crawlers write separate rules for each. Bot fetches are never counted as visitors in Virling. With the server snippet, each ChatGPT-User fetch shows on the AI card as an answer that used one of your pages; see AI answers.

How Virling credits the payment

With the default model, last non-direct, a buyer who clicks through from ChatGPT and returns days later by typing your URL is still credited to ChatGPT, as long as that first click is inside the 90-day lookback window. A search or newsletter visit in between takes the credit instead; model and lookback window explains first touch and the other choices.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • The ChatGPT row has visitors and no revenue. Look at Matched revenue under the Overview numbers; a large unmatched share means the visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.
  • A buyer says they found you in ChatGPT but the payment is credited elsewhere. They may have read the answer without clicking and searched for you later. That visit has no trace of ChatGPT.
  • ChatGPT shows no visits at all. Check that robots.txt does not block OAI-SearchBot, and look at the Answers tab of the AI card to see whether ChatGPT names you when asked your buyer questions.
  • Direct grew when ChatGPT did. Links opened from the apps without the tag land in Direct. Compare the landing pages of Direct visits with those in the ChatGPT row; the same deep pages showing up in both is a hint, not a count.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which ChatGPT visits turn into paying customers?

Use a tool that groups chatgpt.com visits into their own source and joins each payment to the visit before it. In Virling, pass the visitor ID into checkout, then open the AI channel and the ChatGPT row: it shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and the Payments page shows each buyer's visits.

Does ChatGPT add UTM parameters to links?

Yes, for many links. OpenAI's publisher FAQ says ChatGPT adds utm_source=chatgpt.com to referral links so analytics tools can identify the traffic. Virling reads that tag, so a visit with it counts as ChatGPT even when the referrer is missing.

Why does some ChatGPT traffic show up as Direct?

A visit with no referrer and no tag has nothing that names ChatGPT, so it is Direct in Virling and in most other tools. That happens when a link is opened somewhere the referrer is dropped and carries no utm_source. Treat the ChatGPT number as a floor.

Is chat.openai.com the same as chatgpt.com?

Yes. chat.openai.com is ChatGPT's old address. Virling counts both in the AI channel and shows them as one ChatGPT row.

Does a ChatGPT visit still get credit if the buyer pays a week later?

With the default model, last non-direct, yes. If the buyer comes back by typing your URL, that Direct visit does not take the credit; the payment goes to the ChatGPT visit as long as it is inside the lookback window, 90 days by default.

Do ChatGPT-User and OAI-SearchBot visits count as visitors?

No. They are OpenAI's bots, not people. Virling stores crawler hits apart from visits; with the server snippet, ChatGPT-User fetches show on the AI card as answers that used your pages.

Does a link opened from the ChatGPT app count as ChatGPT?

Yes, when it carries utm_source=chatgpt.com: Virling reads the tag whenever the referrer is missing, as it can be for links opened from the apps. A link with neither the tag nor a referrer counts as Direct.
