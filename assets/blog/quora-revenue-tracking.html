Quora revenue tracking: which answers bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-17.

To see which Quora answers bring paying customers, put a tagged link in each answer with utm_source=quora and a campaign named after that answer, then join each payment to the visit before it. Quora shows you views and upvotes per answer; this shows how many readers clicked through, how many of them paid, and how much.

People arrive on Quora with a question, often while choosing a tool or a vendor, so answers suit SaaS and B2B products that solve a problem someone can ask about. Below: the kinds of Quora links, why answers keep sending readers for years, and how to name tags so every answer is a row with its own revenue.

Answers vs Spaces vs Quora ads

Quora traffic to your site comes from three places, and each needs its own tags:

  • Answers. You answer a question under your own name and link to a guide, a template or a pricing page. Answers carry most of the clicks, because each one stays on a question page that keeps getting readers. One answer, one campaign.
  • Spaces. A Space is a group with its own feed, run by its admins, and posts there can link out like answers do. Put the Space in the campaign, such as space-small-business-finance, so a Space's posts add up to one row.
  • Quora ads. Paid placements bought in Quora's ads manager. Tag them with a paid medium such as cpc so they land in Paid and never mix with organic answers. Quora ads revenue tracking covers the ad side.

Your profile is a smaller fourth source: readers who like an answer often open your profile next, so give its link a fixed campaign such as profile.

Why answers from years ago still send visits

A Quora answer belongs to a question, not to a feed. The question page keeps its answers, search engines can rank it for that question long after you wrote it, and Quora keeps suggesting older answers to new readers. An answer from two years ago can send a reader today who pays this week.

So edit your old answers to add tags, not just new ones: you can edit your own answers at any time, and the new link counts for every click after the edit. And read Quora over months, not days. Under the default model a payment goes to the latest visit that was not Direct in the last 90 days, so a reader who clicked your answer and later typed your address still counts for Quora; for longer sales cycles, choose 365 days in Site settings, as attribution window explains.

Where Quora visits land

quora.com is not one of Virling's known social or community hosts, so a browser click that keeps its referrer lands in Referral, listed on the Source tab under quora.com or a subdomain such as a Space or a language site. Clicks from the Quora mobile app can arrive with no referrer: untagged they count as Direct, while with utm_source=quora they land in Referral under your campaign. How each visit gets its channel and how the payment is joined to it are in how Virling works.

Step 1: add the script

Put the Virling script in the <head> of every page your answers link to, so each visit is recorded with its tags and a visitor ID. The quickstart shows the exact tag and where it goes.

Step 2: name a tag per answer

The campaign is what turns Quora from one row into one row per answer, so name it after the question, and keep utm_content for the spot inside Quora:

https://yourdomain.com/guide/invoice-templates?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=answer-best-invoicing-tool&utm_content=answer
https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=space-small-business-finance&utm_content=space-post
https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=profile
  • One campaign per answer, lowercase with hyphens, such as answer-best-invoicing-tool. If you answer two similar questions, give each its own name, so you can see which wording sells.
  • A prefix per kind: answer-, space- and profile. Rows then sort together in the Campaign tab, and an answer is easy to tell from a Space post.
  • utm_medium=referral for everything organic. Only a paid or email medium overrides the channel, so keep cpc for ads.
  • A list of answers and their names, with each question's URL, so you can find an answer again when its row starts selling. The UTM naming template works for this, and the UTM builder makes the links.

A long tagged URL can look like spam in an answer, and a tracked link is shorter. On the dashboard, open the Campaign tab of the Channels card and click + Tracked link. Under Where will you share it? pick Other and type quora.com, set Page to the page the answer points to, and put the question in Name, which becomes the row's label. Copy the short URL when it shows as Link ready; quora.com is not a known source, so its visits land in Referral. See tracked links.

Step 3: connect payments

Connect the provider that takes your payments, such as Stripe or Paddle, and pass the visitor ID into checkout so each payment is joined to the Quora visit before it. The quickstart covers both, and every provider is on integrations.

Judge an answer by customers, not views

Quora's stats reward answers on broad questions, because those get the most views. For a product, a narrow question with few views can bring exactly the readers who pay. To compare answers by money:

  1. Pick a range of several months on the dashboard, so old answers have time to show.
  2. Open the Campaign tab on the Channels card. Each answer is a row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and a chip against your site average.
  3. Click an answer's row. New customers at the top now counts the paying customers that answer brought in the range, and the Pages card shows what they read.
  4. On the Source tab, open Referral to find untagged Quora clicks by host; those are answers still to tag.

Then spend your time where the money is: update the answers that sell, link to them from new answers on related questions, and answer more questions like them. Readers who found you on Quora often return through search before paying, so also compare the Quora rows under first touch in Site settings to see how many buyers it introduced; first-touch or last-touch attribution explains both. If you answer elsewhere too, see what Reddit posts and comments bring and tracking LinkedIn posts and profile links.

When the Quora numbers look wrong

  • Direct is large and Quora small. Your most-read answers or your profile link are still untagged, and app clicks on them carry no referrer.
  • An answer's row stops growing. Open the question and check the answer is still visible and still has its link; Quora can collapse answers, and an edit can drop the link.
  • Quora visits but no payments. The visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See unattributed revenue.
  • A buyer says Quora, the payment says Organic search. They read your answer without clicking and searched your name later. A read that never reached your site cannot be counted.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track conversions from Quora?

Add a link tagged utm_source=quora, with a utm_campaign named after the answer, to each answer, Space post and your profile. With the script on your site and your payment provider connected, the Campaign tab lists each answer with its visitors and revenue.

Which channel do Quora visits land in?

In Virling, quora.com is not a known social or community host, so a click that keeps its referrer lands in Referral under its own host, and a tagged click with no referrer lands in Referral too. A link tagged with a paid medium such as cpc counts as Paid.

Why do some Quora clicks show up as Direct?

The Quora mobile app can open links without a referrer, and readers often come back later by typing your address. An untagged click with no referrer is Direct; a utm_source on the link in your answer keeps it credited to Quora.

Can I see which Quora answer sent a paying customer?

Only with tags. The referrer says quora.com, never which answer, so name a utm_campaign or a tracked link after each answer and read the Campaign tab.

Do answer views tell me if Quora works?

No. Views count people who saw the answer, most of whom never click. What matters is how many clicked through, how many of them paid, and how much they paid. Compare revenue per visitor for each answer with your site average.

Can I edit an old answer to add UTM tags?

Yes. You can edit your own answers and Space posts at any time, and the new link applies to every click after the edit. Clicks before the edit stay as they were recorded.

Does a Quora answer found through Google count as Quora?

Yes, if the reader then clicks your link on the Quora page. That click comes from quora.com, so it counts as Quora even though Google sent the reader to the answer. A Google result that links straight to your site counts as Organic search.
