Quora revenue tracking: which answers bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-17.

To see which Quora answers bring paying customers, tag the link in every answer, Space post and your profile with utm_source=quora and a campaign for that answer. Pass the visitor ID into your checkout, then join each payment to the visit that came before it. You then see the revenue and revenue per visitor that Quora, and each answer on it, brings in. The views and upvotes Quora shows you do not tell you this.

This guide is for SaaS founders and online stores that answer questions on Quora or run a Space, and want to know which of that work pays.

Where Quora clicks come from

Organic Quora traffic comes through a few kinds of links, and each one should be tagged:

  • Answers. A link inside an answer, usually to a guide, a product page or a pricing page. This is where most clicks come from, and each answer deserves its own campaign.
  • Space posts. A Space you run, or one you post in, holds posts that can link out like answers do. Tag them the same way, with the Space's name in the tag.
  • Your profile. Your credentials and bio can carry a link. People who like an answer often open the profile next, so that link deserves a fixed campaign such as profile.

Why old answers keep sending visits

A Quora answer does not expire like a social post. It stays on the question page, it can show up in search engines for that question, and Quora keeps suggesting older answers to new readers. So an answer you wrote months ago can still send visitors today, and some of them become customers long after you stopped thinking about it.

Two things follow. First, tag your old answers, not only new ones: you can edit your own answers at any time, and the tagged link applies to every click after the edit. Second, judge Quora over months, not days. With the default model, last non-direct, a payment goes to the latest visit that was not Direct inside the lookback window, 90 days by default, so a reader who clicked your answer and later typed your address is still credited to Quora. If your buyers take longer, choose 365 days in Site settings; attribution window explains how to pick one, and the Time to pay card shows the median days from first visit to first payment per channel.

Which channel Quora visits land in

There are three ways a visit gets marked as Quora, and only one of them is under your control.

  • The referrer. A click in a web browser usually arrives with quora.com, or a subdomain such as a Space or a language site, as the referrer. quora.com is not on Virling's list of social or community hosts, so these visits land in Referral, under their own host in the Source tab. The referrer is a host only, so it cannot tell you which answer the click came from.
  • The in-app browser. Links opened in the Quora mobile app can arrive with no referrer, and Quora is not on the list of apps Virling credits from the user agent. With no referrer and no tag, the click counts as Direct.
  • A UTM tag. With no referrer, Virling reads utm_source. quora is not a known source, so the visit lands in Referral, which is fine: the tag travels in the link itself, and your campaign still puts it under the right answer.

The full rule order is in channels.

Step 1: add the script

Add the Virling script to the <head> of every page, including the pages your answers link to. It records each visit with its referrer and UTM tags, and sets the first-party visitor ID that payments need later. The app's setup screen shows the exact tag for your site, and add the script covers where it goes, with guides for common frameworks and site builders. On WooCommerce, the Virling WordPress plugin adds it for you.

Step 2: tag every Quora link

Give each answer its own campaign, and use utm_content for where the link sits:

https://yourdomain.com/guide/invoice-templates?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=answer-best-invoicing-tool&utm_content=answer
https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=space-small-business-finance&utm_content=space-post
https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=profile
  • utm_source=quora on every Quora link, so they group under one source and stay out of Direct.
  • utm_medium=referral for organic answers and posts. Any medium except a paid or email one leaves the channel to the referrer or source. Use a paid medium such as cpc only for Quora ads, so ad clicks land in the Paid channel.
  • utm_campaign names the answer: one value per answer, lowercase, with hyphens, such as a short form of the question. For Space posts, name the Space. The Campaign tab groups visitors and revenue by this value, so this is the tag that gives you revenue per answer.
  • utm_content names the spot: answer, Space post or bio. Virling stores it with each visit; keep what you want to compare in the campaign so it shows up in reports.

The UTM builder makes each link for you, and the UTM naming template keeps the values the same across answers and your other channels. More rules are in which UTM parameters to use. If you share an answer's page on LinkedIn or Reddit too, the social link tagger makes those links; for Quora, set the source to quora by hand.

If long tagged URLs look out of place in an answer, use tracked links instead. In Virling, open the Campaign tab on the Channels card, click + Tracked link, pick Other, type quora.com as the source and pick the page it should open. The short link adds the tags on the click, and the Campaign tab lists it with its visitors and revenue. Make one link per answer. See tracked links.

Step 3: connect your payment provider

Tags only get you visits. Revenue needs the payments and the visitor ID on each one. In the app, open your site, then Setup, then Connect payments, paste the key your provider needs and click Connect and backfill; see payments.

  • SaaS. Virling reads Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments, Creem and RevenueCat. If you use Stripe Payment Links, Polar checkout links or Dodo Payments links, the script adds the visitor ID on click. For a checkout you create on your server, pass the virling_vid cookie, for example as client_reference_id on a Stripe Checkout Session. See link checkout to visits.
  • Shopify. Create a custom app with only the read_orders scope and paste its token, then add the short theme snippet that stores the visitor ID on the cart. See Shopify.
  • WooCommerce. Create a read-only REST API key and paste it, then add the short snippet that stores the virling_vid cookie on each new order. See WooCommerce.

Every provider is listed on integrations. Click a tagged link from one of your answers, make a test payment, then check that it appears on the Payments page with Quora as its source.

Judge answers by customers, not views

Quora shows you views and upvotes for each answer. An answer with many views can send few clicks, and an answer on a narrow question with few views can send exactly the readers who buy. Compare answers by the money they bring:

  1. Open the dashboard and pick a range of a few months, since old answers keep sending visitors and buyers often wait before they pay.
  2. On the Channels card, switch to the Campaign tab. With one campaign per answer, each answer is a row with its own visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and a chip that compares it with your site average.
  3. Click an answer's row to filter the whole page. The New customers number at the top is now the paying customers that answer brought in the range, and the Pages card shows where its readers landed.
  4. Switch to the Source tab and open Referral to see untagged Quora clicks by host, such as answers you have not tagged yet. Add any Quora subdomain rows when you compare.
  5. Open a payment from the Payments page to see the buyer's journey: every visit before the payment, with the credited one marked.

Answers that bring paying customers are the ones to update, link to from new answers and write more like. Quora often introduces a buyer who comes back later through search; switch the model to first touch in Site settings and compare the Quora rows under both. History is recomputed, and you can switch back. First-touch or last-touch attribution explains the difference. If you answer on Reddit as well, see what Reddit posts and comments bring, and for B2B posts, tracking LinkedIn posts and profile links.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • Quora looks small but Direct is large. Some links are untagged, and app clicks on them arrive with no referrer. Check your most-viewed answers and your profile link first.
  • Visits but no revenue at all. Check the Matched revenue line under the Overview numbers. If a large share of revenue is unmatched, the visitor ID is not reaching the payment. See Unattributed revenue.
  • A customer says they found you on Quora but the payment is credited elsewhere. They may have read an answer without clicking and searched for you later. No analytics tool can count a read that never reached your site.
  • Visitors who block the script are not counted at all, so Quora's real visitor number can be higher than what any analytics tool shows. Revenue still counts in your total, as Unattributed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track conversions from Quora?

Tag the link in each answer, Space post and your profile with utm_source=quora and a utm_campaign for that answer. Add the tracking script to your site, pass the visitor ID into checkout and connect your payment provider. The Campaign tab then shows visitors and revenue per answer.

Which channel do Quora visits land in?

In Virling, quora.com is not a known social or community host, so a click that keeps its referrer lands in Referral under its own host, and a tagged click with no referrer lands in Referral too. A link tagged with a paid medium such as cpc counts as Paid.

Why do some Quora clicks show up as Direct?

Links opened in the Quora mobile app can arrive with no referrer, and buyers often come back later by typing your address. With no referrer and no tag, the visit is Direct. A utm_source on the link in your answer keeps the click credited to Quora.

Can I see which Quora answer sent a paying customer?

Not from the referrer, which only carries a host such as quora.com. Give each answer its own utm_campaign, or its own tracked link, and the Campaign tab shows visitors and revenue per answer.

Do answer views tell me if Quora works?

No. Views count people who saw the answer, most of whom never click. What matters is how many clicked through, how many of them paid, and how much they paid. Compare revenue per visitor for each answer with your site average.

Can I edit an old answer to add UTM tags?

Yes. You can edit your own answers and Space posts at any time, and the new link applies to every click after the edit. Clicks before the edit stay as they were recorded.
