Pinterest revenue tracking: which pins bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-17.

To see which pins bring paying customers, tag the link on each pin and on your profile with utm_source=pinterest and a campaign named after the pin or board, store the visitor ID on each order, and let Virling join each order to the visit before it. Pinterest Analytics counts impressions, saves and outbound clicks; this counts orders and revenue per pin.

People use Pinterest to plan: they save a product to a board and buy days or weeks later, often on a different visit. That makes it a natural channel for stores on Shopify or WooCommerce, and one whose orders easily end up looking Direct. Below: the kinds of pins, the gap between save and order, why your store calls those orders Direct, and where promoted pins fit.

Pins, idea pins, product pins and rich pins

  • Standard pins. An image or video that links to one page, usually a product, a collection or a blog post. Most clicks come from these, and each deserves its own campaign. Boards have no link of their own; each pin on a board keeps its own.
  • Idea pins. Pinterest has folded idea pins into standard pins, and older ones may have no link at all. A pin with no link can only send people to your profile, so tag the profile website with a fixed campaign such as profile.
  • Product pins. Pins that show price and stock, which Pinterest makes from a catalog you upload. A catalog pin takes its link from the link column of your data source, so that is where the tags go, for example with the product handle as the campaign.
  • Rich pins. Pins that pull the title, description and, for products, the price from the meta tags on your page. They change what the pin shows, not where it links, so tag the link as you would on any pin.

The gap between save and order

A pin is often saved long before anyone buys. The shopper adds your product to a board, comes back to it when the occasion or the payday arrives, and then clicks, or types your address. How long that takes differs by store and price, so measure it rather than guess: once orders are linked to visits, the Time to pay card shows the median days from first visit to first payment per channel.

A saved pin keeps its link, tags included, so a click from a board weeks later is still credited to the pin. If the shopper returns by typing your address instead, the default model, last non-direct, still gives the order to the tagged Pinterest visit as long as it falls inside the lookback window, 90 days by default. If Pinterest's Time to pay comes near that, choose 365 days in Site settings; attribution window explains the trade-off.

Why Shopify and WooCommerce orders show as Direct

Much Pinterest browsing happens in the mobile app, and links opened there often arrive with no referrer. Virling credits some in-app browsers from the user agent, but not Pinterest's, and your store sees the same empty referrer, so a pin that sells well can show up in Shopify or WooCommerce reports as Direct orders. A utm_source=pinterest tag travels in the link and survives the app, and pinterest is a known Social source, so tagged clicks land in Social.

Browser clicks from pinterest.com, its country subdomains such as uk.pinterest.com, and pin.it short links are Social as well, but country domains such as pinterest.co.uk or pinterest.de are not on the list and land in Referral. Why Shopify orders show up as Direct covers the other causes, and how Virling works shows how an order is joined to its visit.

Pinterest ads and promoted pins

A promoted pin is an ad, and its clicks belong in Paid, not Social. Tag its link with utm_medium=cpc and a campaign that matches the ad campaign, so organic and paid pins never share a row. Pinterest also reports conversions from its own tag, counted by its own rules, so expect its figures to differ from orders joined to visits. Pinterest Ads revenue tracking covers the setup.

Step 1: add the script

Add the Virling script to every page of your store: on Shopify it goes in your theme's theme.liquid, and on WooCommerce the Virling WordPress plugin adds it. The quickstart has the exact tag.

Step 2: tag every pin link

Use a campaign per pin and put the board it was first pinned to in utm_content:

https://yourstore.com/products/linen-apron?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=pin-linen-apron-kitchen&utm_content=board-kitchen-ideas
https://yourstore.com/collections/summer?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=pin-summer-collection&utm_content=board-summer-outfits
https://yourstore.com/?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=profile
  • utm_source=pinterest on every pin, so they group under one source and app clicks stay out of Direct.
  • utm_medium=social for organic pins, and cpc only for promoted ones.
  • utm_campaign per pin, lowercase with hyphens. If you pin one product many times, name the product instead and compare products.
  • utm_content for the board, stored with each visit. See utm_content vs utm_term.

With dozens of pins, the UTM naming template keeps names consistent and the UTM builder makes the URLs. For short links instead, open the Campaign tab of the Channels card, click + Tracked link, choose Other under Where will you share it? and type pinterest.com, set Page to the product, and give it a Name such as the pin. pinterest.com is a known social source, so the link is tagged social and its visits land in Social; copy the short URL when it shows as Link ready. See revenue per pin

  1. Pick a range of a few weeks or more, so saved pins have time to turn into orders.
  2. In the Channels card, open Social and find pinterest.com, with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor. Add its country subdomain rows, and any Pinterest country domains under Referral, when you compare.
  3. Switch to the Campaign tab: with one campaign per pin, each pin is a row of its own.
  4. Filter by the pinterest.com row and read the Pages card to see which products and collections pinners land on.
  5. Open an order on the Payments page to see the visits before it, with the credited one marked, and how long the shopper took.

Pins often introduce a shopper who later buys through search or an email. Compare the Pinterest rows under first touch in Site settings to see how many orders it started; first-touch or last-touch attribution explains the two models. For other visual channels, see sales from Instagram bio and story links and orders from Snapchat Stories.

When the Pinterest numbers look wrong

  • Pinterest small, Direct large. Some pins, often older ones, or the profile link are untagged, and app clicks on them have no referrer.
  • A Pinterest domain in Referral. It is a country domain such as pinterest.de. A referrer wins over a tag, so the visit stays in Referral, but its campaign still puts it under the right pin.
  • Catalog pins missing from the Campaign tab. The link column in your data source has no tags. Add them and wait for Pinterest's next catalog update.
  • Pinterest visits but no matched orders. The theme snippet that stores the visitor ID on the order is missing. See unattributed revenue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track revenue from Pinterest?

Put utm_source=pinterest and a utm_campaign for the pin or board on every pin link and on your profile, and store the visitor ID on each order. Orders are then joined to the pin visits before them, and the Campaign tab shows revenue per pin.

Why do Pinterest orders show up as Direct in Shopify or WooCommerce?

The Pinterest app often opens links with no referrer, and shoppers who saved a pin often come back by typing your address. Without a tag, nothing marks the visit as Pinterest. A utm_source on the pin link keeps the click credited to Pinterest.

Can I see which pin sent a paying customer?

Yes, with one utm_campaign or tracked link per pin. The referrer alone says pinterest.com and nothing about the pin.

How long do Pinterest buyers take to purchase?

It varies by store and product. People often save a pin to a board and come back to it later, so measure it on your own orders: the Time to pay card in Virling shows the median days from first visit to first payment per channel. Make sure your lookback window is longer than that.

Do saved pins keep their UTM tags?

Yes. A saved pin keeps the link it was created with, so a click from a board weeks later still carries your tags. The same goes for copies of your pin that other people save to their own boards.

How do I tag product pins from a catalog?

Product pins made from a catalog take their link from your data source, so add utm_source=pinterest and a campaign to the link column there. Rich pins only change what the pin shows, so tag their link like any other pin.
