Meta AI traffic: do Meta AI visits turn into paying customers?

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-21.

To see whether visitors from Meta AI become paying customers, find the visits Meta AI sends, pass the visitor ID into checkout, and join each payment to the visit before it. Meta AI is the assistant inside WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and the meta.ai site. A visit count cannot tell you whether it sends customers; revenue per visitor next to search, social and email can. Two things to know up front: Virling does not list Meta AI as an AI assistant yet, so a click from meta.ai shows in the Referral channel as meta.ai, and a click inside the Meta apps usually lands in Social, credited to the app, or in Direct.

This post covers Meta AI only. For an assistant Virling counts under AI, see ChatGPT revenue tracking; for AI assistants in general, see is AI search traffic worth anything.

How Meta AI visits show up

Every session gets one channel from its first pageview. The AI channel covers a fixed list of assistant hosts: chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com. meta.ai is not on it. When a Meta AI answer links to your site and someone taps it, the visit can reach you in four ways.

  • The meta.ai referrer. A click in the Meta AI web chat can arrive with meta.ai as the referrer. Any referrer host that is not a known AI, search, community or social host is Referral, so the visit lands there and shows as meta.ai in the Referrer breakdown and in the Referral channel's sources, with the revenue credited to it. The referrer is a host only, so it never says which answer the click came from.
  • A Meta app's referrer. If the click carries a facebook.com or instagram.com referrer, including their subdomains, the visit is Social, credited to that host, next to your posts and shared links.
  • An in-app browser with no referrer. Links tapped inside WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger often open in the app's own browser with no referrer. When the user agent names the app, Virling credits the visit to it in Social: whatsapp.com, instagram.com, or facebook.com for Facebook and Messenger. Nothing in that visit says it came from a Meta AI answer rather than a chat or a post.
  • No referrer at all. If the link opens in a plain system browser that does not name the app, the visit counts as Direct, in Virling and in most other tools.

So the meta.ai row is a floor, not a full count, and it is not part of your AI total. Most of what Meta AI sends from inside the apps sits in the Social rows for those apps, mixed with ordinary chat and post traffic; WhatsApp revenue tracking covers how that row is built. People who read an answer and later type your name arrive as Direct or Organic search; no analytics tool can tie that visit to the answer. The full rule order is in channels, and the traffic source checker shows how a given landing URL and referrer are credited.

Tag the links you control

You cannot put the referrer back on a visit that arrived without one, but a utm_source keeps a visit out of Direct and out of the app rows. Wherever you place a link meant for Meta AI users, such as a page you expect to be quoted or a prompt you share, tag it, for example ?utm_source=meta.ai&utm_campaign=meta-ai.

  • The source. With no referrer, Virling reads utm_source. meta.ai is not a known source, so a tagged visit counts as Referral, not AI, and not Direct. Do not use utm_source=facebook or utm_source=instagram: those are known Social sources, and the visits would mix with your posts.
  • The campaign. The Source tab is keyed by referrer host, so a tagged visit with no referrer does not join the meta.ai row. Add utm_campaign=meta-ai and those visits, with their revenue, show by name in the Campaign tab of the Channels card. The UTM builder makes the links.
  • A referrer wins. When the referrer is present it decides the channel, and the tag only names the campaign. A paid medium wins over both: utm_medium=cpc counts as Paid.

A "where did you hear about us" field at signup or checkout catches people who read a Meta AI answer and came back later. Keep it next to the dashboard number rather than mixing the two.

Set up Meta AI revenue tracking in Virling

  1. Add the tracking script to every page. See the tracking script. Nothing Meta-specific is needed: the referrer, the user agent and the tags are read on every visit.
  2. Connect your payment provider: Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments, Creem, RevenueCat, Shopify or WooCommerce.
  3. Pass the visitor ID into checkout. Stripe Payment Links, Polar checkout links and Dodo Payments links get it from the script on click. For a checkout you create on your server, pass the virling_vid cookie, for example as client_reference_id on a Stripe Checkout Session. See link checkout to visits. Without this step meta.ai shows visitors and no revenue.
  4. Open the dashboard and pick a range of a few weeks or more. Short ranges with few visitors are noisy.
  5. In the Channels card, click Referral, or switch to the Source tab, and find meta.ai. The row shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, with a chip that compares it with your site average. If you tag links, check the Campaign tab for your Meta AI campaign too.
  6. Click the meta.ai row to filter the whole page. The Pages card now shows which landing pages Meta AI sends people to, and which of them lead to payments.
  7. Open a payment from the Payments page to see the buyer's journey: every visit before the payment, with the credited one marked.

How Virling credits the payment

People often tap a link in a Meta AI answer, leave, and come back days later by typing your URL. With the default model, last non-direct, that later Direct visit does not take the credit: the payment goes to the Meta AI visit, as long as it started inside the lookback window, 90 days by default. If the buyer subscribes, every renewal keeps counting toward the same source. If they come back through a Google search or a newsletter before paying, that later visit takes the credit instead.

To see how often Meta AI starts journeys that another channel finishes, switch the model to first touch in Site settings. First-touch or last-touch attribution explains the difference. Changing the model recalculates every past payment, so you can switch back.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • Meta AI is missing from the AI channel. That is expected today. Look in Referral, or in the Referrer breakdown, for meta.ai.
  • meta.ai has few visits but the Meta app rows have many. Taps inside WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger are credited to the app, not to Meta AI. Tag the links you control so they show by campaign.
  • meta.ai has visitors but no revenue. Check the Matched revenue line under the Overview numbers. If a large share of revenue is unmatched, the visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.
  • A buyer says they found you in Meta AI but the payment is credited elsewhere. They may have read the answer without tapping, or opened the link in a browser that dropped the referrer and did not name the app. That visit has no trace of Meta AI.
  • Visitors who block the script are not counted, so Meta AI's real visitor number can be higher than any analytics tool shows. Their revenue still counts in your total, as Unattributed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see whether Meta AI visitors become paying customers?

Join each payment to the visit before it. In Virling, pass the visitor ID into checkout, then find meta.ai in the Referral channel or the Referrer breakdown, and check the Social rows for the Meta apps. Each row shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and the Payments page shows each buyer's visits with the credited one marked.

Does Virling count Meta AI in the AI channel?

Not yet. The AI channel covers chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com. A visit with a meta.ai referrer counts in Referral and shows as meta.ai. Add it to the AI channel yourself when you compare assistants.

Why does Meta AI traffic from WhatsApp or Instagram show up as Social or Direct?

Inside the apps, a link often opens in an in-app browser with no referrer. When the user agent names Instagram, Facebook, Messenger or WhatsApp, Virling credits the visit to that app in Social. When nothing names the app, the visit is Direct. Neither says the link came from a Meta AI answer rather than a chat or a post.

What UTM tags should I use for links meant for Meta AI users?

Use a source Virling does not already know, such as utm_source=meta.ai, plus utm_campaign=meta-ai. Avoid utm_source=facebook or instagram, which count as Social and mix the visits with your posts. The campaign then shows in the Campaign tab with its revenue.

Does a Meta AI visit still get credit if the buyer pays a week later?

With the default model, last non-direct, yes. If the buyer comes back by typing your URL, that Direct visit does not take the credit; the payment goes to the Meta AI visit as long as it is inside the lookback window, 90 days by default.
