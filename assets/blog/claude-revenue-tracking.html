Claude revenue tracking: which Claude visits pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-18.

To see which Claude visits turn into paying customers, catch the clicks that name Claude, join each payment to the visit before it, and know which Claude clicks arrive with no name at all. Clicks made on claude.ai in a browser carry a claude.ai referrer. Clicks from the Claude desktop and mobile apps usually carry nothing. Anthropic does not document a utm_source tag on Claude's links the way OpenAI does for ChatGPT, so the Claude row in any analytics tool is real but partial.

The steps every AI source shares, from counting visits to comparing revenue per visitor, are in is AI search traffic worth anything, and ChatGPT has its own post. This one covers only what is true of Claude. To ask Claude about your Virling numbers instead, see ask Claude which channel pays.

What claude.ai and the Claude apps send

  • claude.ai in a browser. A click on a link in an answer opens your page in a new tab with claude.ai as the referrer. Virling matches claude.ai and its subdomains, puts the visit in the AI channel and names the source Claude. It stores the host only, so the conversation behind the click stays private.
  • The Claude desktop app. Links open in your default browser, and a link handed from an app to a browser arrives without a referrer. With nothing that says Claude, the visit is Direct, and Virling files it under Direct as Typed or bookmark.
  • The Claude mobile apps. On iOS and Android the referrer is usually missing too. Virling names some in-app browsers from the user agent, such as Instagram and TikTok, but has no rule for the Claude app, so these visits are also Direct.
  • Tagged links. A visit with no referrer whose URL carries utm_source=claude.ai is credited to Claude in the AI channel, as if the referrer had been there; a bare utm_source=claude counts as AI. These tags only appear on links someone tagged.

A cited link, followed through

Someone asks Claude on claude.ai which invoicing tool handles several currencies. Claude searches the web, reads your pricing page and cites it. Here is what each step leaves behind in Virling.

  1. Claude-User requests /pricing to read it. It runs no JavaScript, so the tracking script never sees it. With the server snippet, the fetch counts on the Answers tab of the AI card as one answer that used /pricing.
  2. The person clicks the citation. The visit lands on /pricing with the claude.ai referrer: channel AI, source Claude, landing page /pricing.
  3. They start a trial, leave, and come back four days later by typing your URL. That return visit is Direct, and with the default model, last non-direct, Direct never takes credit from an earlier named source.
  4. They pay. If checkout received the visitor ID, the payment goes to the Claude visit from step 2, which is inside the 90-day default lookback window. The Payments page shows both visits, with the Claude one marked.

Had the same person asked in the desktop app, step 2 would be a Direct visit, and the payment would go to Direct unless some other named source came first. To check a case yourself, paste the landing URL and https://claude.ai/ as the referrer into the traffic source checker, then clear the referrer and watch it turn Direct.

ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User

Anthropic's crawler documentation names three bots, and a robots.txt rule does something different for each.

  • ClaudeBot collects pages for model training. Anthropic says blocking it signals that your future pages should be left out of training data. Virling lists it as a Training crawler, along with the older anthropic-ai user agent.
  • Claude-SearchBot crawls ahead of time to improve Claude's search results. Anthropic says blocking it may reduce your visibility in those results. Virling lists it as a Search crawler on the Visits tab of the AI card.
  • Claude-User fetches a page at the moment someone asks Claude a question. Blocking it stops Claude from reading the page for that person. Virling lists it as an Answer fetch, the one the Used in column counts.

Anthropic publishes the bot names and its IP ranges, at claude.com/crawling/bots.json, but not full user-agent strings. A Claude-User fetch identifies itself with a string such as Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; Claude-User/1.0; +Claude-User@anthropic.com). Virling looks for the bot name anywhere in the user agent, ignoring case, so a new version number still matches. Anthropic says its bots honor robots.txt, including the Crawl-delay extension. The AI crawler checker shows which of the three your robots.txt lets in, and the robots.txt generator for AI crawlers writes rules that block ClaudeBot while allowing the other two.

When the Claude numbers look wrong

  • Buyers say they found you in Claude, but the row is nearly empty. Ask which app they used. Desktop and mobile clicks land in Direct, so an audience that uses Claude mostly in the apps leaves little trace in the Claude row.
  • Used in is high, visitors are low. Claude reads your pages to answer and people get what they need without clicking. The Mentioned count shows whether those answers at least name you.
  • Used in stays at zero. Either the server snippet is not installed, or robots.txt blocks Claude-User. The AI crawler checker tells you which.
  • Claude visitors, no Claude revenue. The visitor ID is probably not reaching checkout. Compare the Matched revenue line under the Overview numbers with your total, and see Unattributed revenue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Claude visits turn into paying customers?

Pass the Virling visitor ID into checkout, then open the AI channel and click the Claude row. It shows the visitors that came from claude.ai, the revenue credited to them and revenue per visitor, and the Payments page shows each buyer's visits.

Does the Claude desktop app send a referrer?

No. Links from the desktop app open in your default browser without a referrer, so those visits count as Direct in Virling and in most other tools. Only clicks made on claude.ai in a browser carry the claude.ai referrer.

Does Claude add utm_source to its links?

Anthropic does not document one. If a visit has no referrer and its link carries utm_source=claude.ai, Virling credits it to Claude in the AI channel; a bare utm_source=claude counts as AI.

What is the difference between ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User?

ClaudeBot collects pages for training, Claude-SearchBot crawls ahead of time for Claude's search results, and Claude-User fetches a page live when someone asks Claude a question. None of them is a visitor in Virling; with the server snippet, Claude-User fetches show as answers that used your pages.

Should I block ClaudeBot?

That is a training decision, not a traffic one. Anthropic says blocking ClaudeBot keeps your future pages out of training data. Claude-SearchBot and Claude-User are the bots that affect whether Claude can find and read your pages for an answer.
