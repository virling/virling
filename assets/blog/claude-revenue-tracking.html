Claude revenue tracking: which Claude visits pay

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-18.

To see which Claude visits turn into paying customers, count every visit that arrives from claude.ai or carries a Claude utm_source, pass the visitor ID into checkout, and join each payment to the visit before it. Claude revenue tracking then shows the revenue Claude referral traffic brings and what each Claude visitor is worth next to ChatGPT, Perplexity, search and email. A visit count alone cannot tell you that, because Claude usually sends few visitors and the question is how many of them pay.

This post covers Claude visits only. For ChatGPT, see ChatGPT revenue tracking; for AI assistants in general, see is AI search traffic worth anything. If you want to ask Claude about your own Virling numbers instead, that is a different setup: see ask Claude which channel pays.

How Claude visits show up

When Claude searches the web or reads a page to answer someone, it can link to the pages it used. When someone clicks one, the visit can reach your site in three ways, and each one is classified differently.

  • The claude.ai referrer. A click on a link in Claude in the browser usually arrives with claude.ai as the referrer. Virling puts any claude.ai host in the AI channel and lists it as one Claude row. The referrer is a host only, so it never says which answer or question the click came from.
  • A utm_source tag. When the referrer is missing, Virling reads utm_source. A visit tagged utm_source=claude.ai is credited to Claude, in the AI channel, exactly as if the referrer had been there. A bare utm_source=claude also counts as AI. You only see these tags on links that carry them; Virling does not add them.
  • No referrer and no tag. Links opened from the Claude desktop or mobile apps, or anywhere else the referrer is dropped, can arrive with neither. Nothing in the visit names Claude, so it counts as Direct, in Virling and in most other tools. Virling recognizes some apps' in-app browsers by their user agent, such as Instagram and TikTok, but Claude is not one of them.

So the Claude row is a floor, not a full count. People who read an answer and later type your name into a browser or a search engine arrive as Direct or Organic search; no analytics tool can tie that visit to the answer. The full rule order is in channels, and the traffic source checker shows how a given landing URL and referrer are credited: paste a URL with utm_source=claude.ai and an empty referrer to see it land in AI.

One exception to the order: a paid medium wins over everything. A link with utm_medium=cpc counts as Paid even if its source is claude.ai, so ads you run stay apart from answers.

Let Claude read your pages

Claude can only link to pages it can read. Anthropic uses three bots. ClaudeBot collects pages to train models. Claude-SearchBot crawls pages for Claude's web search. Claude-User fetches a page live when someone asks Claude a question. If robots.txt blocks Claude-SearchBot or Claude-User, your pages are less likely to show up in Claude's answers; blocking ClaudeBot only keeps them out of training.

The AI crawler checker reads your robots.txt and tells you which of these bots may read a page. Bot fetches are never counted as visitors in Virling. With the server snippet, each Claude-User fetch shows on the AI card as an answer that used one of your pages; see AI answers.

Set up Claude revenue tracking in Virling

  1. Add the tracking script to every page. See the tracking script. Nothing Claude-specific is needed: the referrer and the tag are read on every visit.
  2. Connect your payment provider: Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments, Creem, RevenueCat, Shopify or WooCommerce.
  3. Pass the visitor ID into checkout. Stripe Payment Links, Polar checkout links and Dodo Payments links get it from the script on click. For a checkout you create on your server, pass the virling_vid cookie, for example as client_reference_id on a Stripe Checkout Session. See link checkout to visits. Without this step Claude shows visitors and no revenue.
  4. Open the dashboard and pick a range of a few weeks or more. Claude usually sends fewer visitors than search, so short ranges are noisy.
  5. In the Channels card, click AI, or switch to the Source tab, and find Claude. The row shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, with a chip that compares it with your site average.
  6. Click the row to filter the whole page. The Pages card now shows which landing pages Claude sends people to, and which of them lead to payments.
  7. Open a payment from the Payments page to see the buyer's journey: every visit before the payment, with the credited one marked.

Compare Claude with other AI assistants

Virling keeps each assistant in its own row inside the AI channel, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot. Open the AI channel and read the rows side by side. Visitors tell you which assistant sends the most people; revenue and revenue per visitor tell you which of those people pay. An assistant that sends a handful of visitors can still be worth more per visitor than one that sends many.

With small numbers, a single payment can swing revenue per visitor a lot. Before deciding that one assistant pays better than another, widen the range until each row rests on more than a few payments. The AI channel total is the sum of every assistant, and it sits next to search, social and email in the Channels card.

How Virling credits the payment

People often click a link in Claude, leave, and come back days later by typing your URL. With the default model, last non-direct, that later Direct visit does not take the credit: the payment goes to the Claude visit, as long as it started inside the lookback window, 90 days by default. If the buyer subscribes, every renewal keeps counting toward Claude. If they come back through a Google search or a newsletter before paying, that later visit takes the credit instead.

To see how often Claude starts journeys that another channel finishes, switch the model to first touch in Site settings. First-touch or last-touch attribution explains the difference. Changing the model recalculates every past payment, so you can switch back.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • Claude has visitors but no revenue. Check the Matched revenue line under the Overview numbers. If a large share of revenue is unmatched, the visitor ID is not reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.
  • A buyer says they found you in Claude but the payment is credited elsewhere. They may have read the answer without clicking, or opened the link from an app that dropped the referrer. That visit has no trace of Claude.
  • Claude shows no visits at all. Check that robots.txt does not block Claude-SearchBot or Claude-User, and look at the Answers tab of the AI card to see whether Claude names you when asked your buyer questions.
  • Visitors who block the script are not counted, so Claude's real visitor number can be higher than any analytics tool shows. Their revenue still counts in your total, as Unattributed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Claude visits turn into paying customers?

Use a tool that groups claude.ai visits into their own source and joins each payment to the visit before it. In Virling, pass the visitor ID into checkout, then open the AI channel and the Claude row: it shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, and the Payments page shows each buyer's visits.

How do I track traffic from Claude?

Look for claude.ai in your referrers. Virling puts every visit with a claude.ai referrer in the AI channel and lists it as Claude. A visit with no referrer but utm_source=claude.ai or utm_source=claude also counts as AI.

Why does some Claude traffic show up as Direct?

A visit with no referrer and no utm_source has nothing that names Claude, so it is Direct in Virling and in most other tools. That can happen when a link is opened from an app that drops the referrer. Treat the Claude number as a floor.

Is Claude traffic worth more than ChatGPT or Perplexity traffic?

It depends on your site, and the only way to know is to measure it. Virling lists each assistant, such as Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot, as a separate row in the AI channel, each with its own visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, so you can compare them on your own payments.

Does a Claude visit still get credit if the buyer pays a week later?

With the default model, last non-direct, yes. If the buyer comes back by typing your URL, that Direct visit does not take the credit; the payment goes to the Claude visit as long as it is inside the lookback window, 90 days by default.

Do ClaudeBot and Claude-User count as visitors?

No. They are Anthropic's bots, not people. Virling stores crawler hits apart from visits; with the server snippet, Claude-User fetches show on the AI card as answers that used your pages.
