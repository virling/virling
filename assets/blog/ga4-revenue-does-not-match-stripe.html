Why GA4 revenue doesn't match Stripe or Shopify

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-01, updated 2026-11-25.

TL;DR: GA4 counts purchase events that a browser sends. Stripe and Shopify count money that actually moved. Anything that stops the event from being sent, or changes the amount after it was sent, opens a gap. Trust your payment provider for revenue, and use analytics only to say where each payment came from.

The eight causes, and how to check each

The order to check them in

Start with what you can settle by looking, then compare orders, and leave the two causes that need a test or cannot be listed for last.

  1. Pick the period. Quickest, one look at a calendar. Use a full month that ended at least a few days ago, because GA4 is still processing recent days.
  2. Time zones. Quick, two settings pages. Compare the zone of the GA4 property with the zone of the provider's report.
  3. Tax, shipping and discounts. Quick, one order. Open a taxed or discounted order in both and see which amount was sent as value.
  4. Subscription renewals. Short, one filter and one sum. Add up the provider's invoices with billing reason subscription_cycle, or what your provider calls renewals.
  5. Refunds and disputes. Short, two totals. Compare the provider's refunds and disputes with the refund amount in GA4.
  6. Currency. Short, one report. Look for purchases in GA4 with no currency, then compare one order in another currency.
  7. Duplicate purchase events. Longer, an export from each side. List GA4 purchases by transaction ID and look for (not set) and for IDs your provider does not have.
  8. The purchase event never fires. Longer, a test purchase. Pay, close the tab before the redirect, and see whether the order reaches GA4.
  9. Ad blockers and declined consent. Slowest, because the orders cannot be listed. What is still missing after the other steps is mostly this.

When the total matches but the channel is wrong

A second problem hides inside the first. When a buyer leaves for checkout.stripe.com or a PayPal page and comes back, GA4 can start a new session with the payment page as the referrer. The sale then belongs to Stripe or PayPal instead of the ad or post that brought the buyer. Add those domains to GA4's list of unwanted referrals in the data stream's tag settings.

Long buying cycles cause the same drift: a buyer who first came from a post and paid two weeks later from a bookmark shows as Direct under a last-click view. See first-touch or last-touch attribution. If the sales sit under Unassigned or (not set), see GA4 unassigned traffic and (not set).

How to send Stripe purchases to GA4

  1. When you create the Checkout Session, put the visitor's GA4 client ID in client_reference_id or metadata.
  2. Listen for the checkout.session.completed webhook on your server.
  3. Send a purchase event to GA4 with the Measurement Protocol, using that client ID, the session ID as transaction_id, the amount as value and the currency.
  4. Send a refund event with the same transaction_id when Stripe reports a refund.
  5. Add checkout.stripe.com to GA4's unwanted referrals so the return visit keeps its source.

That fixes the missing and duplicate purchases. It does not bring in renewals unless you also send them from invoice.paid.

Which number to trust

Use your payment provider for revenue, refunds and renewals, because it is the ledger. Use analytics for the question it can answer: which visit came before each payment. The reliable way to join the two is to pass a visitor ID into checkout and match each payment to that visitor's visits, as in tracking Stripe revenue by traffic source.

Frequently asked questions

Why is GA4 revenue lower than Stripe?

Usually because purchase events never fire: buyers who close the tab after paying, blocked or declined tracking, and subscription renewals that happen without a visit. Missing currency parameters also drop revenue.

Why is GA4 revenue higher than Stripe or Shopify?

Duplicate purchase events, often from a reloaded thank-you page, and refunds that GA4 never hears about. Send a unique transaction_id and a refund event.

Does GA4 track Stripe subscription renewals?

Not on its own. A renewal is charged with no visit, so no browser event is sent. You have to send it from your server or use a tool that reads payments from Stripe.

How do I stop checkout.stripe.com showing as a referrer in GA4?

Add checkout.stripe.com, and any other payment domain you use, to the list of unwanted referrals in your GA4 data stream's tag settings.

How do I connect Stripe to GA4?

Pass the GA4 client ID into the Checkout Session, listen for the checkout.session.completed webhook, and send a purchase event with the Measurement Protocol. Add checkout.stripe.com to GA4's unwanted referrals.

Why did GA4 revenue for a recent day change?

GA4 can take 24 to 48 hours to process data, and reports may change during that time, see Google's data freshness page. Reconcile a period that ended at least a few days ago.

Can exchange rates make GA4 and Stripe disagree?

Yes. GA4 converts with the prior day's exchange rate, see Google's currency reference, and your provider converts with its own rates. Virling converts to USD with European Central Bank rates when it reads each payment, so its USD total can differ from your provider's too, see the currencies docs.
