TikTok revenue tracking: which videos bring paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-17.

TikTok sends traffic in its own way: through one website link in your bio, opened mostly inside the app, from posts that are seen for a short burst and then fade. To learn which of those visits turn into paying customers, tag the bio link with utm_source=tiktok, add the tracking script to your site, and pass the visitor ID into checkout. TikTok's own analytics count views, likes and profile visits, but never what a viewer paid after leaving the app.

A visit with no referrer and no tag lands in Direct, and Virling joins each payment to the visit before it through the visitor ID your checkout carries. The rest of this post is about the parts only TikTok has; channels covers the general rules.

The in-app browser hides where the click came from

Most people watch TikTok in the phone app, and tapping your bio link there opens TikTok's in-app browser instead of Safari or Chrome. That browser usually sends no referrer, so a visit from your best video can look identical to someone typing your address.

  • When the in-app browser names itself. TikTok's in-app browser puts markers such as musical_ly or BytedanceWebview in its user agent. Virling reads those and credits the visit to tiktok.com in Social, even with no tag.
  • When the viewer jumps to their own browser. The menu in the in-app browser offers to open the page in the phone's default browser. That browser does not mention TikTok, so an untagged visit from there is Direct.
  • When the link carries a tag. utm_source=tiktok travels inside the URL, so it survives both cases. A tag or a referrer always beats the user agent guess.
  • On desktop. Someone browsing tiktok.com on a laptop usually arrives with tiktok.com as the referrer. That names the site, not the video or the profile.

One bio link carries every video

On YouTube each video has its own description; on TikTok, every post points to the same bio link. So the link cannot tell you which video a viewer just watched. What you can control is where it points and how it is tagged:

  • Straight to your site. The tagged bio link opens your page and the visit counts as TikTok in Social.
  • A link page on your own domain. Tag the bio link that opens it. Clicks from that page to your other pages stay on the same site, so the visit keeps its TikTok source.
  • A link page hosted on another domain. A click from it reaches your site with that host as the referrer, and the referrer wins over utm_source, so the visit counts as Referral under that host. Give each button its own utm_campaign and the Campaign tab still splits revenue by button.

Because one link serves every post, swap its utm_campaign when you start a new series or push a new offer, then compare the periods. Link-in-bio revenue tracking goes through tagging each button on a link page.

Captions, comments and pinned links

A URL typed into a TikTok caption or comment is usually plain text, not a link, so viewers cannot tap it. That is why creators say "link in bio" in the video and pin a comment repeating it. The pinned comment sends people to your profile, not to your site, so the click you can measure still happens on the bio link.

If you want to know which videos pushed people to tap, give the pinned comment a short code or page name that only that video mentions, such as yourdomain.com/spring. A visitor who types it arrives with no referrer and no tag, so it counts as Direct, but the landing page in the Pages card tells you which video they saw.

How fast a TikTok post's visits fall off

A TikTok post is shown in the For You feed for a while, and once the feed stops showing it, the views and the bio taps that came with it usually drop sharply. Visits from a post tend to come in a burst over a few days, not as a steady trickle over months.

  • Read TikTok by day. Pick a range that starts the day before you post and look at the daily chart. A spike in TikTok visits that lines up with a post is the best sign of which video drove it.
  • Wait for the payments. The visits fade fast, but buyers can take longer. A viewer may tap once, leave, and pay a week later.
  • Post several videos close together, then judge the batch. With one bio link, separate posts a few days apart blur into each other. Compare campaign periods instead of single days.

With the default last non-direct model, a later visit where the viewer types your URL does not take the credit from the tagged TikTok visit, so a slow buyer still counts toward TikTok; the attribution model has the details.

Set up TikTok revenue tracking with Virling

  1. Tag the bio link. Use utm_source=tiktok, utm_medium=social, a utm_campaign for what you are promoting right now, and utm_content=bio. The UTM builder makes the link for you.
  2. Or make a tracked link. In Virling, open Links, set the source to tiktok.com and the channel to Social. A short link is easier to fit in the bio field, and Links shows its clicks, visitors and revenue. See tracked links.
  3. Add the script. Put the Virling tag in the <head> of every page, including any link page on your own domain. See add the script.
  4. Carry the visitor ID into checkout. Payment links from Stripe, Polar and Dodo Payments get it from the script on click; a checkout you create on your server reads the virling_vid cookie. Every provider is covered in link checkout to visits.

A tagged bio link looks like this:

https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spring-offer&utm_content=bio

Read the TikTok numbers

  1. Find TikTok in the Social channel or on the Source tab. Its revenue per visitor chip tells you whether a TikTok visitor is worth more or less than your average visitor.
  2. Filter by TikTok and look at the daily chart for the bursts that follow each post.
  3. Open the Campaign tab to compare bio link campaigns, or link page buttons if you tagged them.
  4. Open a TikTok-credited payment on the Payments page to see how many days passed between the tap and the purchase.

What to check when the numbers look wrong

  • Direct jumped the day a video took off, but TikTok barely moved. The bio link has no tag and viewers chose to open it in their own browser. Tag it.
  • TikTok disappeared after you switched to a link page. The page lives on another domain and its referrer now wins. Look for its host under Referral, or move the page to your own domain.
  • Lots of TikTok visitors and no sales. In-app browser viewers often glance at the page and swipe back to the feed. Check which page the bio link opens; a page made for a phone screen with one clear next step tends to hold them better.
  • TikTok visits but no revenue at all. The visitor ID may not be reaching checkout. See Unattributed revenue.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track revenue from TikTok?

Tag your bio link with utm_source=tiktok and a utm_campaign, add the Virling script, and pass the visitor ID into checkout. The TikTok row then shows its visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor.

Why does TikTok traffic land in Direct?

TikTok's in-app browser usually sends no referrer. Virling still credits TikTok when the in-app browser names itself in the user agent, but if the viewer opens the link in their phone's own browser, an untagged visit is Direct. A utm_source=tiktok tag keeps it credited to TikTok either way.

Does a link-in-bio page break TikTok tracking?

Only if it is hosted on another domain. Then its host becomes the referrer, which wins over utm_source, and the visit counts as Referral under that host. Per-button utm_campaign values still show revenue per button in the Campaign tab.

Can I see which TikTok video sent a paying customer?

Not directly, since every video shares the bio link. Change the bio link's utm_campaign for each series or offer and compare the periods, or match the daily spike in TikTok visits to the day a video went out.

Are links in TikTok captions and comments tracked?

A URL in a caption or comment is usually plain text that viewers cannot tap, so it sends no click to track. A pinned comment that says "link in bio" sends people to your profile, and the visit is measured when they tap the tagged bio link.

Should TikTok ads use the same tags?

Use utm_source=tiktok with a paid medium such as cpc or paid_social, so ad clicks land in the Paid channel and organic posts stay in Social. TikTok Ads revenue tracking covers the ad macros.
