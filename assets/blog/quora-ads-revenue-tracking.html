Quora Ads revenue tracking: see which campaign brings paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-24.

Quora Ads revenue tracking connects the money in your payment provider to the Quora ad, promoted answer or organic answer each buyer clicked first. Put utm_source=quora, utm_medium=cpc and your own utm_campaign on every ad’s landing page URL, run the Virling script on that page, and hand the visitor ID to checkout. Paid clicks then show up as Paid, and links in answers nobody paid for show up as Referral, with revenue for each campaign on the Campaign tab.

Step 1: fill in the landing page URL

Quora asks for the URL at the ad level, in the Landing page url field. Add the tags after it:

https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=quora-course-spring

That is the method in Quora’s own help center: type the page, follow it with ?, then join name and value pairs with = and &. Each value can be text you choose or one of six variables in double brackets, which Quora fills in per ad (source: Quora, UTM Parameters):

  • {{campaign.id}}, {{adset.id}} and {{ad.id}}: numbers Quora assigns.
  • {{campaign.name}}, {{adset.name}} and {{ad.name}}: the names you typed in Quora Ads Manager.

A landing page URL using them could read:

https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}}&utm_content={{ad.id}}

IDs, names and the catches

  • Renaming changes the URL. Quora says that when you edit a name, the name variables move to a new URL with the new name. In Virling, every new utm_campaign value is a new row, so a renamed campaign splits into two. Use {{campaign.id}} if you rename often.
  • Some characters get encoded. Quora percent-encodes characters such as /, & and $. Virling decodes the query string before it stores the tag, so %2F shows up as / again, but plain letters, digits and hyphens are still the tidiest names.
  • Grey and red hints. Below the URL, Quora lists the variables it recognises in grey and the ones it does not in red. Neither blocks the ad, so a typo goes live unless you check.
  • Preview after saving. {{ad.id}} and {{ad.name}} have no value until the ad is saved, so save it, reopen it and click the preview to see the real URL.
  • One medium. Whatever you choose for the campaign, keep utm_medium=cpc the same on every ad.

For split tests, Quora’s structure guide suggests unique UTM parameters per version (source: Quora, Campaign Structure Best Practices). Put the version in utm_content; Virling keeps it on each pageview while reports group by campaign. You can also build tagged links in the free UTM builder.

Promoted answers versus image, text and video ads

Quora sells two kinds of placement. Image, text and video ads are ads you write, each with its own landing page URL. A promoted answer is one of your existing answers, shown in full with a “Promoted by” label, and readers can upvote, share and comment on it (source: Quora, Ads Setup Guide). The same guide suggests bidding on clicks for promoted answers, and on conversions only when the answer links to your site.

That difference matters for tags. A written ad has one URL, used only by paid clicks. A promoted answer has the links you put in the answer, and those are clicked by unpaid readers too. Quora handles this with a separate field: choose Promote an answer as the media type, then fill in UTM Parameters under URL formatting, starting with ? and leaving out the answer URL:

?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=quora-promoted-answer

According to the UTM Parameters article, these are merged into the query parameters already on the answer’s links and win when a name repeats. So the answer itself can keep utm_medium=referral for organic readers, and paid clicks arrive with cpc and the ad’s campaign. Leave the field empty and every promoted click carries the answer’s own tags.

Where organic Quora visits end up

Virling decides one channel per session, from its first pageview. Unlike Reddit, Quora is not on any of Virling’s lists of community, social or search sites, so organic Quora traffic is Referral rather than Communities:

  • Paid medium. cpc, ppc, paid, paidsearch, paid_social or paidsocial, in any letter case, makes the session Paid before the referrer is looked at.
  • quora.com referrer. With no paid or email medium, the session is Referral, and the referrer host shows as quora.com. Untagged ads and answer clicks sit together here.
  • Tag but no referrer. utm_source=quora (or ref=quora) is not a known source, so that is Referral as well.
  • Nothing at all. The session is Direct. Virling does not recognise Quora’s in-app browser, so such a visit is filed under Typed or bookmark.

Labels such as promoted or sponsored are not paid mediums, nor is paid-social with a hyphen. The rules are written out in how channels are decided.

Tag answer links on purpose, so they are easy to find under Referral:

  • Image, text or video ad: utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=quora-course-spring
  • Link in an organic answer: utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=answer-learn-bookkeeping

There is more about unpaid answers in Quora revenue tracking for answers.

The qclid parameter

On each ad click, Quora appends qclid to the landing page URL, and its Conversions API needs that value sent back, because conversions cannot be attributed without it (source: Quora, Ads Setup Guide). Virling has no use for it: the channel and campaign come from UTM tags (or ref), so a click carrying just a qclid is treated like an untagged one. Keep it for Quora and add the tags next to it.

Step 2: run the script on landing pages

The Virling script goes in the <head> of the pages your ads and answers link to, and of your app. It records the tags of the page a visitor lands on and gives them a first-party visitor ID. A redirect that drops the query string drops the campaign with it; UTM parameters lost after redirect shows how to keep it. Installation steps are in the script docs.

Step 3: connect payments

After you connect your payment provider, each checkout needs the visitor ID so Virling can match the payment to a visit. Stripe, Polar, Dodo Payments and LemonSqueezy payment links on a page with the script receive it automatically. Server-side checkouts should pass the virling_vid cookie, as link checkout to visits explains.

The default model gives the payment, minus refunds, to the last visit within 90 days that was not Direct. A buyer who clicks your Quora ad, comes back by bookmark and pays is still counted for the ad. A buyer who later follows a link in one of your organic answers is counted for that answer instead. First touch is described next to it in the attribution model.

Step 4: see revenue per campaign

  • Select Paid in the Channels card to filter to paid-medium visits from Quora and any other network tagged that way.
  • Open the Campaign tab: one row per utm_campaign, with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor. With {{campaign.id}} the rows are numbers, so keep a list of which ID is which.
  • Select Referral and look for quora.com to see what answers earned.
  • From the Payments page, open any payment for the visits that led up to it and the one that was credited.
  • Use Time to pay to see how long buyers usually take before you cut a campaign.

Spend goes in Site settings, under Monthly spend per channel, as one figure for Paid, such as Paid 800. The Profit by channel card compares it with Paid revenue, so add up every network you tag as paid before typing it.

A made-up example

The figures here are invented. Picture an online bookkeeping course that sells for $150, with two Quora campaigns that each spent $400 according to Quora Ads Manager. Campaign A, an image ad, collected 40 leads at $10 each, and 2 of them bought the course: $300 of revenue and $200 of spend per buyer. Campaign B, a promoted answer, collected 20 leads at $20 each, and 5 bought: $750 and $80 per buyer. A wins on cost per lead; B is the one that earned back more than its spend. Try your own figures in the cost per lead calculator.

Troubleshooting Quora numbers

  • Ad clicks are under Referral. The landing page URL has no paid medium, or a promoted answer has an empty URL formatting field.
  • Ad clicks are under Direct. A redirect lost the tags, or an untagged ad opened without a referrer.
  • Answer clicks are under Paid. An answer contains the ad’s link. Give answers their own.
  • A campaign row shows braces. {{campaign.id}} arrived as typed, so the variable is misspelt or unsupported; Quora would have shown it in red.
  • One campaign is split across two rows. It was renamed while using {{campaign.name}}.
  • Payments are Unattributed. The visitor ID is missing at checkout, or the visit is older than the lookback window.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Quora ad campaign brought a paying customer?

Add utm_source=quora, utm_medium=cpc and a utm_campaign to the Landing page url field of each ad. Install the Virling script and send the visitor ID to checkout, and each payment is matched to the visit before it. The Campaign tab shows revenue by campaign.

Which variables can I use in Quora UTM tags?

Quora supports six: {{campaign.id}}, {{campaign.name}}, {{adset.id}}, {{adset.name}}, {{ad.id}} and {{ad.name}}. The IDs stay fixed, while the name variables change the URL when you rename something, which splits a campaign into two rows in Virling.

How do I tag a promoted answer?

Choose Promote an answer as the media type and fill in UTM Parameters under URL formatting, starting with a question mark. Quora merges those into the answer’s links and they win on matching names, so paid clicks get utm_medium=cpc while organic readers keep the answer’s own tags.

Why are organic Quora visits in Referral and not Communities?

Quora is not on Virling’s lists of community, social or search sites, and quora is not a known utm_source. Without a paid or email medium, a visit from quora.com or tagged utm_source=quora is Referral, with quora.com as the referrer host.

Does Virling read the qclid?

No. Quora appends qclid to the landing page URL for its Conversions API. Virling picks the channel and campaign from UTM tags, so a click with only a qclid counts as untagged. Keep the qclid and add UTM tags.

Does this connect to my Quora ad account?

No. Nothing here needs access to Quora Ads Manager: Virling uses your ad tags, the Paid channel and your payments. To see profit, enter one monthly figure for Paid spend in Site settings for the Profit by channel card.
