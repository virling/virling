Quora Ads revenue tracking: see which campaign brings paying customers

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-24.

Quora Ads revenue tracking means joining each payment on your site to the Quora ad click that brought the buyer. Add utm_source=quora, utm_medium=cpc and a utm_campaign to the landing page URL of each ad, add the Virling script to your site, and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Virling counts the click as Paid, while links in your organic answers stay in Referral. The Campaign tab then shows payments and revenue per ad campaign.

Step 1: tag the ad

Put the tags in the Landing page url field of each ad, with your own campaign name:

https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=quora-saas-nov

Quora’s help center describes the same thing: enter the landing page at the ad level, then add the UTM names and values after it. A value can be plain text or one of six variables in double braces: {{campaign.id}}, {{campaign.name}}, {{adset.id}}, {{adset.name}}, {{ad.id}} and {{ad.name}} (source: Quora, UTM Parameters). With variables, the URL looks like this:

https://yourdomain.com/?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={{campaign.id}}&utm_content={{ad.id}}

  • Name or ID. The IDs are numbers that Quora assigns, and the names are the ones you typed in Ads Manager. An ID does not depend on the name; a name is easier to read in a report. Pick one style and keep it, because a campaign tagged two ways becomes two rows.
  • Plain names. The same article says that characters such as /, & and $ are percent-encoded, so letters, numbers and hyphens are the safest choice for names.
  • Check the spelling. Quora says it lists supported variables in grey below the URL and unsupported ones in red, and that the ad ID and ad name have no value until the ad is saved.
  • Split ads with utm_content. Quora’s own guide suggests unique UTM parameters when you test versions of an ad (source: Quora, Campaign Structure Best Practices). Virling stores utm_content with the pageview, but reports group by campaign.

The free UTM builder makes tagged links for you.

Paid clicks or organic answers: how Virling sorts them

An ad and an ordinary answer can both send a visitor from quora.com, so the referrer cannot tell them apart. The medium can. Every session gets one channel from its first pageview, and the first matching rule wins:

  • A paid medium. cpc, ppc, paid, paidsearch, paid_social or paidsocial, in any case, is Paid. It is checked first, so it wins over a quora.com referrer.
  • No paid or email medium, with a Quora referrer. quora.com is not on the list of search, community or social hosts, so the visit is Referral. An untagged ad lands here, mixed in with your answers.
  • No referrer, with a source tag. quora is not a known source either, so utm_source=quora is Referral too.
  • No referrer and no tags. The visit is Direct. Quora’s app is not among the in-app browsers that Virling credits to a channel.

Spelling counts. promoted, sponsored and paid-social with a hyphen are not on the list, so those clicks are not Paid. See how channels are decided.

Tag the unpaid side as well, with a medium that is not paid:

  • Ad: utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=quora-saas-nov
  • Answer: utm_source=quora&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=answer-best-invoicing-tool

For the unpaid side, see Quora revenue tracking for answers.

Promoted answers need their own tags

A promoted answer is an answer you pay to show, so paid and unpaid readers can click the same link in it. Quora documents a way to separate them. When you promote an answer, the ad level has UTM Parameters in the URL formatting section, and what you enter there starts with ?:

?utm_source=quora&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=quora-promoted-answer

Quora says these are merged with the parameters already in the answer’s links, and override them when the names match (source: Quora, UTM Parameters). So keep utm_medium=referral on the link in the answer, and put utm_medium=cpc and the ad’s own utm_campaign in the URL formatting section. If you leave that section empty, a promoted click carries the answer’s tags and is counted as Referral.

What about the qclid?

When someone clicks your ad, Quora adds a qclid parameter to your landing page URL, which its Conversions API uses to attribute conversions (source: Quora, Ads Setup Guide). It is for Quora’s own measurement. Virling does not use it, so a click with only a qclid is sorted like an untagged one. Leave it on and add UTM tags.

Step 2: add the script

Add the Virling script to the <head> of every page an ad can land on, and to your app. It reads the tags on the landing page and sets the first-party visitor ID. Redirects on your site must keep the query string; see UTM parameters lost after redirect. The setup is in the script docs.

Step 3: link checkout

Connect your payment provider and pass the visitor ID into checkout, so each payment can be matched to a visit. Payment links from Stripe, Polar, Dodo Payments and LemonSqueezy get the ID from the script when they are on a page that runs it; a checkout you create on your server needs the virling_vid cookie. See link checkout to visits.

Under the default model the payment, after refunds, is credited to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct. Someone who clicks your ad on Monday and types your address on Thursday is still credited to the ad. Someone who clicks the ad and later clicks a link in your organic answer is credited to the answer. See the attribution model; first touch credits the earliest visit instead.

Step 4: read payments per campaign

  • In the Channels card, click Paid. The page filters to visits with a paid medium, from Quora and any other platform you tag the same way.
  • Open the Campaign tab. Each utm_campaign is a row with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor. Starting typed names with quora- keeps platforms easy to tell apart.
  • Click Referral instead to find what your answers brought, next to your other referring sites.
  • Open a payment from the Payments page to see the visits before it, with the credited one marked.
  • Check Time to pay before you pause a campaign that looks weak.

To see profit, type what you spend on ads each month in Site settings, under Monthly spend per channel, such as Paid 500. The Profit by channel card then shows spend, revenue and profit for the Paid channel as a whole, so include every platform you tag as Paid. The result is only as good as the amount you type.

A made-up example

These numbers are invented to show the arithmetic. Two campaigns each cost $400, as read in Quora Ads Manager. Campaign A brought 40 leads, so $10 per lead, and 2 of them paid $150 each: $300 of revenue and $200 of spend per customer. Campaign B brought 20 leads, so $20 per lead, and 5 of them paid $150 each: $750 of revenue and $80 of spend per customer. Campaign A has the cheaper leads, but Campaign B is the one that brought in more than it cost. Run your own numbers in the cost per lead calculator.

What to check if the numbers look off

  • Ad revenue shows as Referral. The ad has no paid utm_medium, or a promoted answer has nothing in its URL formatting section. Open the ad’s landing page URL and check the address bar.
  • Ad revenue shows as Direct. The tags were dropped by a redirect, or the ad has none and the click sent no referrer.
  • Organic revenue shows as Paid. The ad’s tagged link was pasted into an answer. Make a separate link for each.
  • A campaign row shows braces. If {{campaign.id}} appears as typed, check the spelling and whether Quora lists the variable in red.
  • Revenue shows as Unattributed. The visitor ID is not reaching checkout, or the visit is older than the window.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Quora ad campaign brought a paying customer?

Add utm_source=quora, utm_medium=cpc and a utm_campaign to the landing page URL of each ad, add the Virling script to your site, connect your payment provider and pass the visitor ID into checkout. Each payment is credited to a visit, and the Campaign tab shows revenue per campaign.

Does Quora support dynamic UTM parameters?

Yes. Quora’s help center lists six variables that you can use as UTM values: {{campaign.id}}, {{campaign.name}}, {{adset.id}}, {{adset.name}}, {{ad.id}} and {{ad.name}}. Plain text works too.

How do I tell Quora Ads apart from organic answers?

Use a paid medium such as utm_medium=cpc on ads only, and utm_medium=referral on links in answers. In Virling, ad clicks land in Paid and answer clicks land in Referral. Give every ad campaign and every answer its own utm_campaign.

Which channel does a Quora ad click land in?

Paid, if the link carries utm_medium=cpc, ppc, paid, paidsearch, paid_social or paidsocial. The medium is checked first, so it wins over a quora.com referrer. Without a paid or email medium, a visit with a quora.com referrer or utm_source=quora is Referral, and one with no referrer and no tags is Direct.

Does Virling read the qclid?

No. Virling does not use Quora’s click ID to pick the channel or to name a campaign. It can stay on the link for Quora’s own conversion reports. Add UTM tags for Virling.

Does this connect to my Quora ad account?

No. The setup on this page uses only the tags on your ads, the Paid channel and your payments. You can type what you spend on Paid each month in Site settings, and the Profit by channel card uses that amount.
