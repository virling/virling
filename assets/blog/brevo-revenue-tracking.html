Brevo revenue tracking: see which campaign brought each payment

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-23.

Brevo revenue tracking matters most to SaaS teams and small businesses that send several kinds of message from one account: marketing campaigns, automations such as onboarding and trial reminders, transactional emails from the app, and SMS. Each kind is tagged in a different place. Once those links carry UTM tags and each Stripe or Paddle checkout carries the visitor ID, Virling credits the first payment to the tagged visit before it, so you can see which campaign or automation email led to a paid plan.

Campaigns, automations and transactional emails

Per Brevo’s help article on UTM parameters, the account-wide switch is under Settings > UTM parameters: click Activate UTM tracking. By default it uses brevo as the source, email as the medium, and the campaign, automation or template name as the campaign. Virling checks utm_medium before the referrer, so a tagged Brevo email lands in the Email channel even when the mail app sends a referrer of its own; the rule order is in the channels docs. What the switch covers depends on the kind of send:

  • Campaigns. One-off sends to a list. Each campaign has its own UTM tracking option in its additional settings, so check it before you send. Give each send a name with a date, such as winter-sale-2026 or launch-teams-2026-11, so it gets its own row.
  • Automations. The Send an email step has its own tracking option with a custom UTM campaign name; by default the name of the email template is used. Set it to the automation and the step, such as onboarding-1 or trial-ending-2, so each email in the sequence is a row. See set up a Send an email step in an automation.
  • Transactional emails. Receipts, password resets and trial notices that your app sends through Brevo’s API or SMTP relay. The UTM article speaks of campaigns, templates and automation messages, so do not assume API sends are tagged: click a link in a real one, and if the tags are missing, add them to the links in your template or code.

A campaign link then arrives as https://yourapp.com/pricing?utm_source=brevo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=winter-sale-2026. If a link already had its own query string, check that it survived too. Write your naming rules down once with the free UTM naming template.

Does turning on UTM tracking change old automations?

No. The account setting applies to campaigns, templates and automation messages created after you turn it on. An onboarding sequence built last year keeps whatever each of its emails had, so tagging stays off until you open every Send an email step and turn it on. Until you do, a trial user who clicks one of those emails looks like someone who typed your address, so the email gets no credit for the payment that follows. After the change, send yourself a test from each live automation and look at the address bar.

SMS from Brevo

Brevo’s help articles on UTM tracking cover email, so add the tags to the full URL yourself before you paste it into an SMS campaign or automation. Use utm_source=brevo, utm_medium=sms and a campaign starting with sms-, such as sms-winter-sale-2026, so a text and an email about the same offer stay on separate rows. If you shorten the link, tag it first. Because sms is not an email medium, these visits are filed under Referral with brevo as the source; on the dashboard, click Referral, then brevo. Stripe or Paddle payments for SaaS

Virling reads payments from your billing provider, not from Brevo, and joins them to visits through the visitor ID:

  • Stripe. Connect Stripe. Payment Links on buy.stripe.com need nothing, because the script adds the visitor ID. For Checkout Sessions created on your server, set client_reference_id to the virling_vid cookie.
  • Paddle. Connect Paddle Billing with a read-only API key, and pass customData: { virling_vid: window.virling?.vid?.() } to Paddle.Checkout.open. Virling reads it from the transaction’s custom_data.

Point campaign and automation links at a page that runs the script, such as pricing or billing settings, not straight at a checkout URL, so the visitor ID exists when the customer pays. See link checkout to visits.

Two rules decide what a Brevo email can be credited with. When a free trial on Stripe or Paddle converts on its own, its first charge has no visitor ID and is credited to the visit before the trial started, not to the trial reminder. Renewals, plan changes and any later payment by the same customer keep the channel of the first payment, so an upgrade email shows clicks but not upgrade revenue. What a Brevo email can win is a first payment, such as a campaign or onboarding email whose click leads to checkout. See renewals. If you sell physical products instead, connect Shopify or WooCommerce.

What to check if the numbers look off

  • Automation clicks show as Direct. The email was created before you turned on the account setting, or a redirect dropped the tags. Open its Send an email step and turn tracking on.
  • Transactional email clicks show as Direct. Your app sends them through the API without tags. Add the tags to those links.
  • A Stripe or Paddle payment has no visit. The checkout had no visitor ID: the link went straight to checkout, the server did not set client_reference_id, or Paddle.js got no customData. See unattributed revenue.
  • Texts show as Direct. The SMS link had no tags, or they were added after shortening. Tag the full URL and send a test to your own phone.
  • An upgrade campaign shows no revenue. Expected: later payments keep the channel of the customer’s first payment.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Brevo campaign or automation led to a paid plan?

Turn on UTM tracking in Brevo and on each existing automation email, tag SMS and transactional links yourself, and pass the visitor ID to your Stripe or Paddle checkout. Then filter the dashboard to Email and open the Campaign tab, where each campaign and automation step has its own row of first-payment revenue.

Does turning on UTM tracking in Brevo tag existing automations?

No. The account-wide setting applies to new campaigns, templates and automation messages. Open each existing message and turn UTM tracking on there.

Are Brevo transactional emails tagged automatically?

Do not assume so. Transactional emails are sent by your app through Brevo’s API or SMTP relay, and Brevo’s UTM setting speaks of campaigns, templates and automation messages. Click a link in a real one, and if the tags are missing, add utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign to the links in your template or code.

Where do Brevo SMS clicks show up?

Tag SMS links yourself with utm_source=brevo and utm_medium=sms. Because sms is not an email medium, those visits are filed under Referral with brevo as the source, with the campaign stored on each visit.
