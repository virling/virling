Brevo revenue tracking: see which campaign brought each payment

By Devendra Variya, co-founder of Virling. Published 2026-11-23.

Brevo revenue tracking works best when you join each payment to the click that brought the buyer. Turn on Brevo’s UTM tracking so every campaign and automation email link carries utm_source=brevo, utm_medium=email and a utm_campaign, tag your SMS links yourself, and store the visitor ID on each Shopify or WooCommerce order or Stripe checkout. Virling then credits each payment to the tagged visit before it, and the Campaign tab shows revenue per campaign and automation email, counted once, next to every other channel.

Why Brevo clicks land in Direct

Most people read email and texts in an app, and most of those apps send no referrer when a link is clicked. Without UTM tags, the visit has nothing that says it came from Brevo, so it is counted as Direct, and so is the order or payment that follows. Brevo’s own reports show opens and clicks, but the payment happens in your store or billing provider. For the wider picture, see email marketing revenue tracking for online stores.

Turn on UTM tracking in Brevo

Per Brevo’s help article on UTM parameters, the account-wide setting is under Settings > UTM parameters: click Activate UTM tracking. It then applies to new campaigns, templates and automation messages. It does not change messages that already exist, so open each live one and turn UTM tracking on there too.

  • Email campaigns. Each campaign has its own UTM tracking option in its additional settings, so you can check or change it before you send.
  • Automations. The Send an email step has its own tracking option, with a custom UTM campaign name, such as the name of the automation; by default the name of the email template is used. See set up a Send an email step in an automation.
  • SMS. Brevo’s help articles on UTM tracking cover email. For texts, add the tags to the full URL yourself before you paste it into the message.

By default Brevo uses brevo as the source, email as the medium, and the campaign, automation or template name as the campaign. Check the values on the settings page and keep them. A link from a campaign named winter-sale-2026 then arrives like this:

https://yourstore.com/collections/sale?utm_source=brevo&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=winter-sale-2026

After you send, click a link in your own inbox and check the address bar on your site. The tags that arrive there are the ones Virling reads. If a link already had its own query string, check that it survived as well.

Name campaigns and automations so they read well

Because utm_campaign comes from the name in Brevo, your names become report rows. Write the convention down once with the free UTM naming template, then follow it:

  • Campaigns: one name per send, lowercase, with the date, such as winter-sale-2026 or launch-teams-2026-11.
  • Automation emails: the automation name and the step, such as abandoned-cart-1 and abandoned-cart-2, so each email is its own row and you can tell which automation it belongs to.
  • SMS: utm_source=brevo, utm_medium=sms and a campaign starting with sms-, such as sms-winter-sale-2026, so a text and an email about the same sale stay on separate rows.
  • Keep names stable. If you rename a live automation, later clicks carry the new name and show as a new row. Winter sale and winter-sale are two rows too.

How Virling sorts a Brevo visit

Every session gets one channel from its first pageview, and utm_medium is checked first, ignoring case. A medium of email, newsletter or e-mail is the Email channel, whatever the referrer says, so Brevo email clicks land there with brevo as the source. A text has no referrer and sms is not an email medium, so the next rule reads utm_source; brevo is not a known search, social or AI source, so SMS clicks land in Referral, with brevo as the source and the campaign stored with the visit. Join the click to the payment

Virling reads payments from where they happen, not from Brevo, and joins them to visits through the visitor ID:

  • Shopify or WooCommerce. Connect Shopify with a custom app token that can only read orders, or WooCommerce with a read-only REST API key. Virling imports new orders every minute and lowers revenue when you refund. A short snippet stores the visitor ID on each order.
  • Stripe. Connect Stripe. Hosted payment links on buy.stripe.com need nothing, because the script adds the visitor ID. For Stripe Checkout created on your server, set client_reference_id to the virling_vid cookie.

Link your emails to a page that runs the script, such as a product, pricing or billing page, not straight to a checkout URL, so the visitor ID is there when the buyer pays. See link checkout to visits.

Under the default model, the whole payment is credited to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct, with its channel, source and campaign. A shopper who clicks your abandoned cart email and comes back the next day by typing your address is credited to that email. A shopper who only opened the email and later arrived from a search result is credited to Organic search. See the attribution model.

See revenue per campaign and automation

1. Open the Email channel

On the dashboard, the Channels card lists each channel with visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor. Click Email to filter the whole page to visits from your emails. For texts, click Referral, then the brevo source.

2. Switch to the Campaign tab

The Campaign tab on the same card has one row per utm_campaign, so each campaign and each automation email shows its own visitors and revenue. Compare them by revenue per visitor, not by visitors: an automation email that sends a few buyers can be worth more than a campaign that sends many who do not buy.

3. Open the payments

The Payments page lists every order and payment with the channel it was credited to. Open one to see the visits before it and which one got the credit.

What to check if the numbers look off

  • Brevo clicks show as Direct. UTM tracking is off for that message, or a redirect dropped the tags. Messages created before you turned tracking on keep their old setting, so check each live automation, not only campaigns.
  • Payments show as Unattributed. The order or checkout has no visitor ID: the snippet is missing, or the link went straight to checkout. See unattributed revenue.
  • One automation is split across rows. An email was renamed, or names differ in case. Pick one name and keep it.
  • Texts show as Direct. The SMS link had no tags, or the tags were added after the link was shortened. Tag the full URL first and send a test to your own phone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which Brevo campaign brought a payment?

Turn on UTM tracking in Brevo so every campaign and automation email link carries utm_source=brevo, utm_medium=email and a utm_campaign. Connect Shopify, WooCommerce or Stripe and store the visitor ID on each order or checkout. Each payment is then credited to the tagged visit before it, and the Campaign tab shows revenue per campaign and automation email.

Does turning on UTM tracking in Brevo tag existing automations?

No. The account-wide setting applies to new campaigns, templates and automation messages. Open each existing message and turn UTM tracking on there.

Which channel does Brevo traffic land in?

An email click carries utm_medium=email, so it lands in Email whatever the referrer. An SMS click tagged utm_medium=sms has no referrer and is not an email medium, so it lands in Referral with brevo as its source.

Does Virling replace Brevo?

No. Virling does not send email or texts. It reads tagged visits and your Shopify, WooCommerce or Stripe payments, so keep sending with Brevo.
