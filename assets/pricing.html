questions

Questions about plans.

Is the self-hosted version limited?

No. It is the same open-source code as the hosted plans, with every feature. You run it on your own server and pay nothing.

When do the hosted plans open?

The hosted beta is open now and free to try: start it at beta.virling.com. Paid plans have no date yet; hold a founding spot to lock in the price.

What do the founding pre-orders cost?

Personal is $11.40 today and Agency is $29 today, each holding one of 50 founding spots for that plan. Your first month after launch is already paid, then the same price every month, locked for life. You can cancel and get a full refund any time before launch.

Which payment providers work?

Stripe, Dodo Payments, Polar and LemonSqueezy, with read-only keys where the provider supports them. LemonSqueezy payments import, but linking them to visits is not available yet.
