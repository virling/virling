06/questions

Questions agencies ask.

How do I show a client which marketing channel brought paying customers?

Add one script tag to the client's site and connect their payment provider with a read-only key. Virling joins each payment to the visits before it and shows revenue and revenue per visitor for every channel. The weekly report puts that on one page for the client.

Which payment providers work?

Stripe, Dodo Payments, Polar and LemonSqueezy. Stripe, Dodo Payments and Polar keys can be limited to read access. LemonSqueezy keys can't be limited, but Virling only reads with them, and it links LemonSqueezy payments to visits through a webhook once your server has a public https address. Virling never creates, changes or refunds anything.

What is in the weekly report?

Revenue for the week and the change from the week before, visitors, conversion, revenue per visitor and the top sources by revenue. You pick the day and time and up to 10 recipients per client.

Is the report white-label?

Yes. Each report carries your agency name, logo, accent color and footer. The small Powered by Virling line can be turned off.

Can I see the last few months right away?

Payments are backfilled 90 days from the provider as soon as the key is connected. Visits only exist from when the script is added, so channel credit builds up from then.

Can I self-host it for my clients?

Yes. Virling is open source under AGPLv3 and runs as one binary on your own server, free. The hosted beta is free now and paid plans start at launch; the founding price locks in today.
