The funnel conversion formula

Each step's conversion rate is the share of people from the step before who reached it, and the overall rate is paid customers out of visitors:

step conversion rate = people at this step / people at the previous step × 100%
visit to paid rate = paid customers / visitors × 100%

The overall rate is the step rates multiplied together, so a small gain at any step lifts the end result by the same proportion. Revenue per visitor adds what customers spent:

revenue per visitor = revenue / visitors = visit to paid rate × revenue per customer

Use the same period and the same group of people for every step. For a store, the steps are visitors, add to carts, checkouts and orders; for a SaaS product, visitors, signups, trials and paying customers. If your product has no trial or no cart, leave that step empty.

A worked example

The defaults above are made-up numbers to show the method. 10,000 visitors led to 400 signups, a 4% step rate. 120 of those signups started a trial, 30%, and 30 trials paid, 25%. Overall that is 30 customers from 10,000 visitors, a 0.30% visit to paid rate. Those 30 customers paid $1,500, so each visitor was worth $0.15 and each customer $50.

The biggest drop is the first step: 96% of visitors left without signing up. That is normal for the top of a funnel, so compare each step with its own past rather than with the other steps.

Why the same funnel differs by channel

One funnel for the whole site blends very different visitors. Someone who searched for what you sell, or asked an AI assistant for it, arrives ready to sign up; someone who clicked a post out of curiosity often leaves at the first step. A channel can bring many signups that never pay, or few visitors who nearly all buy. The overall funnel shows where people drop, but not which channels bring the people who drop.

Run the calculator once per channel with that channel's visitors, signups, trials, customers and revenue, and compare their revenue per visitor: it folds every step and the order size into one number per source. The revenue per visitor calculator and the conversion rate calculator compare channels side by side, and trial-to-paid conversion by traffic source walks through measuring the trial step per channel. Funnel conversion calculator FAQ

How do you calculate funnel conversion rate?

Divide the number of people at each step by the number at the step before, then multiply by 100. For the whole funnel, divide paid customers by visitors: 30 customers from 10,000 visitors is a 0.30% visit to paid rate.

What if my funnel has no trial or no cart?

Leave that step empty. The calculator skips it and measures the next step against the last one you filled in, so a funnel of visitors, signups and paid customers works the same way.

Which step should I fix first?

The step with the biggest drop is a starting point, but the top step almost always loses the most people. Compare each step with its own rate in earlier periods and per channel, and fix the one that is worst against its own history.

Why does revenue per visitor matter if I know the conversion rate?

Two channels with the same visit to paid rate can bring customers who spend very different amounts. Revenue per visitor is the visit to paid rate times revenue per customer, so it shows what each visit from a source is worth.
