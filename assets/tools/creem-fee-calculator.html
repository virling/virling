Creem fee FAQ

How much does Creem charge?

On Creem's pricing page, the pay as you go plan is 3.9% + 40 cents per successful transaction. On a $50 one-time sale that is $2.35, so you keep $47.65. On a $20 monthly subscription it is $1.18 a month, so you keep $18.82.

Does Creem charge extra for international cards or subscriptions?

Creem's pricing page lists no international card fees, and it does not list a separate fee for subscriptions. Both extras default to 0 here; change them if your terms differ.

Does Creem charge a monthly fee?

Not on the pay as you go plan. Creem's pricing page lists $0 monthly fees and $0 setup fees. The Partner plan has custom pricing with lower transaction fees.

What does this calculator leave out?

Currency conversion, refunds, chargebacks, payout costs and custom Partner plan pricing. Creem's pricing page does not list separate fees for these, so check Creem's terms for your setup.

Why does Creem cost more than a card processor?

Creem is a merchant of record: it sells your product on your behalf, so it is the seller on the receipt and handles sales tax and VAT as well as card processing. Compare it with other merchants of record in the merchant of record fee calculator.
