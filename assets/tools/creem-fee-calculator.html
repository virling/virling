Creem fees on one-time sales and subscriptions

Because Creem's pricing page lists 3.9% + 40 cents per successful transaction with no subscription surcharge, the billing model decides how many times the 40 cents is paid. Here is one customer's first year under four ways of charging.

BillingCharges in year oneCreem feesShare of revenueYou keep
$9 a month12$9.018.3%$98.99 of $108
$90 a year1$3.914.3%$86.09 of $90
$49 lifetime licence1$2.314.7%$46.69 of $49
$100 one-time1$4.304.3%$95.70 of $100

The $100 row matches the example on Creem's own pricing page. Monthly billing brings in the most cash but loses 8.3% of it, against 4.3% for the annual plan, because twelve charges pay twelve fixed fees.

Tax and payouts with Creem as merchant of record

Creem is the legal seller of record, so it collects and remits VAT, GST and sales tax for you (its pricing page says in 50+ countries, its docs say 190+). The pricing page does not say whether the 3.9% is taken on the price before tax or on the tax-inclusive total, which is why this calculator asks for the price before tax. If it were the total, a $50 sale with 20% VAT, $60 at checkout, would cost $2.74 instead of $2.35. Payouts go out on the 1st and 15th of each month, to a bank account or a USDC wallet; the pricing page lists no payout fee.

Below about $10 the fixed 40 cents does most of the damage: it is 8% of a $5 price, taking the total to 11.9%, while above $99 the share settles near the 3.9% floor. Against other merchants of record, $29 billed monthly to a US buyer costs $1.53 here, $1.71 on Dodo Payments and $1.95 on Paddle; see the merchant of record fee calculator for monthly totals.

Working back from a payout you want: price = (target + 0.40) / 0.961. A $25.00 payout needs a $26.43 price, of which Creem takes $1.43.

Creem fee FAQ

How much does Creem charge?

On Creem's pricing page, the pay as you go plan is 3.9% + 40 cents per successful transaction, with $0 monthly and setup fees. On a $50 one-time sale that is $2.35, so you keep $47.65. On a $20 monthly subscription it is $1.18 a month, so you keep $18.82.

Does Creem charge extra for international cards or subscriptions?

Creem's pricing page lists no international card fees, and it does not list a separate fee for subscriptions. Both extras default to 0 here; change them if your terms differ.

Is monthly or annual billing cheaper on Creem?

Annual, because the 40 cents is paid once a year instead of twelve times. A $9 monthly plan pays $9.01 in Creem fees over a year, 8.3% of $108, while a $90 annual plan pays $3.91, 4.3%.

When does Creem pay out?

Creem's pricing page says payouts are processed on the 1st and 15th of each month, to a bank account or a USDC wallet. It does not list a payout fee or a minimum payout.

Who handles sales tax, refunds and chargebacks?

Creem does. As merchant of record it is the legal seller, collects and remits VAT, GST and sales tax, and its pricing page says it handles refunds and chargebacks for you. The page lists no separate refund or chargeback fee, and no rate for currency conversion, so check Creem's terms before relying on that. The Partner plan offers lower transaction fees at custom prices.
