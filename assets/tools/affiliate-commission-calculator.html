The affiliate commission formula

The payout on one sale is the price times the commission rate:

payout per sale = price × commission rate

A one-time commission is paid once, on the first sale. A recurring commission is paid on every payment the customer makes, often only for a set number of months:

lifetime payout = monthly price × commission rate × months commission is paid

You break even when the payout equals the gross profit the customer brings, the revenue left after the cost of the product, hosting, payment fees and delivery. For a one-time commission, or a recurring one paid for every month the customer stays, the highest commission that breaks even is simply your gross margin. If the commission stops before the customer does, the profit from the later months pays for the earlier commission too:

break-even commission = gross margin × months the customer pays ÷ months commission is paid

That can be more than 100% of each paid month, but only if customers really stay as long as you entered.

A worked example

These are made-up numbers, the defaults above. A product sells for $120 with a 60% gross margin and pays affiliates a one-time 20% commission. Each referred sale pays the partner $120 × 0.20 = $24 and leaves $120 × 0.60 - $24 = $48 of gross profit. The highest commission that breaks even is 60%, or $72 per sale.

Switch to a recurring commission on a $49 monthly plan with an 80% margin, 30% paid for the first 12 months, and customers who pay for 18 months. The partner earns $49 × 0.30 = $14.70 a month and $14.70 × 12 = $176.40 in total. The customer brings $49 × 0.80 × 18 = $705.60 of gross profit, so $529.20 is left, and the commission could go as high as 0.80 × 18 ÷ 12 = 120% before it broke even.

Set rates from paid revenue, not clicks

The formula only holds if the months and the sales you enter are the ones partners really send. Clicks and signups say little about who pays, renews or asks for a refund, and payout software counts sales with its own cookie rules. Affiliate commission calculator FAQ

How do you calculate an affiliate commission?

Multiply the sale price by the commission rate. A $120 sale at a 20% commission pays the affiliate $24. For a recurring commission, multiply the monthly payout by the number of months it is paid.

What is the highest affiliate commission I can afford?

The commission breaks even when it equals the gross profit the customer brings. If you pay on every sale or every month the customer pays, that is your gross margin: at a 60% margin, a 60% commission leaves nothing. Pay less to keep a profit and cover other costs.

What is the difference between a one-time and a recurring commission?

A one-time commission is paid once, on the first sale. A recurring commission is paid on each renewal of a subscription, either for as long as the customer pays or for a set number of months.

What does this calculator leave out?

It counts only the price, gross margin and commission. Refunds, chargebacks, payout fees, coupons partners give away and the cost of running the program are not included, so keep some room below the break-even commission for them.
