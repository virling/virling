Why revenue shows up as Direct

Direct is not a channel. It is what is left when a visit carries no referrer and no UTM tag, or when a payment cannot be tied to the visit that came before it. Most of it comes from a few places: links you did not tag, redirects that drop the tags, and checkouts on another domain that never receive the visitor ID. Each section of this checklist covers one of them, and the test purchase at the end shows whether the whole path works.

A payment with no visitor ID cannot be credited after the fact, so fix the path first and judge channels on the payments that come after.

Attribution audit checklist FAQ

Why is my revenue showing as Direct?

Because the visit before the payment had no referrer and no UTM tag, or the payment never got the visitor ID and could not be tied to a visit. Untagged links, redirects that drop the query string and checkout on another domain are the usual causes.

What should an attribution audit check?

That the tracking script runs on every page, that your links are tagged and keep their tags through redirects, that checkout receives the visitor ID, that the payment provider is connected, and that a test purchase shows the source you tagged.

Can I fix attribution for past payments?

No. A payment that arrived without a visitor ID has no link to a visit. Fix the path, run a test purchase, and judge channels on the payments that come after.

Where is my progress saved?
