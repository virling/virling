Stripe fees: a worked example

A one-time $100 card payment, at the standard rates on Stripe's US pricing page: 2.9% + 30 cents for a domestic card, 1.5% more for an international card and 1% more when currency conversion is needed.

What to charge to receive a set amount

Adding the fee to the amount falls short, because Stripe charges its percentage on the whole price. Use this instead, with the rate as a decimal (2.9% is 0.029):

price = (amount + 0.30) / (1 - rate)

To receive $100 from a US card: (100 + 0.30) / (1 - 0.029) = $103.30. The fee on $103.30 is $3.30, which leaves $100.00. Before you add card fees to what buyers pay, check whether passing them on is allowed where you sell.

Stripe and payment fee FAQ

How much does Stripe charge?

On its US pricing page Stripe lists 2.9% + 30 cents for domestic cards, 1.5% more for international cards and 1% more if currency conversion is needed. Stripe Billing adds 0.7% of billing volume for subscriptions. On a $100 payment from a US card that is $3.20.

What is a merchant of record?

A company that sells your product on your behalf and handles sales tax, VAT and chargebacks. Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Polar, Dodo Payments and Creem work this way, which is part of why their rates are higher than Stripe's.

Why is the fee higher on cheap products?

Every provider adds a fixed fee per sale. On a $5 sale, 50 cents is 10% before the percentage fee, so low prices lose a much larger share.

What does this calculator leave out?

Payout fees, currency conversion, refunds, disputes, and sales tax tools you add yourself, because they depend on your setup. High-volume and custom plans are also left out.

Which provider is cheapest?

It depends on your price, your share of international buyers and whether you need tax handled for you. Enter your own numbers: the order changes with them.

What does Stripe take from $100?

At the US rates on this page, $3.20 from a US card, so you keep $96.80. An international card costs $4.70, or $5.70 when currency conversion is needed. With Stripe Billing, the US card payment costs $3.90.

Does Stripe return the fee on a refund?

No. Stripe's page on fees for refunded payments says the payment processing and currency conversion fees from the original charge are not returned.

How is the Stripe Billing rate added?

Stripe Billing is charged as a percentage of billing volume, so it adds to the card percentage and leaves the fixed 30 cents as it is. For a US card, 2.9% plus 0.7% makes 3.6% + 30 cents, which is $3.90 on a $100 subscription payment.
