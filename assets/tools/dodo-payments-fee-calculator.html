The cap matters on large invoices: a $1,500 one-time payment by ACH would cost $22.50 at 1.5%, so the $15 cap applies and the fee is 1.0%. ACH covers one-time payments only, so subscriptions stay on card rates.

Payout costs on Dodo Payments

The fees above come off each sale; getting the money out can cost more. The pricing table lists payouts as free, but the FAQ on the same page says a $5 fee applies when a payout is under $1,000. A $600 payout would arrive as $595, while $1,000 or more carries no payout fee. Businesses outside the US that take USD by SWIFT pay $25 per payout, and settling in a currency other than USD uses standard foreign exchange rates.

How Dodo's extras add up

This calculator adds the extras to the 4% base: a subscriber paying with a card from abroad is charged 4% + 1.5% + 0.5% = 6%, plus 40 cents. The mix of buyers therefore moves your effective rate more than the price does. At $49 a month, going from no buyers abroad to half of them abroad lifts the fee from $2.61 to $2.97 per sale, and at 100 subscribers that is about $37 a month.

To price for a target payout, use price = (target + 0.40) / (1 - rate) with the rate for your mix. Keeping $25.00 from a US subscriber takes $26.60 (rate 4.5%); from a subscriber abroad it takes $27.02 (rate 6%).

Dodo Payments fee FAQ

How much does Dodo Payments charge?

On the Standard plan, 4% + 40 cents per sale paid with a US card or wallet, with no setup fee, monthly fee or minimum. A card from outside the US adds 1.5% and a subscription adds 0.5%. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Is ACH Direct Debit cheaper than a card on Dodo Payments?

Yes, for one-time payments in USD. Dodo's pricing page lists ACH at 1.5% capped at $15 per transaction, in place of the card fee, so a $40 sale costs $0.60 instead of $2.00. ACH does not cover subscriptions. SEPA Direct Debit works the same way for one-time payments in euros, capped at 15 euros.

Does Dodo Payments charge for payouts?

The pricing table lists payouts as free, but the FAQ on the same page says a $5 fee applies to payouts under $1,000. Non-US businesses paid in USD by SWIFT pay $25, and non-USD settlement uses standard foreign exchange rates. Fewer, larger payouts avoid the $5 fee.

What do refunds and disputes cost on Dodo Payments?

$1 per refund and $30 per dispute, according to the pricing page. It also lists $18 per dispute resolved through Visa RDR, $27 per Ethoca alert and $15 per dispute deflected. None of these are in the calculator, so subtract them separately if you expect them.

Which buyers count as international?

Dodo's pricing page applies the 1.5% to cards and other payment methods from outside the US. Domestic payments in India in rupees are listed at 4% + 15 cents plus the international fee. Put the share of your buyers paying from outside the US in the international buyers field.
