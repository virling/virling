Dodo Payments fee FAQ

How much does Dodo Payments charge?

On Dodo's pricing page, the Standard plan is 4% + 40 cents per domestic US transaction. International payments add 1.5% and subscriptions add 0.5%, so an international subscription is 6% + 40 cents. On a $50 one-time sale to a US buyer that is $2.40, so you keep $47.60.

Does Dodo Payments charge a monthly fee?

Not on the Standard plan. Dodo's pricing page says there are zero setup fees, zero monthly charges and zero minimum commitments. Enterprise pricing is custom.

What counts as an international payment?

Dodo's pricing page describes international payments as cards and other payment methods outside the US, and adds 1.5% to them. Set the international buyers field to the share of your sales that come from outside the US.

What does this calculator leave out?

Dodo's pricing page lists other fees this calculator does not include: $1 per refund, $30 per dispute, 3% extra for PayPal and buy now, pay later methods, a $5 fee on payouts under $1,000, $25 for USD SWIFT payouts to non-US businesses, standard foreign exchange rates on non-USD settlements, and a separate rate for domestic India payments. Check the page for the ones that apply to you.

Why does Dodo Payments cost more than a card processor?

Dodo Payments is a merchant of record: it sells your product on your behalf, so it is the seller on the receipt and handles sales tax and VAT as well as card processing. Compare it with other merchants of record in the merchant of record fee calculator.
