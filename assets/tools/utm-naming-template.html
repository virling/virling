The naming rules

  • Lowercase everything. Most tools treat LinkedIn and linkedin as two sources.
  • Hyphens, not spaces or underscores. spring-launch, never Spring Launch or spring_launch.
  • One source per platform. LinkedIn is always linkedin, whether the post is organic or paid. The medium tells them apart.
  • A short, fixed list of mediums. email, social, cpc, paidsocial, referral, podcast. Do not invent a new one per campaign.
  • Campaign names are the push, then the month. spring-launch-2026-11 sorts by name and stays unique when the campaign runs again.
  • Never tag links between your own pages. The tag replaces the visitor's real source.

The pattern is utm_source = the platform or list, utm_medium = the kind of channel, utm_campaign = campaign name plus -yyyy-mm. Use utm_content only to tell two links in the same place apart, such as header-button and footer-link.

A worked example

You announce a spring launch in November 2026. The newsletter link becomes ?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=spring-launch-2026-11, the LinkedIn post ?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=spring-launch-2026-11 and the LinkedIn ad ?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paidsocial&utm_campaign=spring-launch-2026-11. Every link from the launch groups under one campaign, and each platform still shows up as one source.

UTM naming convention FAQ

What is a UTM naming convention?

A written list of the exact values your team uses for utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign, plus the rules for new ones. It stops one channel from splitting into several rows in your reports.

Should UTM values use hyphens or underscores?

Either works if you never mix them. Hyphens are the common choice and read well in URLs. Pick one, write it down and lowercase every value.

What is the difference between source and medium?

Source is where the link lives, such as linkedin or newsletter. Medium is the kind of channel, such as social, email or cpc. The same source can appear with different mediums for organic posts and ads.

Should campaign names include a date?

Yes, for anything that repeats. Adding the month as yyyy-mm at the end keeps each run separate and makes campaigns sort in order.
