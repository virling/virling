questions

LemonSqueezy fee FAQ

How much does LemonSqueezy charge per sale?

LemonSqueezy's pricing page lists 5% + 50 cents per transaction, with no monthly fee for payment processing. On a $29 sale to a US card that is $1.95, so you keep $27.05.

What extra fees can LemonSqueezy add?

Its fees page lists 1.5% for transactions outside the US, 1.5% for PayPal, 0.5% for subscription payments and 5% for payments recovered through abandoned cart emails. Its affiliate fees page adds 3% for each order referred by an affiliate.

Can I still sign up for LemonSqueezy?

No. LemonSqueezy's pricing page says it no longer accepts new sign-ups and will shut down on March 31, 2027. To compare other merchants of record, use the merchant of record fee calculator.

What does this calculator leave out?

Custom pricing, which LemonSqueezy offers on request for products under $10; abandoned cart and affiliate fees; refunds, where LemonSqueezy deducts the refunded amount minus its platform fee from your next payout; the $15 dispute fee on chargebacks; and payout fees, which its fees page puts at nothing for US bank accounts, 1% for bank accounts outside the US, 50 cents for US PayPal accounts and 3% capped at $30 for PayPal accounts outside the US.

Why does LemonSqueezy cost more than a card processor?

LemonSqueezy is a merchant of record: it is the seller on the receipt, so its fee covers sales tax and VAT handling as well as card processing. Compare it with a card processor in the Stripe fee calculator.
