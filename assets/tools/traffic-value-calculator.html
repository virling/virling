How to calculate the value of website traffic

A visitor is worth what visitors bring in on average. Start with revenue per visitor:

revenue per visitor = conversion rate × average order value

Then multiply by the number of visitors to get what the traffic is worth over the period:

traffic value = visitors × conversion rate × average order value

For a store, the conversion rate is orders divided by visitors and the order value is the average order value. For a subscription business, use the share of visitors who become paying customers and the average revenue per paying customer (ARPU) over the months you care about; first-month ARPU gives the value of a month of traffic in its first month only, while lifetime value per customer gives the longer view. Keep visitors, conversions and revenue from the same date range, and use one definition of revenue, with or without tax and refunds, everywhere.

If you already know revenue and visitors, you can skip the middle step: revenue divided by visitors is revenue per visitor, which is what the revenue per visitor calculator does. The longer form is useful because it shows which lever moves the number: more visitors, a better conversion rate, or larger orders.

The overall value of the traffic is the sum of each channel's value, not the visitors times an average of the channel rates. A channel with a few hundred visitors and a high conversion rate should not count as much as one with thousands, so the calculator adds up channel values and divides by total visitors for the combined revenue per visitor. A worked example

These are round hypothetical numbers, the same ones the calculator starts with, not data from a real site. A store gets 20,000 visitors a month, converts 2% of them and has an average order of $60. Revenue per visitor is 2% of $60, or $1.20, and the traffic is worth $24,000 a month.

Split by channel: organic search sends 8,000 visitors at 2.5% and $60 ($1.50 per visitor, $12,000 a month), paid social 6,000 at 1% and $40 ($0.40, $2,400), email 2,000 at 4% and $70 ($2.80, $5,600) and referral 4,000 at 2% and $50 ($1.00, $4,000). The channels add up to the same $24,000, but an email visitor is worth seven times a paid social visitor.

Why value per channel needs real payment data

The per-channel numbers above are guesses unless each payment is matched to the visit that brought the buyer. Most analytics tools count visitors per channel well but cannot see payments made in Stripe, Shopify or another checkout, and store reports often file many orders under direct. Without that link you end up applying the site-wide conversion rate to every channel, which makes every channel look the same. See how to find which marketing channel pays.

Website traffic value calculator FAQ

How much is my website traffic worth?

Multiply monthly visitors by your conversion rate and by your average order value or ARPU. 20,000 visitors converting at 2% with a $60 average order are worth $24,000 a month, or $1.20 per visitor.

What is a good revenue per visitor?

There is no single good number: it depends on price, product and how ready your visitors are to buy. The useful comparison is between your own channels and against your own past months, using the same definition of revenue.

Should I use average order value or ARPU?

Use average order value for one-off purchases and average revenue per paying customer for subscriptions. For a subscription, decide whether you want a month of revenue or the lifetime value of a customer, and say which one you used when you share the figure.

Why does each channel have a different value?

Channels bring different people: someone who opens your email already knows you and buys more often than someone who clicked a social ad. Both the conversion rate and the order size differ, and the value per visitor multiplies the two.

Where do I get conversion rate and order value per channel?

From a tool that links each payment to the visit that brought the buyer. Analytics tools count visitors per channel, but the payments happen in your checkout, so value per channel needs the two joined.
