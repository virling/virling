Polar fee FAQ

How much does Polar charge per sale?

Polar's fees page lists 5% + 50 cents on the free Starter plan, 3.8% + 40 cents on Pro ($20 a month), 3.6% + 35 cents on Growth ($100 a month) and 3.4% + 30 cents on Scale ($400 a month). International (non-US) cards add 1.5%. On a $29 sale to a US card with no tax, Starter takes $1.95 and you keep $27.05.

What is Polar's Early Member rate?

4% + 40 cents, plus 0.5% on subscription payments, with no monthly fee. Polar's fees page says organizations created before May 27, 2026 stay on it indefinitely, until they upgrade to a paid plan. Organizations created on or after that date start on Starter.

Does Polar charge its fee on sales tax and VAT?

Its fees page has no sentence on this, but its worked example takes the percentage on the total with VAT: a $30 product with 25% VAT is charged on $37.50. This calculator does the same, and leaves the tax out of your payout. Set the tax to 0 if none is added.

When does a paid Polar plan pay off?

Polar's fees page puts break-even against Starter at about $1,379 a month in sales for Pro, $5,634 for Growth and $19,048 for Scale. The fixed part of the fee also drops on paid plans, so the exact point depends on your average sale.

What does this calculator leave out?

The monthly plan fee, refunds, where Polar's fees page says the initial transaction fees are not returned, the $15 fee per dispute, custom volume pricing, and payout fees, which the page lists as Stripe's: $2 per month of active payouts, 0.25% + 25 cents per payout, and 0.25% (EU) to 1% in other countries for cross-border payouts. The page does not publish a separate rate for other payment methods, so none is included.

Why does Polar cost more than a card processor?

Polar is a merchant of record: it is the seller to your customer, so its fee covers sales tax and VAT handling as well as card processing. Compare it with a card processor in the Stripe fee calculator.
