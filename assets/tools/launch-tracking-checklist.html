Why the signup spike is not the answer

A launch day is loud. Visitors and signups jump, then fall back within a day or two. Whether the launch paid off shows up later, when trials end and people decide to pay, and again each month the ones who stay renew. That is why the last section of this checklist is 30 days out, and why the links have to be tagged before the first post goes up.

Product launch checklist FAQ

What should a product launch checklist include for tracking?

A tagged link for every channel you post on, a visitor ID passed into checkout, a log of what went out on launch day, and a review of revenue by channel a month later. Without the first two, payments cannot be tied back to the launch.

How long after a launch should I measure revenue?

At least as long as your trial plus the median time your customers take to pay. For a 14-day trial that is usually four to six weeks. Most launch traffic arrives in a day or two, but the payments it leads to can arrive much later.

Do renewals count toward the launch?

In Virling, yes. A subscription renewal takes the channel of the customer's first payment, so if that first payment was credited to a launch channel, every renewal is too.

Should I add UTM tags to my Show HN link?

You do not have to. A click from the Hacker News site arrives with news.ycombinator.com as the referrer, which Virling credits to Hacker News, and a referrer wins over a tag. A short ref=hn only adds the clicks from reader apps that send no referrer.

Can I reuse the checklist for my next launch?

Yes. Ticks are kept in this browser's local storage and never leave the device, so press Clear ticks before the next launch. To keep a record of this one, copy it as Markdown first.
