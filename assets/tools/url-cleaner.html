Remove UTM parameters from a URL

Paste one link or a whole list, one per line. The URL cleaner removes every utm_ tag and well-known click IDs such as gclid and fbclid, and keeps every other parameter and the # part unchanged. Everything runs in your browser.

To check the tags on a link instead of removing them, use the UTM checker. To tag a clean link again, use the UTM builder.

UTM tags do their job on the landing page. Virling reads them when the visit starts and links the visit to the payment it led to, so a sale keeps its source without a click ID. 14 days free.

How the URL cleaner works

Each line is read as a link. The part between ? and # is split into parameters, and a parameter is removed when its name starts with utm_ or is in the first list below. Capitals in the name do not matter. The rest is put back in the same order and spelling, without re-encoding anything.

  • The fragment stays. Everything from # on is kept, including any tags someone put there by mistake.
  • Links inside links stay. If a parameter holds another encoded address, as in ?next=%2Fpricing%3Futm_source%3Dx, its value is kept as it is.
  • Bad lines are marked. A line that is not a full link is flagged in the results and left as pasted, in its place. Blank lines are skipped.

A worked example

Take the first example link above: https://example.com/pricing?plan=pro&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&gclid=EXAMPLE123&ref=partner#faq. The names and values are made up for the example.

  1. utm_source, utm_medium and gclid are removed. plan=pro, ref=partner and #faq stay: https://example.com/pricing?plan=pro&ref=partner#faq.
  2. With the box ticked, ref goes too: https://example.com/pricing?plan=pro#faq.

When tags disappear on their own

Links can also lose parameters without anyone cleaning them. URL cleaner FAQ

How do I remove UTM parameters from a URL?

Paste the link above and copy the clean one. By hand, delete every utm_ name and its value from the part after ?, together with the & that joins it to the next one. Keep the other parameters and everything from # on.

Will the link still work without its UTM tags?

Almost always. UTM tags and click IDs are there for measurement, and pages rarely need them to load. Other parameters, such as a product number or a search term, can change the page, so the cleaner keeps them. Open a clean link once before you share it widely.

What are gclid, fbclid and msclkid?

They are click IDs. Google Ads adds gclid, Meta adds fbclid and Microsoft Advertising adds msclkid to the link when someone clicks, so the platform can tie the visit back to that click.

Should I remove UTM tags from my own campaign links?

Not from links you place to measure a campaign: without the tags the visit falls back to its referrer, or to Direct when there is none. Clean a link when you copy it from the address bar to share it, so the next visitor is not counted under the old campaign.

Why is ref kept unless I tick the box?

ref means different things on different sites. It can be a campaign tag, an affiliate code that earns someone a commission, or a setting the page needs. Tick the box when you know it is safe to remove.

Are my links sent anywhere?

No. The cleaning runs in your browser and nothing you paste leaves the page.
