What goes in each section

  • Revenue by channel. Money paid this month by customers whose visit came from each channel, next to last month. Count paying customers too, so one large order does not hide a channel that brought few buyers.
  • Change versus last month. The template works it out for you. A channel with no revenue last month shows as new rather than an endless percentage.
  • Top campaigns. The three campaigns that brought the most revenue, named the way they appear in your UTM tags, with the channel they belong to.
  • Next steps. Three short actions for next month, each tied to a row above, such as moving budget to the channel that grew or fixing the campaign that fell.

Where the numbers come from

Revenue belongs to the payment provider, not the ad platform. Take the month's payments from the client's provider, then credit each one to the channel of the visit before it. Ad platforms report their own conversions, and the totals of several platforms usually add up to more than the client was paid. If you cannot link payments to visits, say so in the report rather than mixing the two.

Channel names in the example are six of the channels Virling uses, which also counts AI answers and Communities as their own channels. Keep the same names every month so the change column compares like with like.

Client marketing report FAQ

What should a monthly client marketing report include?

Revenue and paying customers by channel, the change from last month, the campaigns that brought the most revenue and a few next steps. One page is enough for most clients; detail can live in an appendix.

Should I report revenue or conversions?

Revenue from real payments where you can, because that is what the client sees in the bank. Conversions reported by ad platforms are estimates and often overlap across platforms.

How do I show the change versus last month?

Put last month's revenue next to this month's and show the difference as a percentage. When a channel had nothing last month, call it new instead of showing a percentage.

Is this template free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no signup. Nothing you type is sent anywhere; print it, copy it as text or download it as CSV.
