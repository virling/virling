questions

Shopify fee FAQ

How much does Shopify take per sale?

On Shopify's US pricing page, Shopify Payments charges 2.9% + 30 cents per online card payment on Basic, 2.7% + 30 cents on Grow, 2.5% + 30 cents on Advanced and 2.25% + 30 cents on Plus, plus 1% for international cards. On a $50 order on Basic from a US card that is $1.75. The monthly plan is charged separately.

What does Shopify charge with a third-party payment gateway?

A transaction fee of 2% of each order on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced and 0.2% on Plus. That fee is charged by Shopify on top of whatever your gateway charges for processing the card.

Do Shopify fees vary by country?

Yes. Card rates, plan prices and whether Shopify Payments is available at all depend on the country your store is based in, and on your plan. This calculator uses the US rates; check Shopify's pricing page for your country.

What does this calculator leave out?

Premium card rates, PayPal and other wallets, in-person sales, currency conversion, refunds, chargebacks, apps, themes and taxes. Plan prices are the monthly billing prices; yearly billing is cheaper.

Is it cheaper to use Shopify Payments?

Usually, because Shopify charges no transaction fee and the card rate already covers processing. A third-party gateway pays off only if its own rate is low enough to cover Shopify's extra fee. Enter your gateway's rate to compare.
