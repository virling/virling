integrations

Connect your payments.
See which channels pay.

Virling reads payments with a read-only key where the provider allows it, and joins each one to the visit before it.

  • StripeJoin every Stripe charge to the visit before it. See revenue and revenue per visitor by channel, referrer and page, with a read-only restricted key.
  • Dodo PaymentsJoin every Dodo Payments payment to the visit before it. Revenue per channel, renewals and refunds, with payment links that need no code.
  • PolarJoin every Polar order to the visit before it. Revenue per channel, renewals, refunds and retention, with a read-only organization token.
  • LemonSqueezyJoin every LemonSqueezy order to the visit before it through a signed webhook. Revenue per channel, renewals and refunds.
  • RevenueCatPurchases and renewals arrive by webhook. A purchase is credited when it carries a visitor ID, from a web paywall or an app user linked to a web visit; in-app purchases with no web visit count as Unattributed.
  • PaddleJoin every Paddle Billing transaction to the visit before it, with renewals and refunds, from a read-only key.
  • ShopifyJoin every Shopify order to the visit before it. A small theme snippet puts the visitor ID on the cart.
  • WooCommerceJoin every WooCommerce order to the visit before it, with WooCommerce Subscriptions renewals when installed.
  • CreemJoin every Creem payment to the visit before it. Checkout visitor IDs arrive by webhook.
  • Revenue attribution for SaaSSee which channel brings SaaS customers who convert from trial and keep paying. Renewals, retention and MRR by first source, for Stripe, Paddle, Polar and more.
  • Revenue attribution for ecommerceSee which channel brings orders and revenue for your Shopify or WooCommerce store, why orders show as Direct, and which first source brings repeat buyers.
  • Revenue attribution for digital productsSee which launch post, newsletter or creator brought each sale of your templates, courses, ebooks or small software. Works with LemonSqueezy, Polar, Creem and Dodo Payments.
  • Revenue attribution for mobile appsSee which channel brings paying subscribers to an app that sells through RevenueCat and web paywalls, and what stays Unattributed.
  • Revenue attribution for browser extensionsSee which launch post, search or creator brought the users who pay for your Chrome or Firefox extension. Works with Stripe, LemonSqueezy, Polar, Creem and Dodo Payments.
  • Revenue attribution for developer toolsSee whether GitHub, Hacker News, dev.to, your docs or AI assistants bring paying customers to your developer tool, API or open source project with a paid tier.
  • Revenue attribution for online coursesSee which YouTube video, newsletter, webinar or affiliate brought each course or cohort sale. Works with Stripe, LemonSqueezy, Polar, Creem and Shopify.
  • Revenue attribution for AI appsSee whether X, Product Hunt, Reddit, YouTube or AI assistants bring the users who pay for your AI app. Credits, top-ups and subscriptions from Stripe, Polar, Creem and Dodo Payments.
  • Revenue attribution for membership sites and paid communitiesSee which channel brought each paying member of your membership site or paid community, with every renewal credited to the first channel. Works with Stripe, Polar, LemonSqueezy and Paddle.
  • Revenue attribution for plugin and theme sellersSee whether your docs, a marketplace listing, the blog, a newsletter or social brought each WordPress plugin, theme or license sale and renewal. Works with Paddle, LemonSqueezy, Polar, Creem and Stripe.
  • Revenue attribution for coaches and consultantsSee which podcast guest spot, LinkedIn post, newsletter or referral brought each paying coaching or consulting client. Works with Stripe Payment Links and Checkout.
  • Revenue attribution for SaaS on StripeSee which channel brought each Stripe Billing subscription, payment after a trial and renewal. Works with a read-only restricted key.
  • Revenue attribution for SaaS on PaddleSee which channel brought each Paddle transaction, payment after a trial and renewal, for SaaS selling through Paddle as merchant of record.
  • Shopify client reporting for agenciesSee which channel and campaign brought each order, per client Shopify store, and share the report with each client.
  • Revenue attribution for SaaS on Dodo PaymentsSee which channel brought each Dodo Payments subscription payment, trial conversion and renewal, for SaaS selling through Dodo Payments as merchant of record.
  • Revenue attribution for SaaS on LemonSqueezySee which channel brought each LemonSqueezy order, renewal and license sale, for SaaS selling through LemonSqueezy as merchant of record.
  • Revenue attribution for SaaS on PolarSee which channel brought each Polar order, payment after a trial and renewal, for SaaS and developer tools selling through Polar.
  • Revenue attribution for SaaS on CreemSee which channel brought each Creem payment, first payment after a trial and renewal, for SaaS selling through Creem as merchant of record.
  • Stripe client reporting for agenciesSee which channel and campaign brought each client payment on Stripe, with one site per client.
  • Stripe ecommerce analyticsSee which channel and campaign brought each order for your store on Stripe Checkout or Payment Links. Works with a read-only restricted key.
  • Polar analytics for developer toolsFor open source maintainers and developer tool makers selling through Polar: tie each license, sponsorship and subscription payment to the channel and campaign that brought the buyer.
  • LemonSqueezy sales for digital productsFor creators selling ebooks, templates and courses on LemonSqueezy: tie each payment to the channel and campaign that brought the buyer.
  • Paddle attribution for desktop appsFor desktop and Mac apps selling licenses or subscriptions through Paddle: tie each payment to the channel and campaign behind the visit to your download page.
  • Creem for AI appsFor founders of AI apps and wrappers selling through Creem: see which post, search or AI answer brought each paying user, and which channel keeps buying credits.
  • Dodo Payments attribution for digital productsFor creators selling ebooks, templates, courses and other digital downloads through Dodo Payments: see which channel, campaign and post brought each buyer.
  • WooCommerce Subscriptions attributionFor WooCommerce stores with subscription boxes, replenishment or memberships: tie each first order and every renewal to the channel that brought the subscriber.
