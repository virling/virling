integrations / creem for ai apps

See which post, search or AI answer brings your paying users on Creem.

For founders of AI apps and AI wrappers who take payments through Creem. Virling joins each customer's first payment to the visit that came before it, and keeps that channel on every later payment, so you see whether X, Reddit, Google or ChatGPT brings the users who pay, not only the ones who sign up and spend free credits. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

An AI app often gives new users free credits, then charges for a plan or a credit pack. A launch post can bring hundreds of signups who spend the free credits and leave, while an answer in ChatGPT may send a few users who keep paying. Creem knows who paid, but not the visit that brought them. Virling joins the two with a scoped Creem API key and one webhook.

What you get

  • Each paying user credited to a channel. The customer's first payment is joined to the visits before it. By default it goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct, so a user who found you on Reddit and came back later by typing your address is credited to Reddit.
  • AI answers on their own rows. Visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and DeepSeek get the AI channel, search engines get Organic search, and X, Reddit, Product Hunt and YouTube keep their own referrer rows.
  • Top-ups and renewals that keep the first channel. On Creem, every payment after a customer's first is a renewal, so a later credit pack or plan payment copies the channel, referrer, campaign and landing page of the first payment, matched by Creem customer ID.
  • Free credits and trials kept out of revenue. Virling counts payments, not signups or usage. A completed checkout with nothing to pay starts a trial, is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer, and the customer's first paid transaction is credited to the visits before it.
  • Refunds and chargebacks taken off. Transactions from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so later refunds and chargebacks update them.
  • Retention by channel. The Retention page groups customers by the channel of their first payment. With subscriptions:read, subscription status is read once an hour; without it, retention falls back to payment recency.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site and enter your domain, such as yourapp.com, and its timezone.
  2. Install the script. Paste the tag before </head> on your landing page and your app. The virling_vid cookie is set on your registrable domain, so yourapp.com and app.yourapp.com share one visitor ID. Leave cookieless mode off.
  3. Connect Creem. In Creem, open Developers, then API keys, and create a key with transactions:read, plus subscriptions:read for retention and webhooks:write so Virling can add its webhook. Paste it under payments; live and test mode keys both work. The last 90 days are imported, then new transactions every minute.
  4. Link checkout to visits. Creem returns checkout metadata only in webhooks, so Virling adds one checkout.completed webhook when you connect. It reads virling_vid from the checkout's metadata, then the subscription's, then the customer's. Read the virling_vid cookie on your server and set it in metadata when you create the checkout.
const res = await fetch("https://api.creem.io/v1/checkouts", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: { "x-api-key": process.env.CREEM_API_KEY, "content-type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    product_id: "prod_...",
    success_url: "https://yourapp.com/thanks",
    metadata: { virling_vid: req.cookies.virling_vid },
  }),
});
// Send the customer to (await res.json()).checkout_url

Visits from AI assistants

Users who find your app in an assistant can buy a pack on the first visit or weeks later. Where the visit lands depends on what the click carries:

  • Lands in AI. The referrer is chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com or chat.deepseek.com. With no referrer, a utm_source such as chatgpt, perplexity or claude counts too.
  • Lands in another channel. An assistant whose site is not on that list is Referral. A Google or Bing results page is Organic search, AI answers on it included. A paid or email utm_medium wins over the referrer, so a link tagged cpc is Paid.
  • Arrives as Direct. A visit with no referrer and no tag, as when an assistant's app drops the referrer or someone copies the address from an answer. It cannot be told apart from a typed address, so read the AI channel as a minimum.

To read revenue per visitor for them:

  1. Split AI by assistant. On the Channels card, click AI, then open the Source tab. Each row has visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor, with a chip against your site average.
  2. Check New customers. Revenue counts on the payment date, so later credit packs and renewals from users an assistant brought earlier are in it too. New customers leaves renewals out.
  3. Do not read the Direct row as assistants. It mixes them with typed addresses and bookmarks. By default a buyer who came from ChatGPT and returned by typing your address is credited to AI; one whose only visits were Direct is credited to Direct.
  4. Wait for enough visits. With few visitors, one purchase moves the number a lot, so use Last 90 days. Is AI search traffic worth anything? has a worked example.

What to know

  • Transactions older than the 90-day backfill are not imported, so renewals and top-ups from customers whose first payment was before that are Unattributed. Channel numbers fill in as new customers arrive.
  • The webhook needs Virling to be reachable over https. A self-hosted server on plain http still imports payments, but they stay Unattributed.
  • Payments without a visitor ID still count toward revenue as Unattributed.

Frequently asked questions

Which channel brings my paying users on Creem?

Connect Creem to Virling with a scoped API key and set virling_vid in the metadata of the checkout you create. Virling adds a checkout.completed webhook to read that metadata and credits each customer's first payment to the visits before it, so the Channels card shows whether a post, a search or an AI answer brought the users who pay.

Which channel gets credit for a credit top-up on Creem?

On Creem, every payment after a customer's first is a renewal, including a later credit pack. It takes the channel, referrer, campaign and landing page of the customer's first payment, matched by Creem customer ID.

Do free credits count as revenue?

No. Virling counts only payments from your payment provider. A user who only spends free credits is a visitor, not a customer. On Creem, a completed checkout with nothing to pay starts a trial, which is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer.

Are visits from ChatGPT and Perplexity tracked?

Yes. Visits referred by chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com get the AI channel, so revenue from AI answers shows on its own rows.

Does a refunded credit pack still decide the channel on Creem?

Yes. The first payment is the customer's earliest one that did not fail, and a refunded payment did not fail, so later payments keep its channel. The refund lowers the revenue of its transaction, on that transaction's date, and a chargeback counts as a full refund.

Why does a visit from an assistant show as Direct or Referral?

Direct means the visit had no referrer and no utm_source, so nothing names the assistant. Referral means it came from a site that is not a known AI, search, community or social host. Both can be real visits from an assistant, so the AI channel is a minimum.
