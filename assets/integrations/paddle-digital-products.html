See which channel sells your templates, ebooks and fonts on Paddle.

For people who sell templates, ebooks, fonts, presets and other downloads through Paddle. Virling ties each one-time sale and each license to the channel and campaign behind the visit to your sales page, so you see which launch post, newsletter or search brought the money, not just the clicks. Paddle records who bought and how much, but not the visit that brought them. A launch post, a newsletter mention or a search result sends visitors to your sales page, and a few of them buy. When your checkout passes the Virling visitor ID to Paddle as custom data, the sale is credited to that visitor's visits; Paddle in the docs covers the setup.

What you get

  • Each sale credited to a channel and campaign. Every visit gets one channel from its first pageview: Paid, Email, AI, Organic search, Communities, Social, Referral or Direct. By default a sale goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct.
  • Revenue per source, campaign and landing page. The dashboard lists visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor for each channel, source, campaign and landing page, so a post that sent many visitors and no sales stands apart from a small one that paid.
  • Licenses and repeat buyers. A license sold as a one-time purchase is a sale like any other. A second purchase by the same customer, matched by Paddle customer ID, keeps the channel of their first purchase.

One-off products and free copies

A one-off product has no subscription, so each completed Paddle transaction for it is one sale on the day it was billed. A transaction with nothing to pay and no subscription is not imported at all: a free download, or a copy given away with a 100% discount code, adds no sale, customer or revenue. Free copies therefore never inflate your sales count.

Bundles

  • One checkout, one sale. A bundle bought in one checkout is one Paddle transaction, so it counts as one sale for the transaction's total, credited to one channel. Virling does not split it by product.
  • Refunding part of a bundle. An approved refund or chargeback lowers the revenue of its transaction, on that transaction's date, so refunding one file from a bundle takes only that amount off. It can take up to 6 hours to show, and one on a transaction older than 60 days is not picked up automatically.

Discount codes

A sale's revenue is the transaction total after the discount, as Paddle reports it. Virling does not read which code was used, so it cannot list sales by code. To compare codes, share each one with its own tagged link, such as ?utm_source=newsletter&utm_campaign=spring-sale: sales from that link are credited to the campaign, and its row shows visitors and revenue next to your full-price traffic.

A launch post that sells most copies

Launch sales come in bursts, and many buyers read the post, leave, and come back days later by typing your URL. With the default model those sales still go to the post, since a Direct return does not take the credit, as long as they buy in the same browser within the lookback window. Frequently asked questions

How do I track Paddle sales by source?

Add the Virling script to your site, connect Paddle with a read-only API key, and pass the visitor ID as customData when you open Paddle checkout. Each sale is then credited to the visits before it, with its channel, source, campaign and landing page.

Does the script add the visitor ID to Paddle checkout by itself?

No. With Paddle.js, pass customData: { virling_vid: window.virling?.vid?.() } to Paddle.Checkout.open. For a transaction your server creates, set custom_data with the visitor's virling_vid cookie. Custom data on a customer, product or price is not read.

How is a bundle counted?

A bundle bought in one checkout is one Paddle transaction, so it counts as one sale for the transaction's total and is credited to one channel.

Are free copies and 100% discount codes counted?

No. A Paddle transaction with nothing to pay and no subscription is not imported, so a free download or a copy given away with a full discount adds no sale, customer or revenue. A partial discount counts at the total the buyer paid.

How are Paddle refunds counted?

An approved refund or chargeback lowers the revenue of its transaction, on that transaction's date. Transactions from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so a refund can take that long to show, and one on an older transaction is not picked up automatically.

Why is a Paddle sale Unattributed?

Usually because the transaction's custom_data has no virling_vid: checkout was opened without customData, a checkout link was shared on its own, or the buyer paid from an email. The sale still counts toward revenue. Paddle payments are not matched by email.
