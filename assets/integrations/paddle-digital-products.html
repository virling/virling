integrations / paddle for digital products

See which channel sells your templates, ebooks and fonts on Paddle.

For people who sell templates, ebooks, fonts, presets and other downloads through Paddle. Virling ties each one-time sale and each license to the channel and campaign behind the visit to your sales page, so you see which launch post, newsletter or search brought the money, not just the clicks. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

Start free trial See the demo

Paddle records who bought and how much, but not the visit that brought them. A launch post, a newsletter mention or a search result sends visitors to your sales page, and a few of them buy. Virling sets a visitor ID when someone lands on your site, and your checkout passes it to Paddle as custom data. Paddle is read with a read-only API key.

What you get

  • Each sale credited to a channel and campaign. Completed Paddle transactions are imported and joined to the visits before them. Every visit gets one channel from its first pageview: Paid, Email, AI, Organic search, Communities, Social, Referral or Direct. By default a sale goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct.
  • Revenue per source, campaign and landing page. The dashboard lists visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor for each channel, source, campaign and landing page, so a post that sent many visitors and no sales stands apart from a small one that paid.
  • Licenses and repeat buyers. A license sold as a one-time purchase is a sale like any other. A second purchase by the same customer, matched by Paddle customer ID, keeps the channel of their first payment, and so does a license renewal billed as a Paddle subscription.
  • Refunds and chargebacks taken off. Approved refund and chargeback adjustments lower the transaction's revenue, and transactions from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so later refunds show up.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site and enter your domain, such as yourstore.com, and its timezone.
  2. Install the script. Paste the tag before </head> on every page of your site, including the sales and product pages. Leave cookieless mode off, since checkout needs the visitor ID.
  3. Connect Paddle. In Paddle, open Developer tools, then Authentication, and create an API key with transaction.read and adjustment.read, plus subscription.read if you also sell subscriptions. Paste it under payments; live keys start with pdl_live_ and sandbox keys with pdl_sdbx_. The last 90 days are imported, then new transactions every minute.
  4. Link checkout to visits. The script does not add the visitor ID to Paddle checkouts by itself. With Paddle.js overlay or inline checkout, pass it as customData when you open checkout. For transactions you create on your server, set custom_data: { virling_vid: req.cookies.virling_vid }.
Paddle.Checkout.open({
  items: [{ priceId: "pri_...", quantity: 1 }],
  customData: { virling_vid: window.virling?.vid?.() },
});

window.virling?.vid?.() returns nothing when the script is blocked or not loaded yet, so check the value before opening checkout. Paddle UTM tracking covers where UTM tags get lost on overlay and hosted checkout and how to pass custom data. Every setting is on the Paddle integration page, and other providers are on revenue attribution for digital products. To see what Paddle keeps from each sale at your price, use the Paddle fee calculator.

Make a test purchase

  1. Connect a sandbox key. It starts with pdl_sdbx_. A site has one Paddle connection, and sandbox payments stay in your reports after you replace the key with a live one.
  2. Visit through a tagged link. Open a private window and go to your sales page through a link such as https://yourstore.com/?utm_source=test&utm_medium=email.
  3. Pay in the same window. Open a checkout with Paddle.js set to the sandbox environment and virling_vid in customData, and pay with one of Paddle's test cards. Use a new email address, since a later payment by the same customer keeps the first payment's channel.
  4. Read the payment. Wait a minute for the next sync and open /app/yourstore.com/payments. The payment shows the test source and the Email channel of your tagged visit instead of Unattributed.

Bundles, refunds and what stays unattributed

  • Bundles. A bundle bought in one checkout is one Paddle transaction, so it counts as one sale for the transaction's total, credited to one channel.
  • Refunds and chargebacks. An approved refund or chargeback lowers the revenue of its transaction, on that transaction's date, so refunding one file from a bundle takes only that amount off. Pending refunds count once Paddle approves them. It can take up to 6 hours to show, and one on a transaction older than 60 days is not picked up automatically.
  • Unattributed sales. A sale without a visitor ID still counts toward revenue, as Unattributed. Usually checkout was opened without customData, the link was shared on its own, or the buyer paid from an email. Paddle payments are not matched by email.
  • Imported history. Transactions from the first 90-day import were usually made before the script was on your site, so they are Unattributed. Channel numbers fill in as new buyers arrive.

Virling is open source under AGPLv3, and the code is on GitHub. Use the hosted service, or self-host it as one binary with every feature. In a load test on 2026-10-31, in a container limited to one vCPU and 1 GB of RAM, it ingested 100,000 pageviews at 2,829 a second with none failed and a p99 of 34 ms, and used 157 MB of memory after the ingest.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track Paddle sales by source?

Add the Virling script to your site, connect Paddle with a read-only API key, and pass the visitor ID as customData when you open Paddle checkout. Each sale is then credited to the visits before it, with its channel, source, campaign and landing page.

Does the script add the visitor ID to Paddle checkout by itself?

No. With Paddle.js, pass customData: { virling_vid: window.virling?.vid?.() } to Paddle.Checkout.open. For a transaction your server creates, set custom_data with the visitor's virling_vid cookie. Custom data on a customer, product or price is not read.

How is a bundle counted?

A bundle bought in one checkout is one Paddle transaction, so it counts as one sale for the transaction's total and is credited to one channel.

How are Paddle refunds counted?

An approved refund or chargeback lowers the revenue of its transaction, on that transaction's date. Transactions from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so a refund can take that long to show, and one on an older transaction is not picked up automatically.

Why is a Paddle sale Unattributed?

Usually because the transaction's custom_data has no virling_vid: checkout was opened without customData, a checkout link was shared on its own, or the buyer paid from an email. The sale still counts toward revenue. Paddle payments are not matched by email.
