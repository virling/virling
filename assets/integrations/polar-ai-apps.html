See which post, search or AI answer brings your paying users on Polar.

For founders of AI apps and GPT wrappers who sell subscriptions or credits through Polar. Virling ties each paying user to the channel that brought them, including referrals from ChatGPT, Perplexity and other AI answers, so you see which channel brings the users who pay, not only the ones who sign up and spend free credits. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

An AI app often gives new users free credits, then charges for a plan or a credit pack. A launch post can bring hundreds of signups who spend the free credits and leave, while an answer in ChatGPT may send a few users who keep paying. Polar knows who paid, but not the visit that brought them. Virling joins the two with an organization access token and the visitor ID carried through checkout.

What you get

  • Each paying user credited to a channel. The customer's first payment is joined to the visits before it. By default it goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct, so a user who found you on Reddit and came back later by typing your address is credited to Reddit.
  • AI answers on their own rows. Visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and DeepSeek get the AI channel, search engines get Organic search, and X, Reddit, Product Hunt and YouTube keep their own referrer rows.
  • Renewals that keep the first channel. An order with billing reason subscription_cycle is a renewal. It copies the channel, referrer, campaign and landing page of the customer's first payment, matched by Polar customer ID. Plan changes (subscription_update) keep that channel too.
  • Credit packs credited by their own checkout. A one-time credit pack is its own order, not a renewal, so it is credited to the visits before it when its checkout carries the visitor ID.
  • Free credits and trials kept out of revenue. Virling counts payments, not signups or usage. A $0 order with billing reason subscription_create starts a trial, is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer, and the customer's later payment with no visitor ID of its own is credited to the visits before the trial started.
  • Refunds taken off. New refunds update the order they belong to, and a pending refund is read again once it settles. Unpaid orders are checked again every 10 minutes for up to 30 days and imported once paid.
  • Retention by channel. The Retention page groups customers by the channel of their first payment and counts every later payment, credit packs included, in that channel's net LTV. With subscriptions:read, subscription status is read once an hour; without it, retention falls back to payment recency.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site and enter your domain, such as yourapp.com, and its timezone.
  2. Install the script. Paste the tag before </head> on your landing page and your app. The virling_vid cookie is set on your registrable domain, so yourapp.com and app.yourapp.com share one visitor ID. Leave cookieless mode off.
  3. Connect Polar. In your Polar organization's settings, create an Organization Access Token with orders:read and refunds:read, plus subscriptions:read for retention. Paste it under payments; it starts with polar_oat_, and production and sandbox tokens both work. The last 90 days are imported, then new orders every minute.
  4. Link checkout to visits. Checkout links on buy.polar.sh need no code: keep the link as a normal <a href> on a page that runs the script, and the script adds reference_id=<visitor ID>, unless it is already there. Polar stores it in the order's metadata, where Virling reads it. For a checkout session you create on your server, set virling_vid in metadata. Virling reads virling_vid from the order's metadata, then the order's custom field data, then the customer's metadata.
const checkout = await polar.checkouts.create({
  products: ["PRODUCT_ID"],
  success_url: "https://yourapp.com/thanks",
  metadata: { virling_vid: req.cookies.virling_vid },
});
// Send the customer to checkout.url

What to know

  • Virling reads Polar orders, refunds and subscriptions, not usage events, meters or credit balances. On a usage-based or credit-based plan, usage counts only once it is part of a paid order, and an order with billing reason subscription_cycle keeps the customer's first channel whatever the plan charges for.
  • Orders older than the 90-day backfill are not imported, so renewals of subscriptions that started before that are Unattributed. Channel numbers fill in as new customers arrive.
  • Payments without a customer ID cannot be linked to a first payment, and payments without a visitor ID still count toward revenue as Unattributed.

Open source

Virling is open source under AGPLv3, and the code is on GitHub. Use the hosted service, or self-host it as one binary with every feature. In a load test on 2026-10-31, in a container limited to one vCPU and 1 GB of RAM, it ingested 100,000 pageviews at 2,829 a second with none failed and a p99 of 34 ms, and used 157 MB of memory after the ingest.

Frequently asked questions

Which channel brings paying users to my AI app on Polar?

Connect Polar to Virling with an organization access token and pass the visitor ID at checkout. Checkout links on buy.polar.sh get it from the script; for a checkout session, set virling_vid in metadata. Each customer's first payment is credited to the visits before it, so the Channels card shows whether a post, a search or an AI answer brought the users who pay.

Which channel gets credit for a credit pack bought later?

A one-time credit pack is its own order, not a renewal, so it is credited to the visits before it when its checkout carries the visitor ID. Orders with billing reason subscription_cycle or subscription_update keep the channel of the customer's first payment, matched by Polar customer ID.

Does Virling read usage or credit balances from Polar?

No. Virling reads Polar orders, refunds and, when allowed, subscriptions. It does not read usage events, meters or credit balances, so usage counts only once it is part of a paid order.

Do free credits count as revenue?

No. Virling counts payments, not signups or usage. A user who only spends free credits is a visitor, not a customer. A $0 order with billing reason subscription_create starts a trial, which is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer.

Are visits from ChatGPT and Perplexity tracked?

Yes. Visits referred by chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com get the AI channel, so revenue from AI answers shows on its own rows.
