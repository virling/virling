integrations / creem

Creem revenue attribution: each payment joined to the visit behind it.

Virling ties each Creem payment to the visit that came before it, so your analytics show which channel, referrer and page bring paying customers, not just visitors. Open source, one Rust binary, or hosted.

Start free trial See the demo

Virling reads Creem transactions and, when allowed, subscriptions with a scoped API key, and adds one webhook so checkouts link to visits.

Create a key

  1. In Creem, open Developers, then API keys, and create a key. Creem's guide: API introduction.
  2. Give it the transactions:read scope, plus subscriptions:read so Retention can tell who still pays, and webhooks:write so Virling can add its webhook. No other scopes are needed.
  3. Paste it into Virling.

Live and test mode keys both work. Virling tries api.creem.io first and test-api.creem.io second, or the other way round for a key that starts with creem_test_.

The webhook

Creem returns checkout metadata only in webhooks, not on transactions. When you connect, Virling adds one webhook for checkout.completed that points at https://<your Virling>/api/webhooks/creem/<site ID>, and keeps its signing secret encrypted. Each delivery is checked against the creem-signature header, an HMAC-SHA256 of the body.

The webhook needs Virling to be reachable over https. A server on plain http still imports payments, but they stay Unattributed.

What is imported

  • Transactions from the last 90 days on the first run, then new ones every minute, newest first.
  • Refunds and chargebacks. Transactions from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so later refunds update them.
  • Renewals. Every payment after a customer's first is a renewal and keeps the channel of the first payment. See Renewals.
  • Free trials. A completed checkout with nothing to pay starts a trial, and the customer's first paid transaction is credited to it.
  • Subscription status, once an hour, when the key has subscriptions:read. Without it, retention falls back to payment recency.

How a payment finds its visitor

Virling reads virling_vid from, in order: the checkout's metadata, the subscription's metadata, then the customer's metadata.

Set it in metadata when you create the checkout on your server:

const res = await fetch("https://api.creem.io/v1/checkouts", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: { "x-api-key": process.env.CREEM_API_KEY, "content-type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    product_id: "prod_...",
    success_url: "https://yourdomain.com/thanks",
    metadata: { virling_vid: req.cookies.virling_vid },
  }),
});
// Send the customer to (await res.json()).checkout_url

Use https://test-api.creem.io with a test mode key.

What you can see

Once payments come in, these cards on the dashboard answer questions a seller asks:

  • Which channels bring paying customers, not only visits? The Channels card lists visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor for each channel, with Source and Campaign tabs for the site or tagged campaign behind it.
  • Which landing pages do buyers arrive on? The Pages card shows revenue by the first page of the visit, and by the last page before paying.
  • Who bought most recently, and from where? The Recent customers card shows the last 10 paying customers with amount, first source, visits before paying and time to convert.
  • Who reached checkout and did not pay? The Almost paid card shows visitors who reached pricing or checkout and did not pay, by channel or by their last page.
  • Do AI assistants bring Creem revenue? The AI card's Funnel tab goes from crawled to named, visited and paid, with revenue per assistant.

What stays Unattributed

These payments still count toward revenue, but no channel is credited, unless the same customer has an earlier payment or trial that was credited:

  • Every payment while Virling is not reachable over https, since no webhook brings the checkout's metadata. After moving to https, set VIRLING_BASE_URL and use Replace key to add the webhook.
  • Payments from before the webhook, which includes the 90-day import.
  • Payments from a checkout created with no virling_vid in its metadata, when the subscription's and the customer's metadata have none either.
  • Checkouts by visitors with no visitor ID, because the script was blocked or cookieless mode was on.
  • Payments with a visitor ID when no visit of that visitor falls inside the lookback window.
  • Renewals, usually, when the first payment is Unattributed or older than the first import.

Creem payments are not matched by email. To find the cause for one payment, see Unattributed revenue.

Who uses this

SaaS founders who bill subscriptions through Creem and want trials, renewals and MRR by channel (see Virling for SaaS), sellers of digital products such as licenses, templates and courses who want to know which post or referrer sold them (see Virling for digital products), and agencies that report revenue by channel to clients who sell through Creem (see Virling for agencies).

  • SaaS subscriptions on Creem: payments, the first payment after a trial and renewals, by the channel that brought the customer.
  • AI apps that sell plans or credits through Creem: which post, search or AI answer brought each paying user, and which channel keeps buying credits.
  • Templates, ebooks and courses sold on Creem: each sale tied to the post, launch or link that brought the buyer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see which channel a Creem payment came from?

Connect Creem to Virling with a scoped API key and set virling_vid in the checkout metadata. Virling adds a checkout.completed webhook and ties each payment to the visit before it.

Which scopes does the Creem key need?

transactions:read, plus subscriptions:read for Retention and webhooks:write so Virling can add its webhook. No other scopes are needed.

Why does Creem need a webhook?

Creem returns checkout metadata only in webhooks, not on transactions. Without an https address for Virling, payments still import but stay Unattributed.

Which channel do Creem renewals get?

Every payment after a customer's first keeps the channel of the first one. The first payment after a free trial is credited to the visit before the trial when it has no visitor ID of its own. If the first payment is older than the first import, renewals are usually Unattributed.

Are Creem refunds and chargebacks taken off the channel?

Yes. A refund lowers the revenue of its transaction, on that transaction's date, so the channel credited with it drops too, and a chargeback counts as a full refund. Transactions from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so a refund can take that long to show, and one on an older transaction is not picked up automatically.

Why is a Creem payment Unattributed?

Usually because the checkout had no virling_vid in its metadata, or no webhook brought it: the payment is from before you connected, or Virling was not reachable over https.
