See which post, search or AI answer brings your paying users on Stripe.

For founders of AI apps and AI wrappers that bill through Stripe with subscriptions, credit packs or top-ups. Virling ties each subscription, credit purchase and top-up to the channel that brought the user, so you see whether X, Reddit, Google or ChatGPT brings the users who pay, not only the ones who sign up and spend free credits. An AI app often gives new users free credits, then charges for a plan or a credit pack. A launch post can bring hundreds of signups who spend the free credits and leave, while an answer in ChatGPT may send a few users who keep paying. Stripe knows who paid, but not the visit that brought them. Virling joins the two with a read-only key.

What you get

  • Each paying user credited to a channel. A payment is joined to the visits before it. By default it goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct, so a user who found you on Reddit and came back later by typing your address is credited to Reddit.
  • Credit purchases and top-ups tied to their visit. A credit pack bought through Stripe Checkout, a Payment Link or your own PaymentIntent is a payment like any other. Pass the visitor ID and it is credited to the visit that led to it, under the site's attribution model.
  • Renewals that keep the first channel. A charge whose invoice has billing reason subscription_cycle is a renewal. It copies the channel, referrer, campaign, landing page, country and device of the customer's first payment, matched by Stripe customer ID, even though the renewal itself has no visit. Upgrades and plan changes (subscription_update) count as renewals too.
  • Repeat buyers counted toward the first channel. The Retention page groups each customer by the channel, referrer and campaign of their first payment, and counts every later payment, top-ups included, in that channel's net LTV. So you see both the visit behind each purchase and which channel brought the users who keep buying credits.
  • AI answers on their own rows. Visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and DeepSeek get the AI channel, search engines get Organic search, and X, Reddit, Product Hunt and YouTube keep their own referrer rows. The AI card also shows, per assistant, the visitors and revenue it sent you.
  • Free credits and trials kept out of revenue. Virling counts payments, not signups or usage. A Stripe trial start, a checkout.session.completed event with a total of 0, is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer, and the customer's later payment is credited to the visits before the trial started.
  • Refunds and disputes taken off. Stripe events for refunds, lost disputes and late settlements update payments that were already imported.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site and enter your domain, such as yourapp.com, and its timezone.
  2. Install the script. Paste the tag before </head> on your landing page and your app. The virling_vid cookie is set on your registrable domain, so yourapp.com and app.yourapp.com share one visitor ID. Leave cookieless mode off.
  3. Connect Stripe. Create a restricted key with Read on Charges, Checkout Sessions, Customers, Events, Invoices, Subscriptions and PaymentIntents, and paste it under payments. Secret keys are refused. The last 90 days are imported, then new payments every minute.
  4. Pass the visitor ID to checkout. Set client_reference_id to the virling_vid cookie, or use metadata.virling_vid on the Checkout Session or PaymentIntent. Payment Links on buy.stripe.com need no code: the script adds the ID when a visitor clicks one.
// A credit pack through Stripe Checkout
await stripe.checkout.sessions.create({
  mode: "payment",
  line_items: [{ price: "price_...", quantity: 1 }],
  client_reference_id: req.cookies.virling_vid,
  // ...
});

To store UTM tags or the visitor ID on a Checkout Session or Payment Link, see Stripe Checkout UTM parameters. Every setting is on the Stripe integration page, and Virling for AI apps covers other payment providers.

What to know

  • On Stripe, only invoice charges with billing reason subscription_cycle or subscription_update copy the first channel. A one-off top-up is credited to its own visit, so pass the visitor ID on every checkout, not only the first.
  • Payments older than the 90-day backfill are not imported, so renewals of subscriptions that started before that are Unattributed. Channel numbers fill in as new customers arrive.
  • Stripe trial starts are read from the time you connect, so trials that started before that are not seen.
  • Payments without a visitor ID still count toward revenue as Unattributed.

Frequently asked questions

Which channel brings my paying users on Stripe?

Connect Stripe to Virling with a read-only restricted key and pass the virling_vid visitor ID into Checkout as client_reference_id or metadata.virling_vid. Each customer's first payment is credited to the visits before it, so the Channels card shows whether a post, a search or an AI answer brought the users who pay.

Which channel gets credit for a credit top-up on Stripe?

A one-off credit pack bought through Checkout is a payment like any other, credited to the visit before it under the site's attribution model. The Retention page still groups each customer by the channel of their first payment and counts every later payment, top-ups included, in that channel's net LTV.

How are Stripe subscription renewals attributed?

A charge whose invoice has billing reason subscription_cycle is a renewal. It takes the channel, referrer, campaign, landing page, country and device of the customer's first payment, matched by Stripe customer ID. Upgrades and plan changes (subscription_update) keep that channel too.

Do free credits count as revenue?

No. Virling counts only payments from Stripe. A user who only spends free credits is a visitor, not a customer. A Stripe trial start, a checkout.session.completed event with a total of 0, is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer.

Are visits from ChatGPT and Perplexity tracked?

Yes. Visits referred by chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com get the AI channel, so revenue from AI answers shows on its own rows.
