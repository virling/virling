integrations / revenuecat for ai apps

See which post, search or AI answer brings your subscribers on RevenueCat.

For founders of AI mobile apps who sell subscriptions through RevenueCat, often with a web paywall. Virling ties purchases from your web paywall, and from app users linked to a web visit, to the channel that brought them, including referrals from ChatGPT, Perplexity and other AI answers. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

People may find an AI app through a post, a search or an AI answer, and land on your site before the store or a web paywall. RevenueCat knows who paid, but not the visit that brought them. Virling joins the two when the purchase carries a visitor ID. This page says which purchases that covers and which it does not.

What Virling can attribute

  • Web paywall purchases. A purchase from a web paywall on a page that runs the script is credited to the visits before it, once virling_vid is set on the RevenueCat customer.
  • In-app purchases by people who came through your site. Someone who signs up on the web and later subscribes in the app is credited too, when virling_vid was set on their customer before the purchase.
  • Renewals that keep the first channel. A renewal keeps the channel of the customer's first payment, so a channel keeps the credit for the subscribers it brought.
  • Free trials. A trial start is kept apart from payments and never counted as revenue. The first paid renewal is credited to the visit that started the trial.
  • One-time credit packs. Non-renewing purchases arrive by the same webhook, and are credited when they carry a visitor ID. One-time purchases from the last 90 days are imported once when you connect.
  • Refunds. A cancellation by customer support, or with a negative price, marks the payment refunded.

On the dashboard, the Channels card shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor for each channel. Visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and DeepSeek get the AI channel. Payments lists every purchase with the channel it was credited to, and Retention groups customers by the channel of their first payment.

What Virling cannot attribute

  • Purchases that never touch the web. Someone who finds your app in the store and buys in the app has no visit to credit. The purchase counts toward revenue as Unattributed.
  • Store installs. Virling reads payments from RevenueCat, not installs from the App Store or Google Play.
  • Purchases made before the ID was set. A purchase without a visitor ID still counts toward revenue, as Unattributed.
  • Subscription payments from before you connected. RevenueCat lists them per subscription only for some stores, so they are not imported.
  • Subscription status. It is not read, so Retention uses payment recency for RevenueCat customers.

If most of your subscribers find you in the store, the Unattributed share will be large. The channel numbers describe the subscribers who came through the web.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site and enter your domain, such as yourapp.com, and its timezone.
  2. Install the script. Paste the tag before </head> on every page of your marketing site and your web paywall. The virling_vid cookie is set on your registrable domain, so yourapp.com and pay.yourapp.com share one visitor ID. Leave cookieless mode off.
  3. Pass the visitor ID. Virling reads the RevenueCat customer attribute virling_vid, so set it before the purchase. On the web, read the cookie on your server and set it with the call below. In the app, if it receives the visitor ID from a web visit, use the RevenueCat SDK's setAttributes.
  4. Connect RevenueCat. In your project's settings, open API keys and create a V2 secret key with read access to Project configuration and Customer information, and read and write access to Integrations. Paste it under payments. Virling adds one webhook for initial purchases, renewals, non-renewing purchases and cancellations. One key covers one project, so connect each project to its own site.
  5. Check it. Open your site from a tagged link, go on to the paywall or set the attribute for your test user, and make a sandbox purchase. It should show that source in Payments instead of Unattributed.
await fetch(`https://api.revenuecat.com/v2/projects/${projectId}/customers/${appUserId}/attributes`, {
  method: "POST",
  headers: { authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.REVENUECAT_SECRET_KEY}`, "content-type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({ attributes: [{ name: "virling_vid", value: req.cookies.virling_vid }] }),
});

That call needs a key with write access to Customer information, separate from the one you give Virling. Check that the cookie is there before you send it. RevenueCat web paywall attribution covers the paywall step, and How to track RevenueCat revenue by traffic source covers the web-to-app path. Every setting is on the RevenueCat integration page, and Virling for mobile apps covers apps in general.

What to know

  • The RevenueCat webhook needs Virling to be reachable over https. The hosted plan already is. Without it, only the one-time purchases from the first import arrive.
  • Purchases and renewals arrive as they happen, in the currency the customer paid.
  • If you sell on the web through Stripe, Paddle or another provider instead, pass the visitor ID into that checkout and connect that provider too.

Open source

Virling is open source under AGPLv3, and the code is on GitHub. Use the hosted service, or self-host it as one binary with every feature. In a load test on 2026-10-31, in a container limited to one vCPU and 1 GB of RAM, it ingested 100,000 pageviews at 2,829 a second with none failed and a p99 of 34 ms, and used 157 MB of memory after the ingest.

Frequently asked questions

Can Virling attribute App Store and Google Play purchases to a channel?

Yes, when the app user's virling_vid attribute was set from a web visit before the purchase. In-app purchases from people who never came through your site have no visit to credit, so they count toward revenue as Unattributed.

Are web paywall purchases credited automatically?

The paywall page must run the script, and virling_vid must be set on the RevenueCat customer before the purchase. Your server can read the virling_vid cookie from the request and set the attribute. A purchase without a visitor ID still counts toward revenue as Unattributed.

Do free trials count as revenue?

No. A trial start is kept apart from payments and never counted as revenue. The first paid renewal is credited to the visit that started the trial.

Does Virling track app installs?

No. Virling reads payments from RevenueCat, not installs from the App Store or Google Play.

Are visits from ChatGPT and Perplexity tracked?

Yes, when they land on your site. Visits referred by chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com get the AI channel. Someone who reads an AI answer and goes straight to the store leaves no visit to credit.
