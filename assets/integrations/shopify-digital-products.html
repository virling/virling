Shopify digital products revenue tracking

To track revenue from digital products on Shopify by channel, store a visitor ID on the cart, let Shopify copy it onto the order as a note attribute, and join each order to the visits before it. Virling does this for stores that sell downloads, presets, templates or courses, so you see which post, newsletter or search brought each sale. Refunds are taken off, and orders that match no visit show as Unattributed. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

A digital product often sells from one page: someone reads a post about your preset pack, opens the product page and pays. The order has the amount and the buyer, and Virling adds the visit, matched by the ID in the order's note attributes. UTM tags that Shopify stores on the order or the landing page are not read; what Shopify itself shows is in Shopify UTM tracking.

What you get

  • Each order credited to a channel. Every visit gets one channel from its first pageview: Paid, Email, AI, Organic search, Communities, Social, Referral or Direct. By default an order goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct.
  • Revenue per source, campaign and landing page. The dashboard lists visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor for each, so a post that sent many visitors and no sales stands apart from a small one that paid.
  • Posts and newsletters measured by sales. Share a tracked link or a link with UTM tags in each post, newsletter or bio, pointing at your product page, and the Campaign tab and Links show the revenue each one brought.
  • Refunds taken off. Every round also reads the orders updated since the last one, so refunds show up within a minute or two.
  • Repeat buyers kept with the first channel. A later order by the same customer keeps the channel of that customer's first order, so a second template counts for what brought the buyer in.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site, enter your store's domain and paste the script into your theme's theme.liquid, before </head>. Leave cookieless mode off: without the virling_vid cookie there is no visitor ID for the cart to carry.
  2. Create a read-only token. In your Shopify admin, open Settings, Apps and sales channels, Develop apps, create an app, give it only the read_orders scope and install it. In Virling, paste the Admin API access token, which starts with shpat_, and your store's https://your-store.myshopify.com address. Orders from the last 60 days are imported on the first run, then new ones every minute.
  3. Store the visitor ID on the cart. Add this to theme.liquid, after the Virling script. Shopify copies cart attributes onto the order as note attributes.
<script>
// Shopify: in your theme's theme.liquid, after the Virling script.
// It stores the visitor ID on the cart, and Shopify copies cart
// attributes onto the order as note attributes.
addEventListener("load", function () {
  var vid = window.virling && virling.vid && virling.vid();
  if (vid) fetch("/cart/update.js", {
    method: "POST",
    headers: { "Content-Type": "application/json" },
    body: JSON.stringify({ attributes: { virling_vid: vid } })
  });
});
</script>

Headless and Hydrogen stores set the cart attribute from their own cart code, as the Shopify docs show. Every Shopify detail is on the Shopify integration page, and other providers are on revenue attribution for digital products.

Buy now buttons and accelerated checkouts

Buyers of a single download often skip the cart with Buy it now or an accelerated checkout such as Shop Pay. Virling does not look at how the order was paid, only at whether its note attributes include virling_vid. The snippet writes it to the cart, so a button that skips the cart may leave it out, and Virling does not handle that case separately. Check each button you use:

  1. Put Shopify Payments in test mode or turn on the Bogus Gateway.
  2. Open a private window, visit your store through a tagged link such as https://yourstore.com/?utm_source=test&utm_medium=email, and place a test order with the button. Use a new customer email for each test.
  3. Open the order in your Shopify admin and look for virling_vid in its note attributes, shown as additional details.
  4. Wait a minute or two for the next sync. The payment shows the test source and the Email channel instead of Unattributed.

If a button leaves the ID out, its orders can still match by email. When your site sent the buyer's email as an identify event, for example at a newsletter signup, an order with no visitor ID and exactly that email is credited to that visitor and shows Matched by email. It only applies to orders synced after the identify. See Unattributed revenue.

Refunds

A full or partial refund lowers the revenue of its order, on that order's date, so a channel whose buyers ask for their money back drops on the days they bought. A voided order counts as failed, and a canceled order that was paid counts until it is refunded. Chargebacks are not read.

What stays Unattributed

  • Orders from before the snippet was on your store, unless their email matches. The first import asks for the last 60 days, the most Shopify gives without the extra read_all_orders permission.
  • Orders from carts made outside your storefront: cart permalinks, Buy Button embeds and other sales channels. Link to the product page instead of a cart permalink.
  • Orders where the script was blocked or cookieless, or where a button skipped the cart and left the ID out.
  • Orders with a visitor ID but no visit of that visitor inside the lookback window.

All of them still count toward revenue. Orders without a visitor ID can match by email, as above.

What to know

  • Paid, partially refunded and refunded orders count, and voided ones count as failed. Pending and authorized orders wait until they are paid.
  • Free downloads, orders with a total of 0, are not imported.
  • An order that Shopify returns without a customer counts as its own customer, so later orders are not tied to it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track Shopify digital product revenue by channel?

Connect your store to Virling with a custom app token that can only read orders, and add the snippet that stores the visitor ID on the cart. Shopify copies it onto the order as a note attribute, and Virling credits the order to a visit before it, with its channel, source, campaign and landing page.

Does Buy it now or Shop Pay keep the visitor ID?

It depends on whether the order's note attributes include virling_vid. The snippet writes it to the cart, so a button that skips the cart may leave it out, and Virling does not handle that case separately. Place a test order with each button you use and check its note attributes.

Are refunds on Shopify downloads taken off?

Yes. A full or partial refund lowers the revenue of its order, on that order's date, and shows up within a minute or two. A canceled order that was paid counts until it is refunded. Chargebacks are not read.

Why are some Shopify download orders Unattributed?

Usually because the order's note attributes have no virling_vid: the snippet was missing, the script was blocked or cookieless, or the order came from a cart permalink, a Buy Button or another sales channel. An order is also Unattributed when no visit of that visitor falls inside the lookback window. It still counts toward revenue.

Which channel gets a second purchase by the same customer?

The channel of that customer's first order. Orders are tied together by the Shopify customer, so an order that Shopify returns without a customer counts as its own customer.

Can Virling show revenue for each product in my Shopify store?

No. Virling does not read which product an order was for. Give each product its own tracked links and compare them by campaign and landing page.
