integrations / dodo payments for ai apps

See which post, search or AI answer brings your paying users on Dodo Payments.

For founders of AI apps who sell subscriptions, credits or one-off plans through Dodo Payments. Virling ties each payment to the channel that brought the buyer, so you see whether X, Reddit, Google or ChatGPT brings the users who pay, not only the ones who sign up and spend free credits. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

An AI app often gives new users free credits, then charges for a plan, a credit pack or a one-off upgrade. A launch post can bring hundreds of signups who spend the free credits and leave, while an answer in ChatGPT may send a few users who keep paying. Dodo Payments knows who paid, but not the visit that brought them. Virling joins the two with a Dodo Payments API key that it only reads with.

What you get

  • Each payment credited to a channel. A payment is joined to the visits before it through the visitor ID its checkout carries. By default it goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct, so a user who found you on Reddit and came back later by typing your address is credited to Reddit.
  • AI answers on their own rows. Visits from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and DeepSeek get the AI channel, search engines get Organic search, and X, Reddit, Product Hunt and YouTube keep their own referrer rows.
  • Subscription renewals that keep the first channel. A subscription payment is a renewal when its subscription already had a paid payment. It copies the channel, referrer, campaign, landing page, country and device of the customer's first payment, matched by Dodo customer ID. Plan changes keep that channel too.
  • Credit packs and one-off plans credited from their own checkout. A payment that is not part of a subscription is not a renewal. It is credited from the visitor ID its own checkout carries, so a payment link on your pricing page covers every pack.
  • Free credits and trials kept out of revenue. Virling counts payments, not signups or usage. A $0 subscription payment starts a trial and is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer; the first charge after it is credited to the visits before the trial started.
  • Refunds and disputes taken off. Refunds update the payment they belong to, and lost or accepted disputes count as refunded, since the money went back to the customer.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site and enter your domain, such as yourapp.com, and its timezone.
  2. Install the script. Paste the tag before </head> on your landing page and your app. The virling_vid cookie is set on your registrable domain, so yourapp.com and app.yourapp.com share one visitor ID. Leave cookieless mode off.
  3. Connect Dodo Payments. Create an API key in the Dodo Payments dashboard and paste it under payments. Live mode and test mode keys both work. The last 90 days are imported, then new payments every minute. If one Dodo account sells more than this app, list its product IDs under Only these product IDs.
  4. Link checkout to visits. Virling reads metadata.virling_vid on the payment. Static payment links on checkout.dodopayments.com/buy/... need no code: keep the link as a normal <a href> on a page that runs the script, and the script adds metadata_virling_vid=<visitor ID>, which Dodo stores as the payment's metadata.virling_vid. For a checkout session you create on your server, set virling_vid in metadata from the cookie.
const session = await client.checkoutSessions.create({
  product_cart: [{ product_id: "pdt_...", quantity: 1 }],
  return_url: "https://yourapp.com/thanks",
  metadata: { virling_vid: req.cookies.virling_vid },
});
// Send the customer to session.checkout_url

If your app builds a payment link URL in code instead of an <a href>, add the parameter yourself:

const url = new URL("https://checkout.dodopayments.com/buy/pdt_...");
const vid = window.virling?.vid?.();
if (vid) url.searchParams.set("metadata_virling_vid", vid);
location.href = url.href;

The full walkthrough is in how to track Dodo Payments revenue by traffic source, and Dodo Payments UTM tracking covers passing source data through checkout. Every setting is on the Dodo Payments integration page, and Virling for AI apps covers other payment providers.

What to know

  • Payments older than the 90-day backfill are not imported, so renewals of subscriptions that started before that are Unattributed. Channel numbers fill in as new customers arrive.
  • To stay inside Dodo's limit of 240 requests per minute, Virling makes at most a couple of extra lookups per round, so a large backfill can take a few minutes to finish.
  • Nothing is added to payment links without a visitor ID, so not in cookieless mode. Payments without a visitor ID still count toward revenue as Unattributed.

Open source

Virling is open source under AGPLv3, and the code is on GitHub. Use the hosted service, or self-host it as one binary with every feature. In a load test on 2026-10-31, in a container limited to one vCPU and 1 GB of RAM, it ingested 100,000 pageviews at 2,829 a second with none failed and a p99 of 34 ms, and used 157 MB of memory after the ingest.

Frequently asked questions

Which channel brings my paying users on Dodo Payments?

Connect Dodo Payments to Virling with an API key and pass the visitor ID in the payment metadata; static payment links on checkout.dodopayments.com get it from the script automatically. Each payment is credited to the visits before it, so the Channels card shows whether a post, a search or an AI answer brought the users who pay.

Which channel gets credit for a credit pack on Dodo Payments?

A credit pack sold as a one-off payment is not a renewal, so it is credited from the visitor ID its own checkout carries. A subscription payment after the subscription's first paid one is a renewal and keeps the channel of the customer's first payment, matched by Dodo customer ID.

Do free credits count as revenue?

No. Virling counts only payments from your payment provider. A user who only spends free credits is a visitor, not a customer. On Dodo Payments, a $0 subscription payment starts a trial, which is never counted as revenue, a payment or a customer.

Are visits from ChatGPT and Perplexity tracked?

Yes. Visits referred by chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and chat.deepseek.com get the AI channel, so revenue from AI answers shows on its own rows.

Does Virling change anything in my Dodo Payments account?

No. Virling only reads payments, refunds and subscriptions, and never creates, changes or refunds anything. Live mode and test mode keys both work.
