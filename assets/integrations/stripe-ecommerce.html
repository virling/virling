See which channel brings your Stripe orders.

For online stores that take payments through Stripe Checkout or Payment Links, without Shopify or WooCommerce. Virling ties each order to the channel and campaign that brought the buyer, so you see orders and revenue by traffic source, not just a list of charges. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

Start free trial See the demo

Stripe knows who paid and how much, but not the ad, post, email or search result that brought them to your store. Virling joins the two: a visitor ID set when someone lands, carried into Stripe at checkout, and matched to that visitor's visits when the charge comes in.

What you get

  • Each order credited to a channel and campaign. Every visit gets one channel, such as Paid, Email, AI, Organic search, Social or Referral, plus its source, UTM campaign and landing page. By default an order is credited to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct.
  • Revenue per visitor by channel. The Channels card shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor for each channel, with the share refunded. The Source and Campaign tabs show the sites, posts and campaigns behind it.
  • Refunds and disputes. Refunds, lost disputes and late settlements update orders that were already imported, so a channel full of returns stands out.
  • Every order and its journey. The payments list shows each order with the channel it was credited to. Open one to see the visits before it.
  • Matched revenue. A line under the dashboard numbers gives the share of revenue matched to a visit and why the rest was not.
  • Repeat buyers by first source. The Retention page groups customers by the channel of their first payment, so you see which channels bring buyers who order again.

Set it up

  1. Add the site. Click + Add a site and enter your store's domain, such as yourstore.com, and its timezone.
  2. Install the script. Paste the tag before </head> on every page of your store, or add it through Google Tag Manager. Leave cookieless mode off: without the virling_vid cookie there is no visitor ID for the order to carry.
  3. Connect Stripe. Create a restricted key with Read on Charges, Checkout Sessions, Customers, Events, Invoices, Subscriptions and PaymentIntents, and paste it under payments. Secret keys are refused. The last 90 days of charges are imported, then new payments every minute.
  4. Pass the visitor ID at checkout. For Checkout Sessions you create on your server, set client_reference_id to the virling_vid cookie, or use metadata.virling_vid if you already use that field. For your own PaymentIntents with Elements, put it in metadata.virling_vid. Payment Links on buy.stripe.com need no code: the script adds the ID when a visitor clicks one.
await stripe.checkout.sessions.create({
  mode: "payment",
  client_reference_id: req.cookies.virling_vid,
  // ...
});

The full walkthrough is in how to track Stripe revenue by traffic source, and Stripe Payment Link UTM tracking covers stores that sell through links. To see what each channel's buyers spend per order, use the average order value calculator. Every Stripe detail is on the Stripe integration page.

Reading it in the first week

Set the range to Since tracking started, so the imported orders from before the script do not hide the new ones. Then make three checks on the dashboard. A week of orders is a small sample, so read a row with a few orders as a hint.

  1. Are orders reaching a visit? Read the Matched revenue line under the five numbers, then filter the Payments page by unattributed and open a few orders. It decides whether to fix checkout first or to trust the channel numbers. The line disappears when every payment is matched.
  2. Which campaign pays for its visitors? Open the Channels card, then its Source and Campaign tabs, and compare revenue per visitor with the chip that sets each row against your site average. Look at Refunded before you call a row good. It decides which campaign to repeat and which to pause, and a large Direct row tells you which links still need UTM tags.
  3. Which page do buyers land on? Open the Pages card. Landing page shows the first page of the visit and Before paying shows the last page before the payment. It decides which product or collection page your next campaign should link to.

What stays Unattributed

An Unattributed order counts toward revenue but is not credited to a visit. On a Stripe store these are the usual causes, with the fix for each:

  • Checkout Sessions or PaymentIntents without the ID. Set client_reference_id or metadata.virling_vid on your server, and check that it reads the cookie on the request that creates the checkout.
  • Your own order number in client_reference_id. Virling reads it as the visitor ID, so no visit is found. Keep your number there and put the cookie in the session's metadata.virling_vid, which is read first.
  • Payment Links opened elsewhere or on a custom domain. The script adds the ID only to normal <a href> links to buy.stripe.com on a page it runs on. Point emails, QR codes and bios at a product page, and link to Stripe from there.
  • Visits that were never tracked. The script was missing on that page or blocked, or the site is in cookieless mode. Add the tag to every page and turn cookieless mode off. A visitor who blocks the script stays uncredited.
  • No visit inside the window. The ID arrived, but every visit is older than the lookback window. Choose a longer one if your buyers take longer.
  • Imported history. Orders from before the script was on your store usually stay Unattributed. There is nothing to fix.

By email: when checkout cannot carry the ID, send the shopper's email as an identify event once you know it, for example after an account or newsletter signup on your store. Virling keeps only a salted hash of the email. A Stripe payment with no visitor ID whose billing or receipt email matches exactly is credited to that visitor and shows Matched by email on the Payments page. It applies only to payments synced after the event, and the shopper still needs a visit inside the lookback window. The code is in Recover payments by email.

To find the cause for one payment, open it from the Payments page and look for Visitor under Details. If it is missing, checkout carried no ID. If it is there, no visit of that visitor was recorded inside the window. Payments made before a fix usually stay Unattributed, and new ones are credited.

What to know

  • Orders placed before the visitor ID was passed at checkout are Unattributed. They still count toward revenue.
  • Payment Links opened from emails, QR codes or other sites carry no visitor ID. Tag those campaigns and send buyers to a page on your store first.
  • Charges older than the 90-day backfill are not imported.
  • A return from Stripe Checkout never starts a new session, so it does not steal credit from the visit before it.
  • Payments without a Stripe customer ID are grouped by their visitor on the Retention page, so a buyer on two devices can count twice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see Stripe orders by traffic source?

Add the Virling script to your store, connect Stripe with a read-only restricted key, and pass the visitor ID into Checkout as client_reference_id. Payment Links on buy.stripe.com need no code. Each order is then credited to the visits before it, and the Channels card shows revenue and revenue per visitor for every channel, source and campaign.

Do Stripe Payment Links need any code?

No. When a visitor clicks a buy.stripe.com link on a page that runs the script, the script adds client_reference_id with their visitor ID, unless the link already has one. Links opened from emails, QR codes or other sites carry no visitor ID and show as Unattributed.

Are refunds and disputes counted?

Yes. Virling reads Stripe events for refunds, lost disputes and late settlements, and updates orders it has already imported.

Does Virling need write access to Stripe?

No. It asks for a restricted key with Read on Charges, Checkout Sessions, Customers, Events, Invoices, Subscriptions and PaymentIntents, and refuses secret keys.

Which channel gets credit for a repeat order?

A later charge of the same Stripe customer keeps the channel of that customer's first payment, whatever visit came before it. If the first payment is Unattributed, the repeat order falls back to its own visitor ID. Payments without a Stripe customer ID cannot be linked to a first payment, so each one is credited by its own visits.

Are orders paid by Stripe invoice tracked?

Yes, when the invoice is paid with a charge. One marked as paid outside Stripe has no charge and is not imported. For an invoice without a subscription, Virling reads only the metadata of the charge and its PaymentIntent, so set metadata.virling_vid there or match the payment by email.

Can I test it with Stripe test mode?

Yes. With an rk_test_ key, Virling reads test charges and refunds, and the script adds the visitor ID to test Payment Links on buy.stripe.com too. A site has one Stripe connection, and test payments stay in your reports after you replace the key with a live one. Use a new customer for each test, because a later payment by the same customer keeps the first payment's channel.
