integrations / woocommerce for online courses

Track WooCommerce course sales by traffic source.

For course creators who sell through their own WooCommerce store. Virling ties each enrolment order to the visits before it, starting with the free lesson if that is where the student came in, so you see which video, email or partner brings people who pay. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

Few students enrol on their first visit. They watch a free lesson, leave, get a few emails and come back when the cart opens. WooCommerce keeps the order, and its own order attribution keeps the session that placed it. Virling keeps the visits too: a visitor ID set on the first page, saved on the order at checkout, and used to line up every visit that came before.

What you get

  • Each enrolment order credited to a channel. Every visit gets one channel, such as Social for YouTube, Email or Referral, plus its source, UTM campaign and landing page. By default an order goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct, so a student who types your address on launch day is still credited to the email before it.
  • Free lessons at the start of the journey. A visit to a free lesson page is a visit like any other. Open an order and the journey lists the student's visits in the lookback window, oldest first, with the credited one marked. If you want the visit that brought them to the lesson to take the credit, switch the site to first touch; every order is rebuilt, and you can switch back.
  • Videos, emails and partners measured by enrolments. Give each video description and each email its own utm_campaign, and the Campaign tab shows the revenue behind it. Add affiliates and guest teachers under Partners, and Virling makes each a short link and ranks them by revenue, paying customers and refund rate.
  • Renewals kept with the first channel. If you sell a membership or a payment plan with WooCommerce Subscriptions, each renewal order copies the channel, referrer, campaign, landing page, country and device of the student's first payment, matched by WooCommerce customer ID. See WooCommerce Subscriptions by channel.
  • Refunds taken off. Each order's refunds lower its revenue, so a launch that ends in refunds does not look like a good one. Orders from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours.

Set it up

  1. Put the script on lessons as well as the store. Click + Add a site, enter your domain and paste the script before </head> on every page: free lessons, sales pages, cart and checkout. Subdomains such as learn.yourcourse.com share the same visitor ID. Leave cookieless mode off: without the virling_vid cookie there is no visitor ID for the order to carry.
  2. Create a read-only key. In WordPress, open WooCommerce, Settings, Advanced, REST API, add a key and set Permissions to Read. In Virling, paste the consumer key and the consumer secret joined by a colon, as ck_...:cs_..., with your store's address. The store must be reachable over https, with pretty permalinks on so /wp-json/wc/v3/ answers. Orders from the last 90 days are imported on the first run, then new ones every minute.
  3. Save the visitor ID on each order. Add this to your theme's functions.php, or to a snippets plugin. It covers the classic and the block checkout.
add_action('woocommerce_checkout_create_order', 'virling_vid_on_order');
add_action('woocommerce_store_api_checkout_update_order_from_request', 'virling_vid_on_order');
function virling_vid_on_order($order) {
  $vid = $_COOKIE['virling_vid'] ?? '';
  if (preg_match('/^[A-Za-z0-9-]{8,64}$/', $vid)) $order->update_meta_data('virling_vid', $vid);
}

To check it, open a free lesson from a link with ?utm_source=test&utm_medium=test in a private window, go on to the store and place a test order. After the next sync the payments list shows its channel instead of Unattributed. Then tag the links you already share: WooCommerce UTM tracking covers the tags, and revenue attribution for online courses covers launches, cohort deadlines and affiliates. Every WooCommerce detail is on the WooCommerce integration page.

What to know

  • Virling reads WooCommerce orders only. It does not read course plugins, lesson progress or completions, and it does not read which course an order was for.
  • Free enrolments, orders with a total of 0, are not imported. Virling counts paid orders.
  • Lessons watched on another site, such as YouTube or a course host on its own domain, are not visits. The journey starts when the student lands on a page of yours that runs the script.
  • Orders placed before the snippet was added, or created in the admin, have no visitor ID. They count toward revenue, as Unattributed.
  • Orders older than the 90-day backfill are not imported, so renewals of memberships that started before that are usually Unattributed.
  • Renewal detection needs WooCommerce Subscriptions. Instalments taken as separate ordinary orders are credited one by one.
  • Guest orders have no customer ID, so each counts as its own customer and a renewal cannot be linked to it. Have students check out with an account.
  • Processing and completed orders count as paid. Pending and on-hold orders, such as bank transfers, wait until they are paid.
  • Someone who watches a lesson on a phone and enrols on a laptop is two visitors.

Open source

Virling is open source under AGPLv3, and the code is on GitHub. Use the hosted service, or self-host it as one binary with every feature. In a load test on 2026-10-31, limited to one vCPU and 1 GB of RAM, it took in 100,000 pageviews at 2,829 a second with none failed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see WooCommerce course sales by traffic source?

Connect your store to Virling with a read-only REST API key, add the script to your site, and add the snippet that saves the virling_vid cookie on each order. Each enrolment order is then credited to a visit before it, and the Channels card shows visitors, revenue and revenue per visitor for every channel, source and campaign.

Does Virling work with my WooCommerce course plugin?

Virling reads WooCommerce orders, and nothing from a course plugin. If your plugin sells enrolments as WooCommerce orders placed through the WooCommerce checkout, those orders are read like any other. Lesson progress, completions and enrolments made without a paid order are not read.

Can I see that a student watched a free lesson before buying?

Yes, when the lesson page is on your domain or a subdomain and runs the script. The visit is in the journey of the order when it is the same browser and falls inside the lookback window. A free lesson taken as a $0 order is not imported, so the visit is what counts, not the order.

Do renewals of a course membership keep the first channel?

Yes, when they are WooCommerce Subscriptions renewal orders and the customer's first payment is in Virling. A renewal copies the channel and campaign of that first payment, matched by WooCommerce customer ID.

Are course refunds taken off?

Yes. Each order's refunds lower its revenue. Orders from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so later refunds show up. A refund on an older order is not picked up.

Can Virling show revenue for each course?

Not by product. Virling reads the order total, not its line items. Give each course its own campaign on the links you share, and the Campaign tab shows the revenue each campaign brought.
