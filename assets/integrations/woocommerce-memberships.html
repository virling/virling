WooCommerce membership revenue tracking by channel.

To track WooCommerce membership revenue by channel, save a visitor ID on each order and join it to the visits before. Virling does that with a read-only key and a short snippet: a member's first payment is credited to the channel that brought them, each WooCommerce Subscriptions renewal keeps that channel, and refunds come off. Open source under AGPL, hosted or self-hosted.

A membership is paid again and again, but only the first payment follows a visit. WooCommerce keeps every order, and a renewal order is created with no visit behind it. Counted alone, renewals look like revenue from nowhere, and a channel is judged by joins instead of by members who stay.

What you get

  • The first payment, credited to a channel. Every visit gets one channel, such as Email, Communities or Organic search, plus its source, UTM campaign and landing page. By default an order goes to the latest visit in the last 90 days that was not Direct.
  • Renewals that stay with it. With WooCommerce Subscriptions, a renewal order is marked as a renewal and copies the channel, referrer, campaign, landing page, country and device of the member's first payment, matched by WooCommerce customer ID. See WooCommerce Subscriptions by channel.
  • Members still paying, per channel. Subscription status is read once an hour. A member counts as still paying while their subscription is active, or pending-cancel until its end date. The Retention page shows still paying, net LTV, refund rate and 30-day churn by the channel of the first payment.
  • Refunds taken off. Each order's refunds lower its revenue. Orders from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so later refunds show up.
  • Communities and partners compared. Give each community its own utm_campaign, and the Campaign tab has one row for each. Community revenue tracking covers the tags.

First payment versus renewal: an example

These numbers are made up. A membership costs $20 a month. Social brings 50 members: 50 pay in the first month, 10 in the second and 5 in the third. Email brings 30: 30 pay, then 28, then 27. By first payments, Social wins, $1,000 to $600. With renewals on the first channel, Email wins, $1,700 to $1,300. Put your own numbers for each channel into the churn rate calculator.

Set it up

  1. Put the script on every page. Click + Add a site, enter your domain and paste the script before </head> on your articles, join page, cart and checkout. Leave cookieless mode off: without the virling_vid cookie there is no visitor ID for the order to carry.
  2. Create a read-only key. In WordPress, open WooCommerce, Settings, Advanced, REST API, add a key and set Permissions to Read. In Virling, paste the consumer key and the consumer secret joined by a colon, as ck_...:cs_..., with your store's address. The store must be reachable over https, with pretty permalinks on so /wp-json/wc/v3/ answers. Orders from the last 90 days are imported on the first run, then new ones every minute.
  3. Save the visitor ID on each order. Add this to your theme's functions.php, or to a snippets plugin. It covers the classic and the block checkout.
add_action('woocommerce_checkout_create_order', 'virling_vid_on_order');
add_action('woocommerce_store_api_checkout_update_order_from_request', 'virling_vid_on_order');
function virling_vid_on_order($order) {
  $vid = $_COOKIE['virling_vid'] ?? '';
  if (preg_match('/^[A-Za-z0-9-]{8,64}$/', $vid)) $order->update_meta_data('virling_vid', $vid);
}

To check it, open your join page from a link with ?utm_source=test&utm_medium=test in a private window and place a paid test order. After the next sync the payments list shows its channel instead of Unattributed. Renewal orders need nothing extra. What is not covered

  • Virling reads orders, refunds and, with WooCommerce Subscriptions, subscriptions. It does not read membership plans, access rules, content views or which product an order was for, and it does not manage access.
  • Renewal detection and subscription status need WooCommerce Subscriptions. Without it, every order is credited one by one and Retention uses payment recency.
  • The first payment is the customer's earliest order that did not fail, so a member who bought something else from your store first keeps the channel of that order.
  • The first payment must be in Virling. Orders older than the 90-day backfill are not imported, so renewals of memberships that started before that are usually Unattributed. They still count toward revenue.
  • Orders with a total of 0, such as a free membership or the start of a free trial with no sign-up fee, are not imported.
  • Guest orders have no customer ID, so each counts as its own customer and a renewal cannot be linked to it. Have members check out with an account.
  • Orders placed before the snippet was added, or created in the admin, have no visitor ID. They count toward revenue, as Unattributed.
  • A refund on an order older than 60 days is not picked up.
  • Processing and completed orders count as paid, failed and cancelled ones as failed. Pending and on-hold orders wait until they are paid.
  • Someone who reads on a phone and joins on a laptop is two visitors.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track WooCommerce membership revenue by channel?

Connect your store to Virling with a read-only REST API key, add the script to your site, and add the snippet that saves the virling_vid cookie on each order. A member's first payment is credited to a visit before it. With WooCommerce Subscriptions, each renewal order keeps that channel, so the Channels card shows first payments and renewals together.

Can Virling track membership renewals without WooCommerce Subscriptions?

No. Virling recognises a renewal by the mark WooCommerce Subscriptions puts on the renewal order. Without it, orders are still imported and credited one by one from their own visitor ID, and Retention uses payment recency instead of subscription status.

Does Virling read my membership plugin?

No. Virling reads WooCommerce orders, their refunds and, with WooCommerce Subscriptions, subscriptions. Membership plans, access rules, content views and members added without a paid order are not read.

What happens when a membership starts with a free trial?

An order with a total of 0 is not imported, so a trial with no sign-up fee leaves no first payment to copy from. The first charge is a renewal order, which is usually Unattributed and left out of Retention. It still counts toward revenue. A trial with a paid sign-up fee does have a first payment.

Are membership refunds taken off?

Yes. Each order's refunds lower its revenue, for first payments and renewals alike. Orders from the last 60 days are read again every 6 hours, so later refunds show up. A refund on an older order is not picked up.

What about members who joined before I connected Virling?

The first run imports orders from the last 90 days. A renewal whose first payment is older than that has nothing to copy, so it is usually Unattributed. It still counts toward revenue.
