06/questions

Questions founders ask.

How do I see which marketing channel brings paying customers?

Give every visitor an ID, pass that ID into checkout, and join each payment back to the visits before it. Virling does all three. Add one script tag, connect your payment provider with a read-only key, and the dashboard shows revenue and revenue per visitor for every channel, page and country.

Is there an open-source way to attribute Stripe revenue to traffic sources?

Yes. Virling is open source under AGPLv3 and runs on your own server. It reads Stripe with a restricted, read-only key and credits each payment to the visit that led to it. Dodo Payments and Polar work the same way, and LemonSqueezy does too through a webhook once Virling has an https address.

Which traffic source makes money, and which only brings visitors?

Compare revenue per visitor, not visitor counts. A channel with a small share of traffic can bring most of the money. Virling puts revenue next to traffic for each source, so a launch, a post or a newsletter is judged by what it earned.

How does Virling decide which channel gets the credit?

By default, the last visit in the lookback window that was not direct. You can switch to first touch, and set the window to 30, 60, 90 or 365 days. Renewals keep the channel of the customer's first payment.

Can I self-host revenue analytics?

Yes. Virling is one Rust binary with embedded storage, for Linux, macOS or Docker. There is no Postgres, ClickHouse or Redis to run, and a backup is copying one folder. Self-hosting is free.

Do I need a cookie banner?

That depends on where you and your visitors are. Virling sets one first-party cookie so checkout can carry the visitor ID. Cookieless mode sets none, but then payments can't be linked to visits.

How long does switching from another tool take?

Switching takes two minutes. Virling understands another revenue analytics tool's script API: its goal calls, its goal attributes and the visitor ID your checkout passes. Swap the script tag and keep the rest of your code. See Switch in two minutes.

Can I use it today?

Virling is in early development. Releases are on GitHub and run on your own server now, with breaking changes possible until a stable release. Hosted plans start with a 14-day free trial at app.virling.com.
