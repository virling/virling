01/payment trail

Every payment has a path. Virling draws it.

Click any payment and see the visits before it: where the person first came from, what they read, and the visit that made them buy. Credit goes to the last channel that was not direct.

  • Renewals keep the source of the first payment
  • First touch or last touch, your choice

02/channels that pay

Your busiest channel is not your best one.

Visitor counts reward whatever is loud. Put revenue next to traffic and the picture flips: the channel that fills your graph can be the one that pays the least.

  • Revenue per visitor for every channel, page and country
  • Stripe, Dodo Payments, Polar and LemonSqueezy
How it compares

PlausibleCounts visits. Open source and self-hostable.

DataFastTies visits to revenue. Hosted only.

VirlingTies visits to payments and runs on your server.

03/runs on your box

One binary. Your server. Your data stays put.

Download it, start it, add the tag. Your events and settings sit right next to the binary, and a backup is copying one folder.

Binary
One Rust binary. Linux x86_64 and arm64, macOS, Docker.
Storage
Embedded DuckDB for events, SQLite for settings. No Postgres, no ClickHouse, no Redis.
Script
Under 1 KB gzipped, one tag in your head.
License
AGPLv3
Payments
Read-only keys for Stripe, Dodo Payments, Polar and LemonSqueezy. No webhooks.
