Virling vs Plausible

Plausible is a well-loved, privacy-friendly analytics tool for traffic. Virling focuses on revenue: it reads your payments and joins each one to the visit before it.

When Plausible is the better fit

You mainly need clean, privacy-friendly traffic stats and do not sell online, or your team already relies on Plausible's reports.

When Virling is the better fit

You sell a product or subscription and want revenue and revenue per visitor by channel, from Stripe, Dodo Payments, Polar or LemonSqueezy, on your own server.

Frequently asked questions

Does Plausible show revenue by channel?

Not built in. Plausible takes revenue from goals you send with a value, and ecommerce revenue is a cloud feature, not part of the Community Edition. Virling reads your payment provider and shows revenue per visitor by channel.

What does it take to self-host Plausible and Virling?

Plausible Community Edition needs PostgreSQL and ClickHouse. Virling is one Rust binary with embedded storage, so there is no database to run.

When is Plausible the better fit?

When you mainly need clean, privacy-friendly traffic stats and do not sell online, or your team already relies on its reports.
