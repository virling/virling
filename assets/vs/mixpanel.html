Virling vs Mixpanel: a Mixpanel alternative for revenue by channel

Mixpanel is product analytics: funnels, retention and reports on the events you send. Virling reads your payments and joins each one to the visit before it, so marketing channels are judged by revenue.

When Mixpanel is the better fit

You need product analytics across your app, such as feature usage, funnels and cohorts on events you design, with a team that can send and maintain those events or a warehouse to connect.

When Virling is the better fit

You want to know which marketing channels bring paying customers without designing revenue events: Virling reads your payment provider, keeps renewals on the first channel, and shows AI crawler and AI answer data. It also runs on your own server.

Frequently asked questions

Can Mixpanel show revenue by marketing channel?

Mixpanel has attribution models, including first touch by UTM source, on events you send, and Revenue Analytics for customers who use warehouse connectors. Virling reads Stripe, Paddle, Shopify, WooCommerce, Dodo Payments, Polar, LemonSqueezy, Creem or RevenueCat directly and credits each payment to the visit before it.

Can I self-host Mixpanel?

No. Mixpanel is a hosted service; only its SDKs are open source. Virling is open source under AGPLv3 and runs as one binary on your server, or hosted.

When is Mixpanel the better fit?

When you need product analytics across your app on events you design, and revenue by marketing channel is a smaller part of the job.
