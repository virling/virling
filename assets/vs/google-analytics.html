When Google Analytics is the better fit

You run ads in Google's ecosystem, need deep marketing reports, or want a free tool and have time for tag setup.

When Virling is the better fit

You want revenue by channel from your payment provider in minutes, renewals credited to the channel that won the customer, and your data on your own server.

Frequently asked questions

How do I see revenue by channel without Google Analytics ecommerce setup?

Use a tool that reads the payments themselves. Virling reads Stripe, Paddle, Shopify, WooCommerce, Dodo Payments, Polar, LemonSqueezy, Creem or RevenueCat with a key and needs no event code.

Why do subscription renewals show as direct in Google Analytics?

Renewals after the lookback window usually lose their source and tend to show as direct or unassigned. Virling keeps renewals on the channel of the first payment.

When is Google Analytics the better fit?

When you run ads in Google's ecosystem, need deep marketing reports, or want a free tool and have time for tag setup.
