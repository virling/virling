Virling vs Triple Whale: a Triple Whale alternative for attribution

Triple Whale is an ecommerce analytics platform for brands, with ad attribution and pricing based on store revenue. Virling is open-source revenue attribution for stores and subscriptions, at a flat price or free on your own server.

When Triple Whale is the better fit

You run a Shopify, WooCommerce or BigCommerce brand with real ad spend, and want ad platform data, attribution, AI visibility and more in one hosted platform, with pricing that grows with your GMV.

When Virling is the better fit

You sell through Stripe, Paddle, Dodo Payments, Polar, LemonSqueezy, Creem or RevenueCat as well as a store, want retention by first-payment channel and AI crawler data, or want a flat price or your own server instead of pricing by GMV.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an open-source Triple Whale alternative?

Virling is open source under AGPLv3 and can run on your own server. It reads Shopify, WooCommerce and seven other payment providers and credits each payment to the visit before it.

How is Triple Whale priced?

Triple Whale has a free plan. Paid plans are priced by annual GMV and the package you choose, on 12-month terms. Virling is free to self-host, and hosted plans are $19 or $79 a month.

When is Triple Whale the better fit?

When you run an ecommerce brand with real ad spend and want ad platform data, attribution and AI visibility in one hosted platform.
