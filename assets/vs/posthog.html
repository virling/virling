Virling vs PostHog

PostHog is a broad product analytics platform. Virling does one job: it reads your payments and joins each one to the visit before it.

When PostHog is the better fit

You want product analytics, session replay, experiments and feature flags in one tool, and revenue is one of many questions you ask.

When Virling is the better fit

Your main question is which channel brings paying customers. You want payments read for you, revenue per visitor by channel, and one small server to run.

Frequently asked questions

Is Virling a simpler alternative to PostHog for revenue?

Virling does one job: it reads your payments and shows which channels bring paying customers. PostHog is a broad product analytics platform whose revenue analytics reads Stripe data through its data warehouse, which its docs call beta.

Can I self-host PostHog and Virling?

Yes. PostHog's hobby deployment runs with Docker on a Linux server and needs about 4 GB of memory. Virling is one Rust binary with embedded storage and no Postgres, ClickHouse or Redis.
