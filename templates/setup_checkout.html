Link checkout to visitors

Pass the visitor ID from the virling_vid cookie into checkout. That is how a payment is credited to the visit that brought it. Payments without it still count, as Unattributed.

In the browser, window.virling?.vid?.() returns the same ID, or nothing if the script is blocked. On your server, read the virling_vid cookie. New payments are matched within a minute of syncing.
